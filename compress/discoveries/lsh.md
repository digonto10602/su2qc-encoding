- r01-F1-001: DISCOVERY: (claim) on all 47 codes A=[u=v=0] equals not-p on bright codes, so the raw l_nv parity folded into the vp_0 slot (vp_0^nv_1^nv_2^nv_3 = w_0^p, Toffoli of site 0 on (not f_3, f_0) by the Gauss law) replaces the A rccx pair (4 CX instead of 6); the frame is valid with any correct walk.
- r01-F1-001: DISCOVERY: (claim) the A->p swap does not pay: with walk variables (w0, w0^p, a=[y_i=y_j=0], two other y) the minimum Walsh support is 12 (MILP) but an exhaustive C DFS finds no toggle walk of <= 13 CX for any of the six corner-pair flags and none of <= 14 CX for a=[y0=y2=0]; best found 17 (MILP support + TSP), i.e. 73 CX vs 71.
- r01-F1-001: DISCOVERY: (claim) the rccx relative phases of the per-site recoder cancel around the walk because the number of rising flux boundaries (f_{c-1}=0, f_c=1) is invariant under the move (it equals half the number of set w bits).
- r01-F2-001: DISCOVERY: (claim, numerical) in the ch frame the only bright-zero garbage-free 1-AND flags are d_c = nv_c AND NOT w_c; no two of them cover the even-D dark collisions left by the raw nv parity p, so an all-ch (pure swap-diagonal) design needs >= 3 garbage-free ANDs for gating on top of p.
- r01-F2-001: DISCOVERY: (claim, numerical) replacing one transcoded site by the ch frame saves 4 CX in the frame, but the walk support grows 12 -> 14 (TCTT, MILP optimum) and the shortest closed walk grows 13 -> 26 toggles; qiskit rccx recoding is then invalid (its (-i)^{rising} phase is no longer move-invariant), the h/t/cx Margolus is needed.
- r01-F3-001: DISCOVERY: (claim) Folding the four raw l_nv bits into site 0 (3 CX/side) replaces site 0's transcoder AND: three cleaning ANDs (sites 1-3) already make the bright move X on f_0 and give the class bit p for free; qiskit rccx fails here (its +-i phases give +-i partner ratios when w_0=1), a real-phase Margolus Toffoli (3 CX, 4 T) is exact.
- r01-F3-001: DISCOVERY: (claim) In this frame no dark flag is needed: a Walsh multiplexer with 17-18 terms, all coefficients +-beta/8 or +-beta/4, is exact on all 433 states (HiGHS, time-limited; LP bound 16 terms without outer bits), but its parities are heavy and cost 30 CX after routing (beam-searched CX network), so the frame's AND saving is eaten by the multiplexer.
- r01-F3-001: DISCOVERY: (claim) Adding one NOR flag of two dark bits (6 CX) shortens the routed multiplexer only to 28 CX; outer l_2v(0) = d_0 on w_0=0 is a free mux control but saves just 1 term.
- r01-F4-001: DISCOVERY: Folding the four raw l_nv into site 0 gives p = [#w=2] xor (number of dark sites mod 2), invariant on all 47 codes; with Toffolis (w_c, f_c -> l_nv) on sites 1..3 only, the frame holds the same information as the seed's frame after its class AND (p xor y1 xor y2 xor y3 = y0 xor [#w=2]), so the fold replaces that AND but y0 is no longer linear.
- r01-F4-001: DISCOVERY: qiskit rccx with controls (w_c, f_c) leaves a pair-phase ratio of +-i when an odd number of w=1 sites carry a Toffoli; a Margolus Toffoli (h/t/s, 3 CX, 4 T, -1 only on w=1, f=0, l_nv=1) adds no phase on any physical code, so a three-Toffoli frame is phase-free.
- r01-F4-001: DISCOVERY: Flagless multiplexer on the moving bit in this frame: minimum Walsh support 16..18 terms (HiGHS, 400 s; 18 found, all coefficients +-1/8), but the shortest Gray tour over the 10 single-qubit forms is 27-28 CX, so frame (46) + mux = 74 CX; one-AND flags do not lower the LP support below 16.
- r01-F5-001: DISCOVERY: with no bright flag the Walsh multiplexer over (u,v,e,y0..y3) needs all 16 y-characters (the f=0000/1111 link class sees every y pattern), an exact-16 solution is ILP-infeasible and a Gray-blocked walk costs >=31 toggles, so F5 without flags loses to the 71-CX seed.
- r01-F5-001: DISCOVERY: a toggle CX(q,pivot) right after h(pivot) equals CZ(q,pivot) before it, so a first/last walk toggle on a qubit that the ladder CX(pivot,q) targets (v=q6, e=q9) is free (CX-then-CZ = sdg,s,cx,sdg); the seed uses this only at the end.
- r01-F5-001: DISCOVERY: shifting the whole Walsh support by a dark-flag character (y_c) leaves the target unchanged (theta vanishes when y_c=1), so the walk may be re-toured on any shifted support; here it saved 1 CX (74 -> 73), on the seed support it does not help.
