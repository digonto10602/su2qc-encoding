Jargon, once: *rishon* = fermionic constituent of a gauge link in the quantum link model; *quantum link model (QLM)* = lattice gauge theory whose link variables are operators on a finite-dimensional link Hilbert space; *Jordan–Wigner (JW)* = the map from fermion modes to qubits; *link type* = our label $A$, $B$, $Q$ for the three ways two rishons sit on a link (both at the start, both at the end, one at each end); *bright pair* = two orthonormal states coupled by $H_B$; *dark state* = a state annihilated by $H_B$; *inner view* = the verification object that treats the 1296 valid inner codes as the states to be checked.

## R1. The model

**Theory.** SU(2) quantum link model (Brower–Chandrasekharan–Wiese, arXiv:hep-th/9704106, Secs. 2 and 4; Wiese, arXiv:1305.1602, Sec. 4.3) in the two-rishon link representation: per link two colours × two ends = 4 fermion modes $c_{L,i},c_{R,i}$ ($i=1,2$), canonical anticommutation relations (BCW Eq. 4.2), rishon number $N_\ell=\sum_i(c^\dagger_{L,i}c_{L,i}+c^\dagger_{R,i}c_{R,i})$ fixed to **2** (BCW Eq. 4.4, 4.7: $\binom42=6$ states per link — this is a *different theory* from Kogut–Susskind at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$, which has 5 states per link). Link operators (BCW Eq. 4.3, project generator normalisation $T_a=\sigma_a/2$ as in METHODS §3):
$$U_{ij}=c_{L,i}\,c^\dagger_{R,j},\qquad L_a=\sum_{ij}(T_a)_{ij}\,c^\dagger_{L,i}c_{L,j},\qquad R_a=\sum_{ij}(T_a)_{ij}\,c^\dagger_{R,i}c_{R,j},$$
so that $[L_a,U_{ij}]=-(T_aU)_{ij}$, $[R_a,U_{ij}]=+(UT_a)_{ij}$ (BCW Eq. 2.11 / 4.1 with $\lambda^a=2T_a$), $[U_{ij},U_{kl}]=0$, $[N_\ell,U_{ij}]=0$ (all measured exact). The L end of a link is its start ($c_{x,+\mu}$), the R end its end ($c_{x+\hat\mu,-\mu}$). Determinant (BCW Eq. 4.6; Banerjee et al. arXiv:1211.2242 for $N=2$): $\det U=2\,c_{L1}c^\dagger_{R1}c_{L2}c^\dagger_{R2}$.

**Hamiltonian (project normalisation, Paulo Eq. 16 as in METHODS §2; decision research 200 §2):**
$$H=\frac{g^2}2H_E+\frac1{g^2}H_B,\qquad H_E=\sum_{\ell=1}^{12}\tfrac12\sum_a\big(L_a^2+R_a^2\big)_\ell,\qquad H_B=-\big(\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P+\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P^\dagger\big),\quad U_P=U_bU_rU_t^\dagger U_l^\dagger,$$
with the same loop, orientation list and link names as METHODS §1. The determinant coupling $\gamma\sum_\ell(\det U_\ell+\det U_\ell^\dagger)$ (Wiese Eq. 4.11 ff.) is **$\gamma=0$** in M5. Consequences: $H_E$ equals $j_\ell(j_\ell+1)$ on every link ($0$ on the two singlets, $\tfrac34$ on the quartet; measured Casimirs $(0,\tfrac34,\tfrac34,\tfrac34,\tfrac34,0)$), so $U_E(\alpha)=e^{-i\alpha H_E^{\rm inner}}$ and $U_B(\beta)=e^{-i\beta H_B}$, the step orders, the angle map and the outer-electric row are exactly METHODS §2. With $\gamma=0$ the Hamiltonian is also invariant under the local U(1) generated by $Q_x=\sum_{\text{ends at }x}n_{\rm end}$ (Wiese Eq. 4.11); its charges are conserved superselection labels of the physical space (431 sectors on this diagram, measured); the U(2)-QLM physical space is the sector $Q_x=4$ at every corner (2192 states). Normalisation fact: $\|\mathrm{Tr}U_P\,|{\rm vac}_A\rangle\|=4$ (KS: 1); $\beta$ is **not** rescaled.

**Gauss's law.** $G^a_c=\sum_{\text{ends }e\text{ at }c}\sum_{ij}(T_a)_{ij}c^\dagger_{e,i}c_{e,j}$ ($L_a$ for a link starting at $c$, $R_a$ for one ending there; BCW Eq. 2.9), physical iff $G^a_c|\psi\rangle=0$ for all $a$, $c$. Only the SU(2) law is imposed.

## R2. Register, Jordan–Wigner map, codes

* 12 links × 4 qubits = **48 qubits**. Link $\ell$ (METHODS §1 numbering: 0–3 = $b,r,t,l$, 4–11 = $o_0..o_7$) occupies qubits $(4\ell,4\ell+1,4\ell+2,4\ell+3)=(L_1,L_2,R_1,R_2)$; colour 1 = up ($m=+\tfrac12$), 2 = down. Register `d` = qubits 0–15 (inner links), register `o` = qubits 16–47 (outer links; the circuit may read, never write). Occupation bit 1 = mode occupied; little-endian as in METHODS §1.
* JW with link-contiguous modes: $c_k=\big(\prod_{m<k}Z_m\big)\sigma^-_k$, $\sigma^-=|0\rangle\langle1|$, global mode index $k=$ qubit index. **String-freeness (measured, Lean L2):** every bilinear of one link (hence $U_{ij}$, $L_a$, $R_a$, $N_\ell$, $\det U$) is a 4-qubit operator on that link's qubits, and every end bilinear $c^\dagger_{e,i}c_{e,j}$ is a 2-qubit operator on the end's qubits, because the strings of the two factors cancel outside the link. All link operators are even, so operators of different links commute and $W=\mathrm{Tr}(U_bU_rU_t^\dagger U_l^\dagger)=\sum_{abcd}u^{(b)}_{ab}\otimes u^{(r)}_{bc}\otimes u^{(t)\dagger}_{dc}\otimes u^{(l)\dagger}_{ad}$ with $u_{ij}$ the 16×16 link matrices (4096 non-zeros on the 16-qubit inner register).
* **Link codes** (bits $L_1L_2R_1R_2$, integer = $L_1+2L_2+4R_1+8R_2$): $A$ = `1100` (3): both rishons at the start; $B$ = `0011` (12): both at the end; quartet $Q$: `1010` (5) $=(L\!\uparrow,R\!\uparrow)$, `0110` (6) $=(L\!\downarrow,R\!\uparrow)$, `1001` (9) $=(L\!\uparrow,R\!\downarrow)$, `0101` (10) $=(L\!\downarrow,R\!\downarrow)$. The other 10 of 16 link patterns are invalid (don't-care). **Inner codes:** $6^4=1296$ valid 16-bit data codes. $U_{ij}$ maps $A\to Q\to B\to0$ (one rishon hops from L to R), $U^\dagger$ the reverse; $\det U=2|B\rangle\langle A|$ on the sector.
* **Outer codes and the fixed far end.** For outer link $k$ the near end is `near_end` of METHODS §1 (`L` if the link starts at its corner, `R` if it ends there); the far end is a spectator: type $A/B$ fix it (full/empty), type $Q$ has its far colour fixed to 1 (`01` on the far pair). Hence 4 valid patterns per outer link (near end empty, full, colour 1, colour 2) and $4^8=65\,536$ outer patterns (KS: $3^8$).
* The 3-qubit "6-state codeword" variant (any injective map of the 6 link states into 3 qubits) gives 12 data + 24 outer = 36 qubits; it is reported as a register-size row only (plan 001 §8.5), no circuit is built for it.

## R3. Physical states and counts

Per link the type decides the end spins: $A,B\to(j_L,j_R)=(0,0)$, $Q\to(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$. For an assignment of types to the 12 links, the Gauss kernel factorises over corners (the end spaces of different corners are different qubits, link constraints are already fixed by the types): at corner $c$ the kernel of $\sum_a(\sum_eG^a_e)^2$ on the product of the four end spaces (1-dim for $A/B$ ends, 2-dim for $Q$ ends) is computed numerically in the qubit representation (`eigh` on ≤ 16×16), labelled like METHODS §4 by the inner-pair spin $J$, phase fixed by the first component within $10^{-10}$ of the largest modulus positive. A physical state is the tensor product of the four corner tensors over the 48-bit computational basis (COO, ≤ 1296 entries), far ends as in R2. Number of singlets per corner = the KS fusion count, so
$$N_{\rm phys}=\sum_{j\in\{0,\frac12\}^{12}}\prod_cn_c(j)\;2^{\#\{\ell:j_\ell=0\}}=\mathbf{38\,432}$$
(measured by three routes: types × fusion; explicit kernels; the 433 KS labels weighted by $2^{n_0}$; Lean kernel `decide`). Closed sector (all outer links singlets): $4352=2^8\times17$ — per outer singlet choice 16 vacua ($\{A,B\}^4$ on the inner links) and one loop state $|\ell\rangle$ (all inner links $Q$, colours contracted around the loop). Measured: Gauss residual of sampled states $5\times10^{-16}$, norms $1\pm8\times10^{-16}$, total COO size 508 688 entries. The **inner span** (span of the inner components of all physical states) is the **full** 1296-dim code space (corner factorisation, exact; research 200 §5).

**Reference closed vacuum** $|{\rm vac}_A\rangle$: inner $b,r$ of type $A$, $t,l$ of type $B$ (the unique vacuum with $\mathrm{Tr}U_P|{\rm vac}_A\rangle\ne0$), every outer link in its near-end-empty singlet; $|{\rm vac}_B\rangle$ = inner types $B,B,A,A$.

## R4. Inner operators and their structure

All targets are inner operators on the 1296 codes (they act as $O\otimes1_{\rm outer}$ on physical states, which are an invariant subspace):
* $H_E^{\rm in}=\mathrm{diag}\big(\tfrac34\,\#\{\ell\in\{b,r,t,l\}:\text{type }Q\}\big)$; $U_E^{\rm in}(\alpha)=e^{-i\alpha H_E^{\rm in}}$.
* $H_B^{\rm in}=-(W+W^\dagger)|_{\rm codes}$: real symmetric; $[W,G^a_c{}^{\rm in}]=0$ for the inner-end part of every corner generator (measured 0.0). *Heights* $h(A)=0,h(Q)=1,h(B)=2$ for $b,r$ and $h(B)=0,h(Q)=1,h(A)=2$ for $t,l$: $W$ raises all four heights by one (measured; so $W^3=0$). Spectrum: $\pm4\sqrt2$ (×1), $\pm2$ (×48), $\pm1$ (×32), $0$ (×1134). **Bright-pair form (measured):**
$$H_B^{\rm in}=-\sum_{p=1}^{81}\kappa_p\big(|u_p\rangle\langle v_p|+|v_p\rangle\langle u_p|\big),\quad\kappa_p\in\{1\ (32),\,2\ (48),\,4\sqrt2\ (1)\},$$
$\{u_p,v_p\}_p$ orthonormal, real, amplitudes in $\{\pm1,\pm\tfrac1{\sqrt2},\pm\tfrac12,\pm\tfrac14\}$, 990 codes dark. Hence $U_B^{\rm in}(\beta)=\prod_p\exp\!\big(i\beta\kappa_p(|u_p\rangle\langle v_p|+{\rm h.c.})\big)$: 81 commuting two-level rotations by angles $2\kappa_p\beta$.
* Closed bright block in $\{|{\rm vac}_A\rangle,|\ell\rangle,|{\rm vac}_B\rangle\}$: $\langle\ell|\mathrm{Tr}U_P|{\rm vac}_A\rangle=\langle{\rm vac}_B|\mathrm{Tr}U_P|\ell\rangle=4$, $H_E|\ell\rangle=3|\ell\rangle$,
$$H_{\rm closed}(g^2)=\tfrac{g^2}2\mathrm{diag}(0,3,0)-\tfrac4{g^2}\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad\lambda\in\Big\{0,\ \tfrac34g^2\pm\sqrt{\tfrac9{16}g^4+32g^{-4}}\Big\},$$
the other 14 vacua per outer choice have energy 0. KS for comparison (METHODS §4): $\big[\begin{smallmatrix}0&-2/g^2\\-2/g^2&3g^2/2\end{smallmatrix}\big]$, $\lambda=\tfrac34g^2\pm\sqrt{\tfrac9{16}g^4+4g^{-4}}$.

## R5. Shared observables versus Kogut–Susskind (definitions)

Vacuum $|\Omega\rangle$: KS $|0000\rangle$ (all 12 links $j=0$), rishon $|{\rm vac}_A\rangle$ (R3). Step $U_1=U_B(\beta)U_E(\alpha)$ (electric layer first), $n$ steps:
1. electric energy per inner link $E_{\rm link}(n)=\tfrac14\langle\Omega|U_1^{-n}H_E^{\rm inner}U_1^n|\Omega\rangle$;
2. plaquette $P(n)=\langle\Omega|U_1^{-n}\,\tfrac12(\mathrm{Tr}U_P+\mathrm{Tr}U_P^\dagger)\,U_1^n|\Omega\rangle=-\tfrac12\langle H_B\rangle$; identity for both theories: $P(1)=0$ ($U_E|\Omega\rangle$ is a phase, $U_B$ conserves $H_B$, $\langle\Omega|H_B|\Omega\rangle=0$), so the table also reports $n=2$;
3. Loschmidt echo $\mathcal L(n)=|\langle\Omega|U_1^n|\Omega\rangle|^2$;
4. closed-sector spectrum of $H(g^2)$ at $g^2\in\{0.5,1,2\}$ with multiplicities (rishon: per outer singlet choice, 15 states at 0 and the two bright levels).
Closed forms at $n=1$ with $\theta_{\rm KS}=2\beta$, $\theta_{\rm R}=4\sqrt2\beta$: $E_{\rm link}=\tfrac34\sin^2\theta_{\rm KS}$ vs $\tfrac38\sin^2\theta_{\rm R}$; $\mathcal L=\cos^2\theta_{\rm KS}$ vs $\tfrac14(1+\cos\theta_{\rm R})^2$. Values at the nominal and alternate angles: research 200 §8 (locked in M5-O1 to $10^{-12}$: closed-form against $3\times3$/$2\times2$ exponentials and against the production targets restricted to the closed states).

## R6. Verification (what is certified, what is sampled)

The laptop evaluator's `physical`/`inner_span` methods need a dense $N_{\rm phys}\times N_{\rm phys}$ target and dense per-outer-pattern blocks; at $N_{\rm phys}=38\,432$ (and $\sim10^5$ (state, outer-pattern) pairs) this exceeds 20 GB and is not used. Track R uses `su2plaq.rishon.verify.verify`:
1. **Inner-block certification (all physical states).** `evaluate(qc, enc.inner_view(), V_in, gauss=False, tol_err, engine)` with the unchanged laptop evaluator: the inner view's "physical vectors" are the 1296 inner codes (outer bits 0), its target the inner operator $V_{\rm in}$. The verdict's `block_error` $=\|M-e^{i\varphi}V_{\rm in}\|_2$ (one global phase), `leak_amplitude` $=\|X\|_2$ (exact on the sparse engine), `dropped_total` and `certified` are the laptop's. **Theorem (inner-block soundness).** For an outer-blind circuit $U=U_{\rm in}\otimes1_{\rm outer}$ and any physical state $\Psi=\sum_o|o\rangle\otimes\psi^o$ with $\sum_o\|\psi^o\|^2=1$ and $\psi^o$ in the code space, write the simulated inner operator as $M+X$ with $M$ the code-space block and $X$ the part leaving the code space or dirtying ancillas. Outputs for different outer patterns $o$ are orthogonal, so
$$\big\|(U-e^{i\varphi}V_{\rm in}\otimes1)\Psi\big\|\;\le\;\|M-e^{i\varphi}V_{\rm in}\|_2+\|X\|_2+d,$$
where $d$ is the certified pruning bound of the sparse engine (`dropped_total`). The code reports exactly this **sum** of the three terms, `op_norm_error = block_error + leak_amplitude + dropped_total ≤ tol_err` (`block_error` $=\|M-e^{i\varphi}V_{\rm in}\|_2$, `leak_amplitude` $=\|X\|_2$): it is the triangle-inequality bound of METHODS §6 with the code-space isometry (Lean `verdict_sound`, `opNorm_add_three_le_of_orth`). The quadrature form $\sqrt{\|M-e^{i\varphi}V_{\rm in}\|_2^2+\|X\|_2^2}$ would also be valid for the exact engine (the two parts are orthogonal) but is tighter than what is reported; the sum is what the verdict uses (reports/tests/203 N3; prompts/202 §8). Because the inner span is the whole code space (R3), the condition is also necessary. The check is first order in every deviation (it compares full vectors on the code space, so the first-order blindness of the projected $A$-matrix of METHODS §6 does not arise).
2. **Outer reads.** If any outer qubit is read (`classify_outer_gates`), the inner view is unsound and the verdict is `outer_read_unsupported` (not ok). The block-wise exact path (per read-pattern group $r$, the span $S_r=\bigoplus_{\rm inner\ types}\bigotimes_cS_c(o_c)$ from the corner factorisation) is specified for M6, not built in M5.
3. **Gauss residual (sampled, binding tolerance).** Outputs $U\Psi_k=\sum_o|o\rangle\otimes M\psi_k^o$ of the 17 closed reference states and 256 seeded physical states (seed 20261104) are assembled from one sparse `run_batch` of the inner codes and checked with the 2-qubit end generators of R1: $\max_{k,c,a}\|G^a_cU\Psi_k\|\le$ `tol_gauss` ($10^{-10}$ exact angles; $N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon+10^{-9}$ synthesised; METHODS §6). Amplitudes with modulus below $10^{-14}$ (absolute, `_AMP_DROP`; harmless for unit-norm states) are dropped when the outputs are assembled. Item 1 already implies $\|G^a_cU\Psi_k\|\le2\,$`op_norm_error`; the sample is a cross-check and the first-order detector the binding rule asks for.
4. **Explicit physical cross-check (sampled, tester tool).** $A_{k'k}=\langle\Psi_{k'}|U|\Psi_k\rangle$ on the sample versus $\langle\Psi_{k'}|V_{\rm in}\otimes1|\Psi_k\rangle$; not a verdict input.
Verdict: `ok` iff the inner-block verdict is ok and the Gauss residual is within tolerance; `certified` = the laptop's flag (engine accuracy versus $\min$(tol_err, tol_gauss); aer_mps only at $\ge10^{-9}$). Tolerances, prune cap ($10^{-2}$ tol_err) and the synthesised-circuit rule are METHODS §6 unchanged; no tolerance is new.

**Outer electric row (separate; sampled in M5, certified in M5x).** The outer electric row of $U_E$ is not part of the inner-block verdict above (the outer qubits are only touched by the row's own gadgets). Two separate checks:
* *Sampled (M5, `results.outer_electric_check`, `results/rishon/UE_outer_row.json`):* a seeded diagonal-phase check on at most 4000 valid 48-bit codes (drawn from the codes of the 17 closed and 256 seeded physical states), comparing the output with $e^{-i\frac34\alpha\,\#Q}$ up to one global phase; reported with `sampled: true, certified: false`. Evidence, not a certificate.
* *Certified (M5x, `results.outer_electric_certify`, `results/rishon/UE_outer_row_certified.json`, prompts/202 O1):* the row is a tensor product of 12 per-link gadgets on disjoint qubits; the gates are partitioned by link ($q\,\mathrm{div}\,4$; a gate across links makes the row non-certifiable), each link's exact $16\times16$ operator $V_\ell$ is compared on the 6 sector codes with $T_\ell=\mathrm{diag}(e^{-i\frac34\alpha[\text{type}=Q]})$ after one phase $\varphi_\ell$, and $\epsilon_\ell=\|V_\ell[:,\text{sector}]-e^{i\varphi_\ell}T_\ell\|_2$. On every superposition of valid 48-bit codes, hence on all 38 432 physical states,
$$\Big\|\Big(\bigotimes_\ell V_\ell-e^{i\sum_\ell\varphi_\ell}\bigotimes_\ell T_\ell\Big)\Psi\Big\|\le\sum_{\ell=0}^{11}\epsilon_\ell=\texttt{error\_bound}$$
(telescoping of a product of unitaries, as the laptop's `prod_sub_prod_le`/`synth_bound`, METHODS §11); `certified` iff no cross-link gate, no ancilla register and `error_bound` $\le$ `tol_err` ($10^{-10}$). Exhaustive over every product of per-link sector codes ($6^{12}$ codes, a superset of the $6^4\cdot4^8$ valid codes of R2, so the bound is stronger than needed). Measured: all $\epsilon_\ell=0$ at $\alpha=0.37$, 5 ms. *Arithmetic:* $V_\ell$ and $\epsilon_\ell$ are evaluated in float64 with no explicit rounding allowance; the rounding error is at most about $4\times10^{-15}$ per link (audit 201 N3), far below `tol_err` $=10^{-10}$, so `certified` means "exact up to float64 rounding". *Lean coverage (audit 201 N1):* Lean proves only the structure of the rendered outer-row gate list (12 consecutive gadgets, `ue_outer_gates_structure`) and the quartet phase of one gadget on a bare 4-bit link code (`ue_gadget_sector`, symbolically for all $\alpha$); applying the gadget lemma to each of the 12 links and composing them over the 48-qubit register is the tensor-product argument above, not a Lean theorem.
