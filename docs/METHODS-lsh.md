# METHODS-lsh — loop-string-hadron (LSH) encoding (milestones M7/M8)

Methods and conventions specific to the LSH encoding, with sources. Complements `docs/METHODS.md` (its §1–§11 apply unchanged: geometry, Hamiltonian, routes A/B, evaluator, metrics, Lean) and reuses `docs/METHODS-spinnet.md` §2–§4 (gauge, decoding rule, phase walk). Written by the planner on 2026-11-10 (`prompts/400-lsh-m7-plan.md`); numbers quoted as "measured" come from the planner prototypes listed in `reports/research/400-lsh-model.md` (coding env, qiskit 2.5.2, qiskit-aer 0.17.2, pygridsynth 1.2.0). Section L11 is filled with the numbers of the production code at step 7.11.

Jargon, once: *loop-string-hadron (LSH) formulation* = rewriting of SU(2) lattice gauge theory in which each site carries only gauge-invariant integers (for pure gauge: loop numbers); *prepotentials / Schwinger bosons* = two harmonic oscillators at each link end from which $U$ and $E$ are built; *point splitting* = replacing a 4-valent site by two 3-valent (trivalent) sites joined by a zero-length *virtual link*; *loop number* $\ell_{ij}$ = units of flux running through a trivalent site from leg $i$ to leg $j$; *flux* $N_i=2j_i$ of a leg; *Abelian Gauss law (AGL)* = the flux of a link read at its two ends agrees; *site code* = the 3 bits of a site; *'in' / 'out' site* (our labels) = the trivalent site of a corner holding its two inner / two outer links; *inner code* = the 12 data bits; *bright / dark* = inner codes the plaquette moves / annihilates; *transcoder* (our label) = reversible circuit rewriting the LSH data bits in place into spin-network data bits; *frame, flag, phase walk* as in METHODS-spinnet.

## L1. Model and sources
Pure-gauge SU(2) Kogut–Susskind theory on the plaquette with 8 outer links (METHODS §1–§2), written in LSH variables on the point-split diagram: 8 trivalent sites, **(inner | outer) pairing** at every corner (`geometry.point_split`; METHODS §5), no matter sites.

II "Truncation and binary representation" (qubits per loop number, flux numbers). Background: I. Raychowdhury, arXiv:1804.01304, Eur. Phys. J. C 79, 235 (2019) (pure-gauge point splitting); R. Anishetty, I. Raychowdhury, arXiv:1408.6331, Phys. Rev. D 90, 114503 (2014); Z. Davoudi, I. Raychowdhury, A. Shaw, arXiv:2009.11802, Phys. Rev. D 104, 074505 (2021) and Z. Davoudi, A. F. Shaw, J. R. Pairing note: RS20 split every site as $(+e_1,+e_2\,|\,-e_1,-e_2)$, which differs from ours at corners $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$. In that basis $H_B$ on the 433 states has 400 non-zero entries (up to 4 per row, moduli including $\tfrac{\sqrt3}{4},\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\sqrt3$), depends on the outer spins and acts on 6 sites (18 qubits); in ours it is a matching with 256 entries acting on 4 sites (measured, research 400 §6; same spectrum to $3.3\cdot10^{-15}$).

## L2. Site basis, truncation, register
Trivalent site with legs $(1,2,3)$: orthonormal loop basis $|\ell_{12},\ell_{23},\ell_{31}\rangle$ (RS20 Sec. V),
$$N_1=\ell_{31}+\ell_{12},\quad N_2=\ell_{12}+\ell_{23},\quad N_3=\ell_{23}+\ell_{31},\qquad \ell_{12}=j_1+j_2-j_3,\ \ell_{23}=j_2+j_3-j_1,\ \ell_{31}=j_3+j_1-j_2,\quad j_i=\tfrac{N_i}2 .$$
Legs: 'in' site of corner $c$: $(1,2,3)=(p(c),n(c),v)$ with $(p,n)=(l,b),(b,r),(r,t),(t,l)$ for $c_0..c_3$ (previous/next inner link along the loop $b\to r\to t\to l$); 'out' site: (outer 1, outer 2, $v$) with outer links $(o_0,o_1),(o_2,o_3),(o_4,o_5),(o_6,o_7)$; $v$ = virtual link of the corner.
Truncation: $j\le\tfrac12$ on the 12 physical links, $J\le1$ on the virtual links ⇒ every loop number $\in\{0,1\}$ ⇒ 1 qubit per loop number (RS20b: $N=\lceil\log_2(\bar j+1)\rceil$), 3 qubits per site, **24 qubits**. Site code $s=\ell_{12}+2\ell_{23}+4\ell_{31}$ (bit 0 = $\ell_{12}$). **Valid site codes** = flux cap $N_1\le1$, $N_2\le1$: $\{0,1,2,4,6\}$ = `000` $(0,0,0)$, `001` $(\tfrac12,\tfrac12,0)$, `010` $(0,\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$, `100` $(\tfrac12,0,\tfrac12)$, `110` $(\tfrac12,\tfrac12,1)$ (bit strings written $\ell_{31}\ell_{23}\ell_{12}$; spins $(j_1,j_2,J)$).
Registers (canonical frame; `layout_of` maps through the register names): data `d[0..11]`, 'in' site $c$ at qubits $3c,3c+1,3c+2=(\ell_{pn},\ell_{nv},\ell_{vp})$; outer `o[0..11]` (read-only), 'out' site $c$ at $12+3c,12+3c+1,12+3c+2=(\ell_{12},\ell_{2v},\ell_{v1})$; ancillas after. 24-bit code $=\sum_{c}s^{\rm in}_c8^{c}+\sum_c s^{\rm out}_c8^{4+c}$.

## L3. Abelian Gauss law, physical codes, counts
Validity of a 24-bit code: (i) all 8 site codes valid; (ii) AGL on the inner links, $N_n(\text{in},c)=N_p(\text{in},c{+}1)$ (4 conditions, named `b`, `r`, `t`, `l`); (iii) AGL on the virtual links, $N_v(\text{in},c)=N_v(\text{out},c)$ (`v0`..`v3`). Outer links have one end (far end = spectator, METHODS §3) and no condition.
Equivalence with the Gauss-law space: label $((j_0..j_{11}),(J_0..J_3))\mapsto$ site codes by L2 (`code_of_label`); the valid codes are exactly the images of the 433 route-B labels (METHODS §5), which label the corner-singlet basis of route A (METHODS §4). Measured by brute force over the $5^8=390\,625$ site-valid states: **433** valid codes; inner register: **47** inner codes of $5^4=625$ (16 bright, 31 dark = some site in `110`); 305 distinct outer-site patterns (1 or 2 states each); without the flux cap the AGL alone admits 82 inner patterns and 5920 states of $2^{24}$ (the cap is part of the rule). Kernel-checked: `lean/Su2Plaq/LSH/Counts.lean` (L10).

## L4. Operators
Site-local loop operators (RS20; $\tilde a_\alpha=\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}a_\beta$, $\epsilon_{12}=1$): $\mathcal L^{++}_{ij}=a^\dagger(i)\!\cdot\!\tilde a^\dagger(j)$, $\mathcal L^{+-}_{ij}=a^\dagger(i)\!\cdot\!a(j)$, $\mathcal L^{-+}_{ij}=a(i)\!\cdot\!a^\dagger(j)$, $\mathcal L^{--}_{ij}=a(i)\!\cdot\!\tilde a(j)$. Action on the loop basis for the pair $(12)$, $S=\ell_{12}+\ell_{23}+\ell_{31}$ (RS20 factorisation table):
$$\mathcal L^{++}_{12}|\ell\rangle=\sqrt{(\ell_{12}+1)(S+2)}\,|\ell_{12}{+}1\rangle,\quad \mathcal L^{--}_{12}|\ell\rangle=\sqrt{\ell_{12}(S+1)}\,|\ell_{12}{-}1\rangle,$$
$$\mathcal L^{+-}_{12}|\ell\rangle=-\sqrt{(\ell_{31}+1)\ell_{23}}\,|\ell_{31}{+}1,\ell_{23}{-}1\rangle,\quad \mathcal L^{-+}_{12}|\ell\rangle=-\sqrt{\ell_{31}(\ell_{23}+1)}\,|\ell_{31}{-}1,\ell_{23}{+}1\rangle .$$
Dressed ladder factor (`ladder_amplitude`): $\langle\ell'|(N_2+1)^{-1/2}\mathcal L^{\sigma_1\sigma_2}_{12}(N_1+1)^{-1/2}|\ell\rangle$ ($N_1$ evaluated on $|\ell\rangle$, $N_2$ on $|\ell'\rangle$). Link operator $U=U_LU_R$, $U_L=\tfrac1{\sqrt{N_L+1}}\big(\begin{smallmatrix}a_2^\dagger&a_1\\-a_1^\dagger&a_2\end{smallmatrix}\big)$, $U_R=\big(\begin{smallmatrix}a_1^\dagger&a_2^\dagger\\-a_2&a_1\end{smallmatrix}\big)\tfrac1{\sqrt{N_R+1}}$ (RS20 Eqs. (24)–(25)). Vertex factors (RS20 "Vertex factors and contractions"; incoming leg $p$, outgoing leg $n$; each entry is to be dressed as above): type of a corner from the loop orientation (arrive along $+$link → its R end, along $-$link → L end with $U^\dagger$; leave along $+$link → L end, along $-$link → R end with $U^\dagger$):
$$c_0\,(l,b)\ \mathrm{LL}:\begin{pmatrix}\mathcal L^{-+}&-\mathcal L^{--}\\ \mathcal L^{++}&\mathcal L^{+-}\end{pmatrix},\quad c_1\,(b,r)\ \mathrm{RL}:\begin{pmatrix}\mathcal L^{++}&\mathcal L^{+-}\\-\mathcal L^{-+}&\mathcal L^{--}\end{pmatrix},\quad c_2\,(r,t)\ \mathrm{RR}:\begin{pmatrix}\mathcal L^{+-}&-\mathcal L^{++}\\ \mathcal L^{--}&\mathcal L^{-+}\end{pmatrix},\quad c_3\,(t,l)\ \mathrm{LR}:\begin{pmatrix}-\mathcal L^{--}&-\mathcal L^{-+}\\ \mathcal L^{+-}&-\mathcal L^{++}\end{pmatrix}.$$
$$\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P=\mathrm{tr}\big(\mathcal V_{c_0}\mathcal V_{c_1}\mathcal V_{c_2}\mathcal V_{c_3}\big),\qquad H_B=-\big(\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P+\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P^\dagger\big),\qquad H_E=\sum_{\ell}\tfrac{N_\ell}2\big(\tfrac{N_\ell}2+1\big)$$
(inner links read at the $n$-leg of their 'in' site, outer links at their 'out' site; virtual links carry no electric energy, RS20). Truncation = dropping every term that leaves the valid codes (identical to projecting each $U$ to $j\le\tfrac12$, since the four links are distinct). Code: `su2plaq.lsh.operators` (`VERTEX`, `ladder_amplitude`, `loop_ladder`, `plaquette_inner`, `K_lsh`, `h_magnetic_lsh`, `h_electric_lsh`), built from loop-number arithmetic only (no 6j, no electric basis).
Measured (research 400 §3; locked by M7-K1, K2, X1): ladder formulas and vertex factors hold in the oscillator Fock space ($\le1.2\cdot10^{-15}$); on the valid site codes the non-zero dressed factors are `000`→`001`: $+1$; `001`→`000`: $+1$; `010`→`100`: $-1$; `100`→`010`: $-\tfrac12$ (moduli = the 6j coefficients $F$ of METHODS §5); $K_{\rm LSH}=-(P+P^{\mathsf T})$ on the 47 inner codes **equals $K_{\rm inner}$ of route B entry by entry** ($1.8\cdot10^{-15}$, no sign gauge): a matching of 8 bright pairs, partner = per-site flip `000`↔`001`, `010`↔`100`, couplings $k=-2$ (all $w_c=0$), $-\tfrac12$ (all $w_c=1$), $+1$ (two $w_c=1$) with $w_c=N_v\bmod2=[J_c=\tfrac12]$; 31 dark rows; $H_B$ is block-diagonal in the outer-site pattern with every block the restriction of $K_{\rm LSH}$ (the circuit needs no outer information); spectra of $\tfrac{g^2}2H_E+\tfrac1{g^2}H_B$ agree with routes A and B to $1.0\cdot10^{-14}$ at $g^2\in\{0.5,1,2\}$.

## L5. Target and gauge
Target $=$ the LSH operator in route-B label order: $U_B(\beta)=e^{-i\beta H_B^{\rm LSH}}$, $U_E(\alpha)=e^{-i\alpha H_E^{\rm inner}}$, steps as METHODS §2; it is the same $433\times433$ matrix as the spin-network target (`SpinNet.target`), $\le10^{-12}$. Route A relates by the real diagonal gauge $D=\pm1$ of `gauge_align(H_A,H_B^{LSH})` (64 minus signs, residual $8.9\cdot10^{-16}$): $V_{\rm LSH}=D^\dagger V_AD$. As in METHODS-spinnet §2, $K$ is a matching, so the sign of $\beta$ is a basis-sign convention at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$ ($U_B(-\beta)=D'U_B(\beta)D'$, one $-1$ per bright pair); "$-\beta$ rejected" in M7-S1 pins the circuit to the target's gauge, it is not a physics check. Inner target: $e^{-i\beta K_{\rm LSH}}$ on the 47 inner codes (sorted), electric phase $\tfrac34\sum_cN_n(c)$.

## L6. Decoding for the Gauss check (`decode_state`)
Physical code $k$ ↦ `gauge[k]` × route-A state $k$. A code with a valid data part (one of the 47 inner codes) and site-valid 'out' sites that breaks the AGL on a virtual link ($47\cdot625-433=28\,942$ codes) ↦ one electric basis state in which every spin-$\tfrac12$ end at corner $c$ is a $G^z$ eigenstate: inner ends $+\tfrac12$ if $J_{\rm in}(c)\ne1$, $-\tfrac12$ if $J_{\rm in}(c)=1$; outer ends $-\tfrac12$ if $J_{\rm out}(c)\ne1$, $+\tfrac12$ if $J_{\rm out}(c)=1$; far ends $m=+\tfrac12$ (cg convention: $G^z=-m_L$ on an 'out' end, $+m_R$ on an 'in' end). A corner with $J_{\rm in}\ne J_{\rm out}$ then has total $G^z\in\{\pm\tfrac12,\pm1,\pm\tfrac32,\pm2\}$, so the Gauss residual is $\ge\tfrac12$; the map code ↦ key is injective and no key lies in the support of a physical state (measured on all 28 942 codes: residual in $[0.5,2.0]$). Hence `decode_state` is an isometry on the decodable code space and the span-leak bound of prompts/004 ($\|s\|\le4\cdot$`gauss_residual`) keeps its hypothesis. Data part outside the 47 inner codes, or an 'out' site outside the flux cap → `ValueError` (the evaluator never decodes those: invalid data is counted as leak, and the outer register is never written).

## L7. General circuit
**Why a Toffoli-type gate per site.** The plaquette move on a bright site is $s\mapsto s\oplus(1,0,0)\oplus w(s)(1,1,1)$; the 8 pair classes have 8 different 12-bit difference vectors, so no CNOT-only frame maps the move to a single-qubit $X$.
**Transcoder** $T$ (per 'in' site, $(x,y,z)=(\ell_{pn},\ell_{nv},\ell_{vp})$ at qubits $3c,3c+1,3c+2$): `cx(y,z)` ($z\leftarrow w=N_v\bmod2$); `cx(y,x)` ($x\leftarrow N_n=2j_{n(c)}$); `rccx(z,x,y)` ($y\leftarrow[J_c=1]$). Afterwards wires $0,3,6,9$ hold $j_b,j_r,j_t,j_l$, wires $1,4,7,10$ the corner bits, wires $2,5,8,11$ $w_c=j_{p(c)}\oplus j_{n(c)}$: the spin-network data code plus redundant parities. 20 CX, 16 T, no ancilla. `rccx` (relative-phase Toffoli, Maslov arXiv:1508.03273; METHODS §7, §11.1) differs from the Toffoli here only by the phase $-i$ on site state `010`; both members of every bright pair contain the same number of such sites, so the phase commutes with $U_B$ (measured exact with either control order).
**$U_B$** $=T^\dagger\,[\text{frame}\cdot H\cdot\text{flags}\cdot\text{walk}\cdot\text{flags}^{-1}\cdot H\cdot\text{frame}^{-1}]\,T$ with the spin-network construction of METHODS-spinnet §4 on the transcoded wires: frame `cx(3,9); cx(0,3); cx(0,6)` (target wire 0); flags $A,B,F,P,Q,G_1,G_2$ on ancillas `a[0..6]` by 7 `rccx`; walk `rz(θ0); cx(F,0) rz(θF) cx(F,0); cx(G1,0) rz(θ1) cx(G1,0); cx(G2,0) rz(θ2) cx(G2,0)` (Walsh series, Welch et al. arXiv:1306.3991). Angles are solved at build time from the LSH coupling table, $\sum_r\theta_r(-1)^{p_r(y)}=2\beta k(y)$ on all 47 codes: $(\theta_0,\theta_F,\theta_1,\theta_2)=(-\tfrac72,-1,3,\tfrac32)\beta$. Measured: **94 CX, 4 rotations, 88 fixed T, 7 ancillas, depth 87**.
**$U_E$** stand-alone: $N_n=\ell_{pn}+\ell_{nv}$ at each 'in' site, never both 1 ⇒ `p(-3α/4)` on both qubits (8 phases, no CX). **Step**: the electric layer is applied inside the transcoded frame, `p(-3α/4)` (order 1) or `p(-3α/8)` twice (order 2) on wires $0,3,6,9$ — the transcoder is a permutation of basis states (up to the phase above, which is diagonal), so a diagonal layer may be applied on either side of it; 8 (order 1) / 12 (order 2) rotations, CX unchanged. **Outer electric row** (separate row, METHODS §2): $N_1+N_2=2\ell_{12}+\ell_{2v}+\ell_{v1}$ at each 'out' site ⇒ `p(-3α/2)`, `p(-3α/4)`, `p(-3α/4)` (12 phases; reads 12 outer qubits, 305 blocks).
Skeleton language: `su2plaq.lsh.walk` (steps `cx`, `x`, `h`, `rccx`, `rz`, `p_alpha`; `classical_slots`, `solve_angles`, `angle_fractions`, `mirror`, `skeleton`, `build`).

## L12. Compression task (M8; `prompts/401`; loop of METHODS §9, registry and headline rule of METHODS §12)
Unit: the first-order step $U=U_B(\beta)\,U_E(\alpha)$ on the 24-qubit LSH register (12 data qubits, 12 read-only outer qubits; ancillas $\le32$, cap 56 qubits), exact up to one global phase on the 433 physical codes at $(0.37,0.61)$ and, with the same gate skeleton, at $(0.91,0.23)$, $(1.70,1.00)$; validity `total_error` $\le10^{-10}$, Gauss residual $\le10^{-10}$ (a final state that breaks the Abelian Gauss law or the flux cap is an error, L6), clean ancillas, allowed gates of METHODS §7. Archive key $(\mathrm{CX},T_{10^{-6}},\mathrm{depth}_{\rm CT},\mathrm{qubits})$; headline by the robust key of L14. Proposers are free to recode the sites (transcoder of L7) or not.
`lsh` is an entry of the shared encoding registry (`su2plaq.encodings.registry`, METHODS §12): factory `su2plaq.encodings.lsh:LSH`, `stamp="encoding"`, evaluator files `encodings/lsh.py`, `lsh/operators.py`, `exact/spinnet.py`, `exact/compare.py`, `lsh/walk.py`, `circuits/lsh.py` (appended to the shared list: `evaluator_sha256 = encoding_sha256 = code_hash_for("lsh")`), hooks in `su2plaq.lsh.loop` (seeds, spec text, brief note, Lean hook, angle-linearity), which patches nothing and is in no hash list.
What the loop measures. With the (inner | outer) pairing the LSH loop basis is the spin-network basis in another bit code (L7, L8), so every spin-network circuit $C$ gives the LSH circuit $T^\dagger CT$ at $+40$ CX, $+32$ T (16 CX linear, 24 CX and 32 T in four relative-phase Toffoli pairs; a relative-phase Toffoli needs 3 CX: Song, Klappenecker, arXiv:quant-ph/0312225; a CNOT-only recoding does not exist, L13). ## L13. Raw-bit structure of the inner codes (kernel-checked in `lean/Su2Plaq/LSH/Facts.lean`; numerics in research 401 §3)
Site bits $(x_c,y_c,z_c)=(\ell_{pn},\ell_{nv},\ell_{vp})$ of 'in' site $c$, $w_c=y_c\oplus z_c$. On the 47 inner codes:
(i) dark $\Leftrightarrow\exists c:\ y_c\wedge z_c$ (`coupling_zero_iff_dark`, `dark_site_is_and`);
(ii) the plaquette move of a bright code flips $x_c$ where $w_c=0$ and $y_c,z_c$ where $w_c=1$ (`move_per_site`); the 8 bright pairs have 8 different move vectors, so no CNOT-only frame exists (`eight_differences`);
(iii) on bright codes $\sum_cy_c=\sum_cz_c\in\{0,1,2\}$ and $2K=-4,+2,-1$ respectively, i.e. $k=-2+3\sum_cy_c-\tfrac92(y_0y_2+y_1y_3)$ (`coupling_by_y_weight`); the parity $\bigoplus_cy_c$ is unchanged by the move and equals $[k=+1]$ (`y_parity_invariant`);
(iv) Abelian Gauss law as a parity: $x_c\oplus y_c=x_{c+1}\oplus z_{c+1}$ = the bit of inner link $n(c)$ (`link_bit_two_ways`).
These are facts about the problem given to the proposers (`compress/specs/lsh-structure.json`, written by `su2plaq.lsh.loop.write_structure`), not a circuit.

## L14. Headline and stop rule (M8)
Headline = the valid archive entry with the smallest robust key $\big(\mathrm{CX},\sum_{i=1}^{20}T_{10^{-6}}(\alpha_i,\beta_i),\rho,\sum_{i=1}^{20}\mathrm{depth}_{\rm CT},\mathrm{qubits}\big)$ of METHODS §12 (`su2plaq.compress.robust`, angle set seed 20261107), with the hardenings of audit 302: the skeleton must be identical over the 20 pairs **and** the nominal and alternate pairs (N2); $\rho=0$ only if the LSH Lean exporter returns a certificate for the nominal circuit (N6; hook `su2plaq.lsh.loop.angle_linear`).
Stop rule: the loop ends after round 8, or at 20 proposer sessions (ideation counts as one), or after 3 consecutive rounds whose headline does not improve. "Improves" compares the records $(\mathrm{CX},\sum T,\rho,\mathrm{qubits})$ lexicographically — the depth component is left out (audit 302 §7.7: it is synthesis-word noise, $\le0.6\,\%$ between programs of equal structure) — with one exception: if CX, $N_{\rm rot}$ and $T_{\rm fixed}$ are all unchanged and $|\Delta\sum T|\le20\,N_{\rm rot}$, the $\sum T$ difference is a tie and $(\rho,\mathrm{qubits})$ decide. Justification of the band: at fixed structure $\sum T$ changes only through the Ross–Selinger fluctuation $\delta(\theta)$ of each rotation, and what a choice of coefficients can extract from it is $\lesssim1$ T per rotation and angle pair with 20 pairs (`prompts/104` §11 E3). Code: `su2plaq.lsh.loop.{stop_record, improves, stop_state}`; record file `results/compress/lsh/stop.json`.
