# METHODS — su2qc-encoding

Every numerical method and convention in use, with equations and sources. Written by the planner at kickoff (2026-11-04, `prompts/002`); later planner runs extend it (M3+). Numbers quoted as "measured" come from `scratch/planner/proto_*.py` run in the `coding` env on 2026-11-04 (see `reports/research/001-…`).

Jargon, once: *link* = edge of the lattice carrying an SU(2) matrix; *plaquette* = the square loop of 4 links; *corner* = a lattice site where 4 links meet; *Gauss's law* = the constraint that the 4 links at a corner combine to an SU(2) singlet (zero net colour charge); *physical state* = a state obeying Gauss's law at every corner; *Trotter step* = one factor of the product formula that approximates $e^{-iHt}$; *T gate* = the non-Clifford gate $\mathrm{diag}(1,e^{i\pi/4})$ whose count is the standard fault-tolerant cost measure; *Clifford+T* = the gate set $\{H,S,T,\mathrm{CNOT}\}$ (and inverses).

## 1. Geometry and conventions

* Plaquette with 8 outer links: inner $b=U_1(0,0)$: $(0,0)\to(1,0)$; $r=U_2(1,0)$: $(1,0)\to(1,1)$; $t=U_1(0,1)$: $(0,1)\to(1,1)$; $l=U_2(0,0)$: $(0,0)\to(0,1)$. Outer links 4–11 (`o0..o7`) in corner order: $o_0=U_1(-1,0)$, $o_1=U_2(0,-1)$ (into $(0,0)$); $o_2=U_1(1,0)$ (out of $(1,0)$), $o_3=U_2(1,-1)$ (into $(1,0)$); $o_4=U_1(1,1)$, $o_5=U_2(1,1)$ (out of $(1,1)$); $o_6=U_1(-1,1)$ (into $(0,1)$), $o_7=U_2(0,1)$ (out of $(0,1)$). Corners $c_0..c_3=(0,0),(1,0),(1,1),(0,1)$. Loop $U_P=U_bU_rU_t^\dagger U_l^\dagger$, orientation list `[(b,+1),(r,+1),(t,-1),(l,-1)]`. Source: `refs/su2_plaquette_truncations_encodings.md` §1.
* Spins $j\in\{0,\tfrac12,\dots,j_{\max}\}$ as floats in numerical code, `Fraction` in counting code.
* **Qubit conventions.** Little-endian: qubit $k$ is bit $k$ of the basis integer. `p(λ)=diag(1,e^{iλ})`, `rz(θ)=diag(e^{−iθ/2},e^{iθ/2})` (Qiskit 2.5.2). Ancillas start and end in $|0\rangle$. Register order in every circuit: data `d`, then outer controls `o` (if present), then ancillas.

## 2. Hamiltonian, Trotter step, angle map

Kogut–Susskind on this diagram with Paulo's normalisation (notes Eq. 16, $a=1$, $g_s=g_t=g$):
$$H=\frac{g^2}{2}H_E+\frac{1}{g^2}H_B,\qquad H_E=\sum_{\ell=1}^{12}j_\ell(j_\ell+1),\qquad H_B=-\big(\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P+\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P^\dagger\big).$$
(Standard KS texts use $-\tfrac{1}{2g^2}(\mathrm{Tr}U_P+{\rm h.c.})$; only the angle map changes.)
$$U_E(\alpha)=e^{-i\alpha H_E^{\rm inner}},\quad U_B(\beta)=e^{-i\beta H_B}=e^{+i\beta(\mathrm{Tr}U_P+\mathrm{Tr}U_P^\dagger)},\qquad \alpha=\tfrac{g^2dt}{2},\ \beta=\tfrac{dt}{g^2}.$$
Paulo's notebook implements $e^{+i\alpha_P\,\mathrm{Re\,Tr}\,\square}$ with $\alpha_P=2dt/(g_sg_ta)$ ⇒ $\alpha_P=2\beta$ (his compiled file: $dt=g=1$ ⇒ $\beta=1$). First-order step $U_1=U_B(\beta)U_E(\alpha)$ (electric layer first in the circuit); second order $U_2=U_E(\alpha/2)U_B(\beta)U_E(\alpha/2)$, error $O(dt^3)$ per step (Suzuki–Trotter; Hatano–Suzuki, arXiv:math-ph/0506007). Outer electric energy commutes with everything: $e^{-iHt}=e^{-iH_E^{\rm outer}t}\,e^{-i(H_E^{\rm inner}+H_B)t}$ exactly; main gate counts exclude the 8 outer phase gates, reported as a separate row. Nominal $(\alpha,\beta)=(0.37,0.61)$; alternates $(0.91,0.23)$, $(1.70,1.00)$ (generic, not multiples of $\pi/4$).

Electric step at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$: a $j=\tfrac12$ link has $j(j+1)=\tfrac34$ ⇒ phase $e^{-3i\alpha/4}$ on its $j=1$ codeword ⇒ `p(-3α/4)` on the link's $j$-qubit. (Paulo Eq. 35 writes $P(3\alpha_E/2)$ with $P(\lambda)=\mathrm{diag}(1,e^{-i\lambda})$ and $\alpha_E=g^2/4$ multiplying $T_L^2+T_R^2=2j(j+1)$ — same operator, opposite gate-sign convention.)

## 3. Link Hilbert space (electric basis) and operators

Source: su2qc-skqd `src/skqd/su2.py` (vendored into `src/su2plaq/su2/algebra.py`); Clebsch–Gordan with Condon–Shortley phase from Racah's closed formula (Edmonds, *Angular Momentum in Quantum Mechanics*, Eq. (3.6.11)).

* States $|j,m_L,m_R\rangle$, wavefunction $\sqrt{2j+1}\,D^j_{m_Lm_R}(U)$; local order at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$: $[(0,0,0),(\tfrac12,\tfrac12,\tfrac12),(\tfrac12,\tfrac12,-\tfrac12),(\tfrac12,-\tfrac12,\tfrac12),(\tfrac12,-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12)]$ (dimension 5). Colour index 0 ↔ $+\tfrac12$, 1 ↔ $-\tfrac12$.
* $U_{ij}$ = multiplication by $D^{1/2}_{ij}(U)$ projected to $j\le j_{\max}$:
  $$\langle j'm_L'm_R'|U_{ij}|j\,m_Lm_R\rangle=\sqrt{\tfrac{2j+1}{2j'+1}}\;C^{j'm_L'}_{\frac12 i;\,j m_L}\;C^{j'm_R'}_{\frac12 j;\,j m_R}.$$
* Generators $L_a=-J_a^{\mathsf T}$ on $m_L$, $R_a=+J_a$ on $m_R$; identities (verified to $4\cdot10^{-16}$, test M0-A1): $[L_a,U_{ij}]=-(T_aU)_{ij}$, $[R_a,U_{ij}]=+(UT_a)_{ij}$, $T_a=\sigma_a/2$; $\sum_aL_a^2=\sum_aR_a^2=j(j+1)$.
* Gauss generator at corner $c$: $G^a_c=\sum_{\text{links starting at }c}L_a+\sum_{\text{links ending at }c}R_a$. Physical: $G^a_c|\psi\rangle=0$ for all $a,c$.
* Multi-link electric index: $\mathrm{key}=\sum_{i=0}^{11}s_i5^i$ with $s_i$ the local index of link $i$ (link 0 least significant); inner part $\mathrm{key}\bmod625$, outer part $\lfloor\mathrm{key}/625\rfloor$. Far end of every outer link fixed to the first $m$ value ($+\tfrac12$ in the CG convention); it is a spectator index, so correctness on the 433 fixed-far-end states implies correctness on the 17 362-dimensional far-end-free space.

### 3.1 Paulo's basis and the adapter $V_P$
Paulo (notes Eqs. 22–28) expands $\Psi[U]=\sum\Psi^j_{mm'}D^{j*}_{mm'}(U)$, so his $|mm'\rangle_P\propto D^{1/2*}_{mm'}=\sum_{ab}\epsilon_{ma}D^{1/2}_{ab}\epsilon_{m'b}$ with $\epsilon=i\sigma^2$, $\epsilon_{+-}=1$. Hence, per link,
$$V_P:\quad|0\rangle_P=|0\rangle_{\rm CG},\qquad |mm'\rangle_P=s(m)\,s(m')\,|\tfrac12,-m,-m'\rangle_{\rm CG},\qquad s(\pm)=\pm1,$$
a symmetric signed permutation ($V_P=V_P^{\mathsf T}=V_P^{-1}$) with $V_PU^{\rm CG}_{ij}V_P^{\mathsf T}=\tilde U_{ij}$, where $\tilde U$ is Paulo's Eq. 28: $\tilde U_{ab}|0\rangle=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}s(a)s(b)|\bar a\bar b\rangle$, $\tilde U_{ab}|mm'\rangle=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\delta_{ma}\delta_{bm'}|0\rangle$ (Paulo order $[0,{+}{+},{+}{-},{-}{+},{-}{-}]$). Measured residual $2\cdot10^{-16}$ (test M0-A3). Eq. 25 of the notes omits the conjugate that Eq. 22 implies (typo; the rules in Eq. 28 are consistent with $V_P$).

### 3.2 The 12-qubit codec (Paulo Eq. 31) and the outer 2-qubit code
Per inner link $l\in\{b,r,t,l\}=0..3$, qubits $(3l,3l+1,3l+2)=(q_{m'},q_m,q_j)$; codes (bits $q_jq_mq_{m'}$): $|0\rangle\to000$, $|{+}{+}\rangle\to111$, $|{+}{-}\rangle\to110$, $|{-}{+}\rangle\to101$, $|{-}{-}\rangle\to100$; $001,010,011$ unused (don't-care). 625 valid inner codes. Outer link $k=0..7$ at qubits $(12+2k,12+2k+1)=(q_m,q_j)$ holding $(m_{\rm near},j)$: $j=0\to00$; $j=\tfrac12$: $q_j=1$, $q_m=1$ for $m_{\rm near}=+$, $0$ for $-$ ($m_{\rm near}=m_R$ for links ending at the corner, $m_L$ for links starting there; the far-end index is fixed). Physical vectors of the encoding = route-A states transformed by $V_P^{\otimes12}$ and mapped through these codes (superpositions over inner codes, entangled with the outer register).

## 4. Physical states (route A: corner singlets)

For each assignment of spins to the 12 links and each corner $c$ with ends $(a,b\,|\,1,2)$ (two inner, two outer), the corner singlets are the kernel of $\sum_a\big(\sum_{\text{ends}}G^a\big)^2$ on the $\prod(2j+1)$-dimensional product of the end spaces (an `eigh` on ≤ 16×16), labelled by the eigenvalue $J(J+1)$ of the inner-pair Casimir $(G^a_a+G^a_b)^2$, $J\in\{0,\tfrac12,1\}$; phase convention: largest-modulus component positive (skqd `vertex.py`). A physical state is the tensor product of the 4 corner tensors, stored as a sparse COO vector over the electric multi-index (≤ 1296 components). Counting rule (note §5, reproduced independently): number of singlets at a corner = number of $J$ with $(j_a,j_b,J)$ and $(j_1,j_2,J)$ admissible. Results at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$: **433** states; 2 closed (all outer $j=0$); 256 distinct link-spin strings, 95 of them with two or more states; far-end-weighted dimension $\sum_k\prod_{\rm outer}(2j+1)=17\,362$; at $j_{\max}=1$: 65 250 (route B count). Gauss residual $\max_{c,a}\|G^a_c\psi\|$ measured $5\cdot10^{-16}$.

Inner plaquette operator $W=\mathrm{Tr}(U_bU_rU_t^\dagger U_l^\dagger)=\sum_{abcd}U^{(b)}_{ab}U^{(r)}_{bc}(U^{(t)}_{dc})^\dagger(U^{(l)}_{ad})^\dagger$ (625×625; operator adjoint for the daggered links); $H_B^{\rm phys}=-(\Psi^\dagger(W\otimes1)\Psi+{\rm h.c.})$ (433×433), physical leak $\|W\psi_k\|^2-\sum_{k'}|\langle\psi_{k'}|W\psi_k\rangle|^2$ measured $10^{-15}$. Closed sector at $g^2$: $\big[\begin{smallmatrix}0&-2/g^2\\-2/g^2&3g^2/2\end{smallmatrix}\big]$ in $\{|0000\rangle,|16\rangle\}$ (Paulo Eq. 37: $\square|0000\rangle=|16\rangle$ with norm 1).

Don't-care structure for the 12-qubit encoding: for an outer computational pattern $o$, $S_o$ = span of the inner components of the physical states with that outer pattern; 3153 of the $3^8=6561$ patterns have support; all 625 inner codes occur; $\mathrm{rank}\,\mathrm{span}_oS_o$ is measured in M1-O1.

## 5. Spin-network basis and the 6j plaquette (route B)

Point-split graph: each corner $c$ → sites $(\text{in},c)$ with legs (inner $a$, inner $b$, virtual $J_c$) and $(\text{out},c)$ with legs (outer 1, outer 2, $J_c$) — the **(inner \| outer) pairing**. Labels $(j_1..j_{12};J_0..J_3)$ with the triangle rule at all 8 trivalent sites; 433 labels at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$. $H_E$ diagonal. Plaquette (Zache, González-Cuadra, Zoller, arXiv:2304.02527, PRL 131, 171902 (2023), Eq. (2), specialised to a loop through the 4 inner sites):
$$\langle\mathbf j',\mathbf J|\mathcal P|\mathbf j,\mathbf J\rangle=\prod_{c=0}^{3}F^{\,J_c\,j_{p(c)}\,j_{n(c)}}_{\;\frac12\;j'_{n(c)}\;j'_{p(c)}},\qquad j'_\ell=j_\ell\pm\tfrac12,\quad \mathbf J'=\mathbf J,$$
corner pairs $(p,n)$: $c_0:(l,b)$, $c_1:(b,r)$, $c_2:(r,t)$, $c_3:(t,l)$; recoupling coefficient (Edmonds Eq. (6.1.5); Varshalovich–Moskalev–Khersonskii §8.1)
$$F^{\,j_1j_2j_{12}}_{\;j_3\,J\,j_{23}}=\langle(j_1j_2)j_{12},j_3;J|j_1,(j_2j_3)j_{23};J\rangle=(-1)^{j_1+j_2+j_3+J}\sqrt{(2j_{12}+1)(2j_{23}+1)}\begin{Bmatrix}j_1&j_2&j_{12}\\j_3&J&j_{23}\end{Bmatrix},$$
$6j$ by Racah's formula (Edmonds Eq. (6.3.7)), cached. $H_B^{\rm phys,B}=-(\mathcal P+\mathcal P^{\mathsf T})$. Properties (all measured, tests M0-X4/X5/X6): matrix elements depend only on the 47 inner labels $((j_b,j_r,j_t,j_l),(J_0..J_3))$ ⇒ $K_{\rm inner}$ (47×47), 16 bright + 31 dark labels (a corner with $J=1$ is annihilated); $H_B^{\rm phys}$ is block-diagonal in the 8 outer spins with every block the restriction of $K$; $J$ is conserved; route A = route B up to a diagonal $\pm1$ gauge (residual $3\cdot10^{-15}$); spectra agree to $10^{-14}$. Consequence: Paulo's Fig. 2 claim (the plaquette needs no outer-link information, not even as controls) holds in this pairing; the note's "depends on the outer links" holds in other pairings (e.g. Raychowdhury–Stryker's $(+e_1,+e_2)|(-e_1,-e_2)$ split at corners $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$).

Gauge alignment: `gauge_align(A,B)` finds a diagonal unitary $D$ with $A=DBD^\dagger$ by propagating phases along a spanning tree of the non-zero pattern of $B$ and reports $\max|A-DBD^\dagger|$.

## 6. Evaluator (one for acceptance tests and the compression loop)

Input: Qiskit circuit, encoding, target $V$ (433×433 in physical-basis order). Steps: (1) `transpile` to `SIM_BASIS` at optimisation level 0 (the design is what is tested); (2) qubit classes from the encoding's layout; any gate touching an outer qubit must be diagonal on it (numerical check $[O,Z_q]=0$ at $10^{-12}$), otherwise verdict `writes_outer`; $R$ = outer qubits read; (3) for each pattern of $R$ (≤ $2^{|R|}$), specialise the circuit and run the batched sparse COO simulator on the compatible inner codes with ancillas $|0\rangle$ (cap $5\cdot10^6$ amplitudes → `unsimulable`); (4) assemble $A_{k'k}=\langle\psi_{k'}|U|\psi_k\rangle$ on the physical states (`physical` method) or, when $R=\varnothing$, check $\|(M-e^{i\varphi}V_{\rm inner})P_S\|_2$ on the inner span (`inner_span` fast path, exact because every physical inner component lies in $S$); (5) one global phase $\varphi=\arg\mathrm{tr}(V^\dagger A)$; `op_norm_error` $=\|A-e^{i\varphi}V\|_2$; leak = weight outside the physical span (data) or with any ancilla bit set, summed directly; (6) Gauss residual of the decoded outputs. Tolerances: exact-angle circuits error ≤ $10^{-10}$, leak ≤ $10^{-12}$ (float64 over ≤ $10^4$ gates accumulates ~$10^{-13}$); synthesised circuits error ≤ $N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon+10^{-9}$ (triangle inequality over unitaries), leak ≤ $(N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon)^2+10^{-12}$ (gridsynth words are not diagonal). Engines: `sparse` (batched COO, all inputs at once; gates may be executed in any topological order of the circuit DAG — a scheduling heuristic that keeps few branching qubits open at once is allowed and exact) and `aer_mps` (qiskit-aer `matrix_product_state` per input, `matrix_product_state_truncation_threshold=0.0`, Aer's statevector-sized memory estimate overridden with `max_memory_mb`, amplitudes obtained by contracting `save_matrix_product_state` — NOT exact to float64: measured 2.6e-11–5.8e-11 per amplitude vs the statevector on random 8-qubit circuits, 2.0e-10 on an 18-qubit 578-gate almost-classical circuit, leak noise up to 1.6e-11, independent of the truncation threshold (reports/tests/008); therefore `aer_mps` cannot certify the 1e-10 / 1e-12 exact-angle verdicts and is used only where the tolerance is ≥ 1e-9 (e.g. synthesised circuits such as Paulo's QPY); a verdict records which engine produced it; measured on Paulo's 34-qubit compiled circuit: 625 inputs in 139 s, max bond dimension 16, op-norm error $2.9\cdot10^{-6}$ vs $e^{+i\beta(\mathrm{Tr}W+{\rm h.c.})}$ at $\beta=1$). `auto` = sparse, falling back to `aer_mps` when the amplitude cap is exceeded (Qiskit's level-3 schedule of Paulo's circuit opens up to 21 Hadamards simultaneously: $2^{21}$ amplitudes per input). Cross-checks: qiskit-aer statevector on ≤ 24-qubit circuits; dense `Operator` ≤ 10 qubits. Never `save_amplitudes` with `matrix_product_state` in Aer 0.17.2 (returns zeros; verified again 2026-11-04).

## 7. Metrics, synthesis, T-count

* Logical counts: `transpile(qc, basis_gates=["cx","h","s","sdg","t","tdg","x","z","rz"], optimization_level=0, seed_transpiler=20261104)`; CX = number of `cx`; depth of the lowered circuit; $N_{\rm rot}$ = `rz` whose angle is not a multiple of $\pi/4$ (tol $10^{-9}$); $T_{\rm fixed}$ = `t`+`tdg`+`rz` at odd multiples of $\pi/4$.
* Macro lowering costs (CX, $T_{\rm fixed}$, $N_{\rm rot}$), measured with that transpile: `ccx` (6,7,0) [7 T optimal ancilla-free: Gosset–Kliuchnikov–Mosca–Russo arXiv:1308.4134; T-depth-3 decomposition Amy–Maslov–Mosca–Roetteler arXiv:1206.0758], `rccx` (3,4,0) [relative-phase Toffoli, Maslov arXiv:1508.03273], `ccz` (6,7,0), `swap` (3,0,0), `ch` (1,2,0) [$S^\dagger HT^\dagger\cdot\mathrm{CX}\cdot THS$], `crz` (2,0,2), `crx` (2,0,2), `cp` (2,0,3), `cz` (1,0,0). Qiskit's native `mcx` with ≥ 3 controls lowers to $\pi/8$-type `rz` (not Clifford+T-exact) and is excluded; $k$-control Toffolis use $k-2$ clean ancillas: $(k-2)$ `rccx` + `ccx` + $(k-2)$ `rccx` = $6+6(k-2)$ CX, $7+8(k-2)$ T (measurement-based uncomputation, Gidney arXiv:1709.06648, out of scope v1).
* T-count: $T_{\rm total}(\varepsilon)=T_{\rm fixed}+\sum_{r=1}^{N_{\rm rot}}T_r(\varepsilon)$. $T_r$ measured with pygridsynth 1.2.0 (`gridsynth_gates(mpf(repr θ), mpf(repr ε))`, Ross–Selinger arXiv:1403.2975: optimal ancilla-free Clifford+T approximation of $R_z(\theta)=\mathrm{diag}(e^{-i\theta/2},e^{i\theta/2})$ to operator-norm error ε up to a global phase, typical T-count $3\log_2(1/\varepsilon)+O(\log\log(1/\varepsilon))$ against the lower bound $K+3\log_2(1/\varepsilon)$). Word letters act right-to-left; `W` = global phase $e^{i\pi/4}$ (dropped). Word error $\min_\phi\|U_{\rm word}-e^{i\phi}R_z(\theta)\|_2\le\varepsilon$ asserted. Measured: 42–44 ($10^{-4}$), 62–64 ($10^{-6}$), 82–86 ($10^{-8}$), 104–106 ($10^{-10}$). Acceptance band $3\log_2(1/\varepsilon)-4\le T_r\le3\log_2(1/\varepsilon)+3\log_2\log_2(1/\varepsilon)+10$. Estimate reported beside it: $T_{\rm est}=T_{\rm fixed}+3N_{\rm rot}\log_2(1/\varepsilon)$. The form "$T=\#T+3\ln\varepsilon$" is wrong in sign ($\ln\varepsilon<0$), base ($\log_2$) and multiplicity (per rotation).
* Qiskit Clifford+T pass manager (second implementation, Paulo's settings): `generate_preset_clifford_t_pass_manager(basis_gates=["cx","h","s","sdg","t","tdg"], optimization_level=3, rz_synthesis_config={"rz_synthesis_error": ε, "rz_cache_error": ε/10}, seed_transpiler=12345)`; deterministic under the seed; T within ±6 of pygridsynth per rotation (measured 62/64/63 vs 62/64/64 at $10^{-6}$).
* ε grid $\{10^{-4},10^{-6},10^{-8},10^{-10}\}$; the loop uses $10^{-6}$. Cost order: CX, then $T_{\rm total}(10^{-6})$, then depth, then qubits.
