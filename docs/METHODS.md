# METHODS — su2qc-encoding

Every numerical method and convention in use, with equations and sources. Written by the planner at kickoff (2026-11-04, `prompts/002`); amended by `prompts/003` (Gauss tolerance, engine policy) and `prompts/004` (leak as amplitude); later planner runs extend it (M3+). Numbers quoted as "measured" come from `scratch/planner/proto_*.py` run in the `coding` env on 2026-11-04 (see `reports/research/001-…`).

Jargon, once: *link* = edge of the lattice carrying an SU(2) matrix; *plaquette* = the square loop of 4 links; *corner* = a lattice site where 4 links meet; *Gauss's law* = the constraint that the 4 links at a corner combine to an SU(2) singlet (zero net colour charge); *physical state* = a state obeying Gauss's law at every corner; *Trotter step* = one factor of the product formula that approximates $e^{-iHt}$; *T gate* = the non-Clifford gate $\mathrm{diag}(1,e^{i\pi/4})$ whose count is the standard fault-tolerant cost measure; *Clifford+T* = the gate set $\{H,S,T,\mathrm{CNOT}\}$ (and inverses).

## 1. Geometry and conventions

* Plaquette with 8 outer links: inner $b=U_1(0,0)$: $(0,0)\to(1,0)$; $r=U_2(1,0)$: $(1,0)\to(1,1)$; $t=U_1(0,1)$: $(0,1)\to(1,1)$; $l=U_2(0,0)$: $(0,0)\to(0,1)$. Outer links 4–11 (`o0..o7`) in corner order: $o_0=U_1(-1,0)$, $o_1=U_2(0,-1)$ (into $(0,0)$); $o_2=U_1(1,0)$ (out of $(1,0)$), $o_3=U_2(1,-1)$ (into $(1,0)$); $o_4=U_1(1,1)$, $o_5=U_2(1,1)$ (out of $(1,1)$); $o_6=U_1(-1,1)$ (into $(0,1)$), $o_7=U_2(0,1)$ (out of $(0,1)$). Corners $c_0..c_3=(0,0),(1,0),(1,1),(0,1)$. Loop $U_P=U_bU_rU_t^\dagger U_l^\dagger$, orientation list `[(b,+1),(r,+1),(t,-1),(l,-1)]`. Source: `refs/su2_plaquette_truncations_encodings.md` §1.
* Spins $j\in\{0,\tfrac12,\dots,j_{\max}\}$ as floats in numerical code, `Fraction` in counting code.
* **Qubit conventions.** Little-endian: qubit $k$ is bit $k$ of the basis integer. `p(λ)=diag(1,e^{iλ})`, `rz(θ)=diag(e^{−iθ/2},e^{iθ/2})` (Qiskit 2.5.2). Ancillas start and end in $|0\rangle$. Register order in every circuit: data `d`, then outer controls `o` (if present), then ancillas.

## 2. Hamiltonian, Trotter step, angle map

Kogut–Susskind on this diagram with Paulo's normalisation (notes Eq. 16, $a=1$, $g_s=g_t=g$):
$$H=\frac{g^2}{2}H_E+\frac{1}{g^2}H_B,\qquad H_E=\sum_{\ell=1}^{12}j_\ell(j_\ell+1),\qquad H_B=-\big(\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P+\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P^\dagger\big).$$
(Standard KS texts use $-\tfrac{1}{2g^2}(\mathrm{Tr}U_P+{\rm h.c.})$; only the angle map changes.)
$$U_E(\alpha)=e^{-i\alpha H_E^{\rm inner}},\quad U_B(\beta)=e^{-i\beta H_B}=e^{+i\beta(\mathrm{Tr}U_P+\mathrm{Tr}U_P^\dagger)},\qquad \alpha=\tfrac{g^2dt}{2},\ \beta=\tfrac{dt}{g^2}.$$
Paulo's notebook implements $e^{+i\alpha_P\,\mathrm{Re\,Tr}\,\square}$ with $\alpha_P=2dt/(g_sg_ta)$ ⇒ $\alpha_P=2\beta$ (his compiled file: $dt=g=1$ ⇒ $\beta=1$). First-order step $U_1=U_B(\beta)U_E(\alpha)$ (electric layer first in the circuit); second order $U_2=U_E(\alpha/2)U_B(\beta)U_E(\alpha/2)$, error $O(dt^3)$ per step (Suzuki–Trotter; Hatano–Suzuki, arXiv:math-ph/0506007). Outer electric energy commutes with everything: $e^{-iHt}=e^{-iH_E^{\rm outer}t}\,e^{-i(H_E^{\rm inner}+H_B)t}$ exactly; main gate counts exclude the 8 outer phase gates, reported as a separate row. Nominal $(\alpha,\beta)=(0.37,0.61)$; alternates $(0.91,0.23)$, $(1.70,1.00)$ (generic, not multiples of $\pi/4$).

Electric step at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$: a $j=\tfrac12$ link has $j(j+1)=\tfrac34$ ⇒ phase $e^{-3i\alpha/4}$ on its $j=1$ codeword ⇒ `p(-3α/4)` on the link's $j$-qubit. (Paulo Eq. 35 writes $P(3\alpha_E/2)$ with $P(\lambda)=\mathrm{diag}(1,e^{-i\lambda})$ and $\alpha_E=g^2/4$ multiplying $T_L^2+T_R^2=2j(j+1)$ — same operator, opposite gate-sign convention.)

## 3. Link Hilbert space (electric basis) and operators

Source: su2qc-skqd `src/skqd/su2.py` (vendored into `src/su2plaq/su2/algebra.py`); Clebsch–Gordan with Condon–Shortley phase from Racah's closed formula (Edmonds, *Angular Momentum in Quantum Mechanics*, Eq. (3.6.11)).

* States $|j,m_L,m_R\rangle$, wavefunction $\sqrt{2j+1}\,D^j_{m_Lm_R}(U)$; local order at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$: $[(0,0,0),(\tfrac12,\tfrac12,\tfrac12),(\tfrac12,\tfrac12,-\tfrac12),(\tfrac12,-\tfrac12,\tfrac12),(\tfrac12,-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12)]$ (dimension 5). Colour index 0 ↔ $+\tfrac12$, 1 ↔ $-\tfrac12$.
* $U_{ij}$ = multiplication by $D^{1/2}_{ij}(U)$ projected to $j\le j_{\max}$:
  $$\langle j'm_L'm_R'|U_{ij}|j\,m_Lm_R\rangle=\sqrt{\tfrac{2j+1}{2j'+1}}\;C^{j'm_L'}_{\frac12 i;\,j m_L}\;C^{j'm_R'}_{\frac12 j;\,j m_R}.$$
* Generators $L_a=-J_a^{\mathsf T}$ on $m_L$, $R_a=+J_a$ on $m_R$; identities (verified to $4\cdot10^{-16}$, test M0-A1): $[L_a,U_{ij}]=-(T_aU)_{ij}$, $[R_a,U_{ij}]=+(UT_a)_{ij}$, $T_a=\sigma_a/2$; $\sum_aL_a^2=\sum_aR_a^2=j(j+1)$.
* Gauss generator at corner $c$: $G^a_c=\sum_{\text{links starting at }c}L_a+\sum_{\text{links ending at }c}R_a$. Physical: $G^a_c|\psi\rangle=0$ for all $a,c$.
* Multi-link electric index: $\mathrm{key}=\sum_{i=0}^{11}s_i5^i$ with $s_i$ the local index of link $i$ (link 0 least significant); inner part $\mathrm{key}\bmod625$, outer part $\lfloor\mathrm{key}/625\rfloor$. Far end of every outer link fixed to the first $m$ value ($+\tfrac12$ in the CG convention); it is a spectator index, so correctness on the 433 fixed-far-end states implies correctness on the 17 362-dimensional far-end-free space.

### 3.1 Paulo's basis and the adapter $V_P$
Paulo (notes Eqs. 22–28) expands $\Psi[U]=\sum\Psi^j_{mm'}D^{j*}_{mm'}(U)$, so his $|mm'\rangle_P\propto D^{1/2*}_{mm'}=\sum_{ab}\epsilon_{ma}D^{1/2}_{ab}\epsilon_{m'b}$ with $\epsilon=i\sigma^2$, $\epsilon_{+-}=1$. Hence, per link,
$$V_P:\quad|0\rangle_P=|0\rangle_{\rm CG},\qquad |mm'\rangle_P=s(m)\,s(m')\,|\tfrac12,-m,-m'\rangle_{\rm CG},\qquad s(\pm)=\pm1,$$
a symmetric signed permutation ($V_P=V_P^{\mathsf T}=V_P^{-1}$) with $V_PU^{\rm CG}_{ij}V_P^{\mathsf T}=\tilde U_{ij}$, where $\tilde U$ is Paulo's Eq. 28: $\tilde U_{ab}|0\rangle=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}s(a)s(b)|\bar a\bar b\rangle$, $\tilde U_{ab}|mm'\rangle=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\delta_{ma}\delta_{bm'}|0\rangle$ (Paulo order $[0,{+}{+},{+}{-},{-}{+},{-}{-}]$). Measured residual $2\cdot10^{-16}$ (test M0-A3). Eq. 25 of the notes omits the conjugate that Eq. 22 implies (typo; the rules in Eq. 28 are consistent with $V_P$).

### 3.2 The 12-qubit codec (Paulo Eq. 31) and the outer 2-qubit code
Per inner link $l\in\{b,r,t,l\}=0..3$, qubits $(3l,3l+1,3l+2)=(q_{m'},q_m,q_j)$; codes (bits $q_jq_mq_{m'}$): $|0\rangle\to000$, $|{+}{+}\rangle\to111$, $|{+}{-}\rangle\to110$, $|{-}{+}\rangle\to101$, $|{-}{-}\rangle\to100$; $001,010,011$ unused (don't-care). 625 valid inner codes. Outer link $k=0..7$ at qubits $(12+2k,12+2k+1)=(q_m,q_j)$ holding $(m_{\rm near},j)$: $j=0\to00$; $j=\tfrac12$: $q_j=1$, $q_m=1$ for $m_{\rm near}=+$, $0$ for $-$ ($m_{\rm near}=m_R$ for links ending at the corner, $m_L$ for links starting there; the far-end index is fixed). Physical vectors of the encoding = route-A states transformed by $V_P^{\otimes12}$ and mapped through these codes (superpositions over inner codes, entangled with the outer register).

## 4. Physical states (route A: corner singlets)

For each assignment of spins to the 12 links and each corner $c$ with ends $(a,b\,|\,1,2)$ (two inner, two outer), the corner singlets are the kernel of $\sum_a\big(\sum_{\text{ends}}G^a\big)^2$ on the $\prod(2j+1)$-dimensional product of the end spaces (an `eigh` on ≤ 16×16), labelled by the eigenvalue $J(J+1)$ of the inner-pair Casimir $(G^a_a+G^a_b)^2$, $J\in\{0,\tfrac12,1\}$; phase convention: largest-modulus component positive (skqd `vertex.py`). A physical state is the tensor product of the 4 corner tensors, stored as a sparse COO vector over the electric multi-index (≤ 1296 components). Counting rule (note §5, reproduced independently): number of singlets at a corner = number of $J$ with $(j_a,j_b,J)$ and $(j_1,j_2,J)$ admissible. Results at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$: **433** states; 2 closed (all outer $j=0$); 256 distinct link-spin strings, 95 of them with two or more states; far-end-weighted dimension $\sum_k\prod_{\rm outer}(2j+1)=17\,362$; at $j_{\max}=1$: 65 250 (route B count). Gauss residual $\max_{c,a}\|G^a_c\psi\|$ measured $5\cdot10^{-16}$.

Inner plaquette operator $W=\mathrm{Tr}(U_bU_rU_t^\dagger U_l^\dagger)=\sum_{abcd}U^{(b)}_{ab}U^{(r)}_{bc}(U^{(t)}_{dc})^\dagger(U^{(l)}_{ad})^\dagger$ (625×625; operator adjoint for the daggered links); $H_B^{\rm phys}=-(\Psi^\dagger(W\otimes1)\Psi+{\rm h.c.})$ (433×433), physical leak $\|W\psi_k\|^2-\sum_{k'}|\langle\psi_{k'}|W\psi_k\rangle|^2$ measured $10^{-15}$. Closed sector at $g^2$: $\big[\begin{smallmatrix}0&-2/g^2\\-2/g^2&3g^2/2\end{smallmatrix}\big]$ in $\{|0000\rangle,|16\rangle\}$ (Paulo Eq. 37: $\square|0000\rangle=|16\rangle$ with norm 1).

Don't-care structure for the 12-qubit encoding: for an outer computational pattern $o$, $S_o$ = span of the inner components of the physical states with that outer pattern; 3153 of the $3^8=6561$ patterns have support; all 625 inner codes occur; $\mathrm{rank}\,\mathrm{span}_oS_o$ is measured in M1-O1.

## 5. Spin-network basis and the 6j plaquette (route B)

Point-split graph: each corner $c$ → sites $(\text{in},c)$ with legs (inner $a$, inner $b$, virtual $J_c$) and $(\text{out},c)$ with legs (outer 1, outer 2, $J_c$) — the **(inner \| outer) pairing**. Labels $(j_1..j_{12};J_0..J_3)$ with the triangle rule at all 8 trivalent sites; 433 labels at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$. $H_E$ diagonal. Plaquette (Zache, González-Cuadra, Zoller, arXiv:2304.02527, PRL 131, 171902 (2023), Eq. (2), specialised to a loop through the 4 inner sites):
$$\langle\mathbf j',\mathbf J|\mathcal P|\mathbf j,\mathbf J\rangle=\prod_{c=0}^{3}F^{\,J_c\,j_{p(c)}\,j_{n(c)}}_{\;\frac12\;j'_{n(c)}\;j'_{p(c)}},\qquad j'_\ell=j_\ell\pm\tfrac12,\quad \mathbf J'=\mathbf J,$$
corner pairs $(p,n)$: $c_0:(l,b)$, $c_1:(b,r)$, $c_2:(r,t)$, $c_3:(t,l)$; recoupling coefficient (Edmonds Eq. (6.1.5); Varshalovich–Moskalev–Khersonskii §8.1)
$$F^{\,j_1j_2j_{12}}_{\;j_3\,J\,j_{23}}=\langle(j_1j_2)j_{12},j_3;J|j_1,(j_2j_3)j_{23};J\rangle=(-1)^{j_1+j_2+j_3+J}\sqrt{(2j_{12}+1)(2j_{23}+1)}\begin{Bmatrix}j_1&j_2&j_{12}\\j_3&J&j_{23}\end{Bmatrix},$$
$6j$ by Racah's formula (Edmonds Eq. (6.3.7)), cached. $H_B^{\rm phys,B}=-(\mathcal P+\mathcal P^{\mathsf T})$. Properties (all measured, tests M0-X4/X5/X6): matrix elements depend only on the 47 inner labels $((j_b,j_r,j_t,j_l),(J_0..J_3))$ ⇒ $K_{\rm inner}$ (47×47), 16 bright + 31 dark labels (a corner with $J=1$ is annihilated); $H_B^{\rm phys}$ is block-diagonal in the 8 outer spins with every block the restriction of $K$; $J$ is conserved; route A = route B up to a diagonal $\pm1$ gauge (residual $3\cdot10^{-15}$); spectra agree to $10^{-14}$. Consequence: Paulo's Fig. 2 claim (the plaquette needs no outer-link information, not even as controls) holds in this pairing; the note's "depends on the outer links" holds in other pairings (e.g. Raychowdhury–Stryker's $(+e_1,+e_2)|(-e_1,-e_2)$ split at corners $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$).

Gauge alignment: `gauge_align(A,B)` finds a diagonal unitary $D$ with $A=DBD^\dagger$ by propagating phases along a spanning tree of the non-zero pattern of $B$ and reports $\max|A-DBD^\dagger|$.

## 6. Evaluator (one for acceptance tests and the compression loop)

Input: Qiskit circuit, encoding, target $V$ (433×433 in physical-basis order). Steps: (1) `transpile` to `SIM_BASIS` at optimisation level 0 (the design is what is tested); (2) qubit classes from the encoding's layout; any gate touching an outer qubit must be diagonal on it (numerical check $[O,Z_q]=0$ at $10^{-12}$), otherwise verdict `writes_outer`; $R$ = outer qubits read; (3) for each pattern of $R$ (≤ $2^{|R|}$), specialise the circuit and run the batched sparse COO simulator on the compatible inner codes with ancillas $|0\rangle$ (cap $5\cdot10^6$ amplitudes → `unsimulable`); (4) assemble $A_{k'k}=\langle\psi_{k'}|U|\psi_k\rangle$ on the physical states (`physical` method) or, when $R=\varnothing$, check $\|(M-e^{i\varphi}V_{\rm inner})P_S\|_2$ on the inner span (`inner_span` fast path, exact because every physical inner component lies in $S$); (5) one global phase $\varphi=\arg\mathrm{tr}(V^\dagger A)$; `block_error` $=\|A-e^{i\varphi}V\|_2$ (the physical-block error; with the `inner_span` method the larger of the inner-span and the physical error); **leak is judged as an amplitude (prompts/004, Digonto 2026-11-04; audit 001 N1)**: write the output on the physical inputs as $U\Psi=\Psi A+S+X$ with $X$ the part outside the code space (any ancilla bit set or an invalid data code) and $S$ the part inside the code space but outside the physical span; the three pieces are orthogonal, so $\|(U-e^{i\varphi}\Psi V\Psi^\dagger)\Psi\|_2\le\|A-e^{i\varphi}V\|_2+\|X\|_2+\|S\|_2$ (reports/research/003 §2). The verdict's error is the combined bound $$\texttt{op\_norm\_error}=\texttt{block\_error}+\texttt{dropped\_total}+\texttt{leak\_amplitude},\qquad\texttt{leak\_amplitude}=\|X\|_2=\sqrt{\lambda_{\max}(X^\dagger X)},$$ compared with the single tolerance `tol_err`; there is no separate leak tolerance (a weight tolerance of $10^{-12}$ let ancilla errors of amplitude $10^{-6}$ pass because the block error sees a leaked amplitude $\delta$ only as $\delta^2/2$). **Certified pruning bound and its cap (prompts/005, Digonto 2026-11-05; reports/tests/020 §C).** The sparse engine drops, after every branching gate, amplitudes with $|a|\le$ `PRUNE_REL`$\cdot$(largest $|a|$ of the same input column), `PRUNE_REL` $=10^{-14}$, and certifies per input column $c$ the dropped L2 weight $d_c$ (sum over coalesces of $\sqrt{\sum_{\rm dropped}|a|^2}$; unit-tested to dominate the true column error). Per outer-bit group $r$ (one simulation per group, shared by all outer patterns in it) the Frobenius bound $t_r=\sqrt{\sum_c d_c^2}\ge\|E_r\|_2$ bounds the pruning error $E_r$ of the $2^n\times625$ block; the full-register pruning error $E=\sum_o|o\rangle\langle o|\otimes E_{r(o)}$ is block-diagonal in the outer pattern, so $\|E\|_2=\max_o\|E_{r(o)}\|_2\le\max_rt_r$ and $$\texttt{dropped\_total}:=\max_{r}t_r$$ (a maximum over outer groups, **not** a sum; the sum used before prompts/005 was valid but up to $n_{\rm blocks}\times$ pessimistic) — a single $+$`dropped_total` in `op_norm_error` is rigorous because every judged quantity is built from the same pruned columns $U'\Phi=(U+E)\Phi$ and $\|(U-T)\Phi\|\le\|(U'-T)\Phi\|+\|E\|$ (prompts/005 §2). The separate verdict `pruning_bound` is only a safety net that keeps the simulator's share of the budget negligible: $$\texttt{pruning\_bound}\iff\texttt{dropped\_total}>\texttt{PRUNE\_FRACTION}\cdot\texttt{tol\_err},\qquad\texttt{PRUNE\_FRACTION}=10^{-2},$$ i.e. cap $10^{-12}$ for exact-angle circuits (`tol_err` $10^{-10}$, as before, but now on the Frobenius bound rather than the per-column maximum $\max_cd_c$, which is kept only as the diagnostic `extra["max_dropped_l2"]`) and $10^{-2}(N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon+10^{-9})\ge1.1\cdot10^{-11}$ for synthesised circuits ($10^{-7}$ at the loop value $N_{\rm rot}=10,\varepsilon=10^{-6}$). The previous fixed cap `TOL_PRUNE` $=10^{-12}$ on the per-column maximum, independent of `tol_err`, rejected the correct synthesised step order 2 at $\varepsilon=10^{-4}$ ($\max_cd_c=2.6\cdot10^{-12}$, `dropped_total` $3.9\cdot10^{-11}$, `op_norm_error` $5.8\cdot10^{-4}\le1.4\cdot10^{-3}$) and left a factor 2.1 at $\varepsilon=10^{-6}$. Measured headroom (cap/`dropped_total`, `results/twelve/*.json`, commit 0495049): exact-angle UB/step1/step2 $8.3/6.8/6.6$ (`dropped_total` $1.2$–$1.5\cdot10^{-13}$, UE has no branching gate: 0); synthesised $\ge78$ ($\varepsilon=10^{-10}$) and $\ge8.7\cdot10^{3}$ at $\varepsilon=10^{-6}$. A `pruning_bound` verdict on a circuit whose `op_norm_error` $\le$ `tol_err` is a stop-and-ask condition, never a reason to raise `PRUNE_FRACTION` or `PRUNE_REL`. The Gauss residual is computed from the pruned outputs; its pruning error $\le\|G^a_c\|_{\rm op}\max_rt_r=2\,$`dropped_total` $\le2\cdot10^{-2}$`tol_gauss` under the cap and is not added to the Gauss comparison. `sparse` engine: $X^\dagger X=\sum_o(X_oP_o)^\dagger(X_oP_o)$ accumulated over the outer blocks (outputs of different outer patterns are orthogonal), exact to float64. `aer_mps` engine: the out-of-code amplitudes are not enumerable, so per inner basis input $i$ the weights $w^{\rm anc}_i=\sum_{q\in{\rm anc}}\langle\Pi_{q=1}\prod_{q'<q}\Pi_{q'=0}\rangle$ (disjoint "first set ancilla" terms, transfer-matrix contraction) and $w^{\rm inv}_i=\sum_{c\notin{\rm codes}}|\langle c,{\rm anc}=0|U|i\rangle|^2$ (all $2^{n_{\rm data}}-|{\rm codes}|$ invalid data patterns contracted; $n_{\rm data}\le16$) are computed **directly — never as $1-\sum|M|^2$ or $1-\langle\Pi_{\rm anc=0}\rangle$, whose cancellation noise is $10^{-15}$–$10^{-11}$ in weight** — and the certified per-physical-state bound is $\|X\Psi\|_2^2\le\max_oW_o$, $W_o=\sum_{i\in{\rm supp}P_o}w_i$ (Cauchy–Schwarz over the $\le16$ inner components of a physical state in one outer pattern; tight for coherent leak, up to $4\times$ pessimistic otherwise), so `leak_amplitude` $=\sqrt{\max_oW_o}$; measured noise of the direct weights on exact-angle 34-qubit circuits $\le9.4\cdot10^{-21}$ per input, i.e. an amplitude floor $\le3.9\cdot10^{-10}$ (research/003 §4). Weight diagnostics kept but never judged: `max_leak` (sparse: $\max_k\|X\psi_k\|^2$; aer_mps: $\max_oW_o$) and `dirty_ancilla` (its ancilla part), with `max_leak ≤ leak_amplitude²`. Span leak $S$ is not in the error sum: it is bounded by the Gauss check, $\|s_k\|\le4\,$`gauss_residual` (the smallest non-zero eigenvalue of $\sum_{c,a}(G^a_c)^2$ is $\tfrac34$, 12 terms; research/003 §3), and reported as the weight `max_span_leak`; (6) Gauss residual of the decoded outputs. Tolerances (`tol_err`, `tol_gauss`; prompts/003 §2 as amended by prompts/004): exact-angle circuits `op_norm_error` (block error + leak amplitude) ≤ $10^{-10}$, Gauss ≤ $10^{-10}$ (float64 over ≤ $10^4$ gates accumulates ~$10^{-13}$ in amplitude; `tol_gauss=None` selects this default); synthesised circuits (each of $N_{\rm rot}$ rotations replaced by a Clifford+T word within $\varepsilon$) `op_norm_error` ≤ $N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon+10^{-9}$ (triangle inequality over unitaries: $\|U-U_{\rm ex}\|_2\le N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon$ on the full register bounds block error and leak amplitude *jointly* in root-sum-square; the adopted sum of the two can reach $\sqrt2N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon$ for a correct circuit — a circuit with both parts ≤ budget but their sum in $(\text{tol},\sqrt2\,\text{tol}]$ is a stop-and-ask condition, research/003 §5; measured on Paulo's circuit: block $2.9\cdot10^{-6}$ + leak bound $7.9\cdot10^{-6}$ = $1.08\cdot10^{-5}$ vs $1.4\cdot10^{-5}$), Gauss ≤ $N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon+10^{-9}$ (Digonto 2026-11-04): since $G^a_cV\psi_k=0$, $\|G^a_c U\psi_k\|=\|G^a_c(U-U_{\rm ex})\psi_k\|\le\|G^a_c\|_{\rm op}N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon$ with $\|G^a_c\|_{\rm op}=2$ (four spin-½ ends); the budget equals the error budget because the measured ratio residual$/\|U-U_{\rm ex}\|$ is 1.0 for a gauge-breaking error on an $m$-type qubit and 0.5 on a $j$-type qubit (reports/research/002), the ratio exceeds 1 only if several rotation errors add coherently at all four ends of a corner, and Paulo's circuit measures $1.16\cdot10^{-10}$ against a budget of $1.4\cdot10^{-5}$; a residual in $(\text{tol},2\,\text{tol}]$ on a circuit that passes error and leak is a stop-and-ask condition, not a loosening. The rule lives in `synth_tolerances(n_rot, eps)`. The Gauss residual is the first-order detector of gauge-breaking errors (an amplitude $\delta$ outside the physical span appears as $\delta$ in the residual but only as $\delta^2$ in the physical-block op-norm error, reports/tests/009 F3), so `gauss=True` is mandatory for acceptance and loop verdicts. Certification: `engine_accuracy` = 0 for `sparse`, $2\cdot10^{-10}$ for `aer_mps` (per amplitude; the combined quantity's aer_mps noise is ≤ $2\cdot10^{-10}+3.9\cdot10^{-10}<10^{-9}$, so $10^{-9}$ remains the smallest certifiable aer_mps tolerance); `certified = engine_accuracy ≤ min(tol_err, tol_gauss)` (`tol_err` alone when `gauss=False`); `Verdict.tolerances` records the two values applied; only `ok and certified` counts as verified (acceptance tests assert both; the loop marks `ok and not certified` as unverified, never valid). Engines: `sparse` (batched COO, all inputs at once; gates may be executed in any topological order of the circuit DAG — a scheduling heuristic that keeps few branching qubits open at once is allowed and exact) and `aer_mps` (qiskit-aer `matrix_product_state` per input, `matrix_product_state_truncation_threshold=0.0`, Aer's statevector-sized memory estimate overridden with `max_memory_mb`, amplitudes obtained by contracting `save_matrix_product_state` — NOT exact to float64: measured 2.6e-11–5.8e-11 per amplitude vs the statevector on random 8-qubit circuits, 2.0e-10 on an 18-qubit 578-gate almost-classical circuit, leak noise up to 1.6e-11, independent of the truncation threshold (reports/tests/008); therefore `aer_mps` cannot certify the 1e-10 / 1e-12 exact-angle verdicts and is used only where the tolerance is ≥ 1e-9 (e.g. synthesised circuits such as Paulo's QPY); a verdict records which engine produced it; measured on Paulo's 34-qubit compiled circuit: 625 inputs in 139 s, max bond dimension 16, op-norm error $2.9\cdot10^{-6}$ vs $e^{+i\beta(\mathrm{Tr}W+{\rm h.c.})}$ at $\beta=1$). `auto` = sparse, falling back to `aer_mps` when the amplitude cap is exceeded (Qiskit's level-3 schedule of Paulo's circuit opens up to 21 Hadamards simultaneously: $2^{21}$ amplitudes per input). Cross-checks: qiskit-aer statevector on ≤ 24-qubit circuits; dense `Operator` ≤ 10 qubits. Never `save_amplitudes` with `matrix_product_state` in Aer 0.17.2 (returns zeros; verified again 2026-11-04). **Span leak as an exact operator norm (prompts/008 §5; audit 100 N3, audit 002 R1; reports/research/005 §2; in force from step B2 of prompts/008).** The piece $S=(P_{\mathcal C}-\Psi\Psi^\dagger)U\Psi$ of the decomposition $U\Psi=\Psi A+S+X$ above was controlled only per state through the Gauss check ($\|s_k\|\le4\,g$); it is now computed. Because the three ranges are orthogonal, $\|U\Psi-e^{i\varphi}\Psi V\|_2=\sqrt{\lambda_{\max}(D^\dagger D+S^\dagger S+X^\dagger X)}\le\|D\|_2+\|S\|_2+\|X\|_2$ with $D=A-e^{i\varphi}V$. ## 11. Formal verification (Lean 4) — methods, structure, and the loop (prompts/006; research/004)

Jargon, once: *Lean 4* = interactive theorem prover whose small kernel re-checks every proof; *Mathlib* = its mathematics library; *axiom* = a statement accepted without proof, listed by `#print axioms`; the three *standard axioms* are `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`; *kernel-checked* = proof verified by the kernel with the standard axioms only; *`decide`* = kernel evaluation of a decidable statement; *native-certified* = decided by compiled Lean code (`native_decide`, which adds one per-theorem axiom `<name>._native.native_decide.ax_…`, or the compiled checker executable — both trust the Lean compiler and the checker source); *certificate* = exact data produced by Python and verified by the Lean checker. Toolchain `leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0`, Mathlib tag `v4.35.0` (same as the sibling project su2qc-skqd/2x4, commit `30f84af1`), project `lean/` with libraries `Su2Plaq` (Mathlib, theorems) and `Su2PlaqCert` (Mathlib-free computable checker), executable `su2plaq_cert`. Rule: `lake build` passes, no `sorry`, standard axioms only in `Su2Plaq`; `native_decide` only in `lean/certs/`. The numerical evaluator (§6) remains the ground truth of every verdict; Lean adds proofs and exact certificates and never decides validity.

**11.1 Generic theorems (kernel-checked).** Over finite-dimensional complex inner product spaces / `E →L[ℂ] F` with the operator norm (Mathlib `ContinuousLinearMap.opNorm_le_bound`, `le_opNorm`, `norm_add_sq_eq_norm_sq_add_norm_sq_of_inner_eq_zero`, `inner_sum`, `Real.sum_mul_le_sqrt_mul_sqrt`): (a) Pythagoras for pairwise orthogonal vectors and $\|A+B+C\|\le\sqrt{\|A\|^2+\|B\|^2+\|C\|^2}\le\|A\|+\|B\|+\|C\|$ for maps with mutually orthogonal ranges (`norm_sum_sq_of_pairwise_orth`, `opNorm_add_three_le_of_orth`, `rss_le_sum`) — the combined bound of §6; (b) `pruning_triangle` $\|(U-T)\Phi\|\le\|(U'-T)\Phi\|+\|E\|$ and `opNorm_le_of_blockdiag` $\|E\|\le\max_o\|P_oEP_o\|$ for $E=\sum_oP_oEP_o$ with orthogonal projections — the max-over-outer-groups rule of §6; (c) `verdict_sound`: $\|U\Phi-c\Phi V\|\le b+d+l+s$ from $\|A-cV\|\le b$, $\|E\|\le d$, $\|X\|\le l$, $\|S\|\le s$, $|c|=1$, $\Phi$ an isometry — the evaluator's inequality chain; (d) `gauss_max_ge`, `span_leak_le_four_gauss`: $\sum_{i<12}x_i^2\ge\tfrac34t^2\Rightarrow\max_ix_i\ge t/4$, hence $\|s\|\le4\cdot$`gauss_residual` **under the hypothesis** $\sum_{c,a}\|G^a_cs\|^2\ge\tfrac34\|s\|^2$ (the spectral gap $\tfrac34$ of $\sum(G^a_c)^2$ is SU(2) representation theory, not formalised; it is a 16-dimensional exact computation per corner and is verified in the structure certificate); (e) `cs_block_bound` $\|\sum_ia_ix_i\|\le\|a\|_2\sqrt{\sum\|x_i\|^2}$ (aer_mps bound of §6); (f) synthesis: `prod_sub_prod_le` $\|U_1\cdots U_N-V_1\cdots V_N\|\le\sum_i\|U_i-V_i\|$ for factors of norm $\le1$ in any normed ring, `synth_bound` with words $W_r=c_rR_r+\Delta_r$ ⇒ $\|\prod W-(\prod c)\prod R\|\le\sum\|\Delta_r\|\le N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon$ (§7, prompts/003); (g) gates: monomial matrices $M_{\pi,\phi}e_q=\phi(q)e_{\pi q}$ (`mono_mulVec_e`, unitary for $|\phi|=1$: `mono_conjTranspose_mul`), ancilla-controlled families $\sum_h|h\rangle\langle h|\otimes W_h$ (`ctrl_mulVec_e`) and the **sandwich lemma**: for unitary $C$ with $Ce_{(x,z)}=\phi(x)e_{(x,f(x))}$ and $W_h$ changing neither $f$ nor $\phi$, $C^\dagger(\sum_h|h\rangle\langle h|\otimes W_h)C\,e_{(x,z)}=\sum_y(W_{f(x)})_{yx}e_{(y,z)}$ — the clean-ancilla compute/uncompute principle behind `rccx·crx·rccx = ccrx` (`rccx_crx_rccx_eq_ccrx`, RCCX = Qiskit's relative-phase Toffoli: controls set ⇒ target flips with phase $i$ (0→1) / $-i$ (1→0), $|a{=}1,b{=}0,h{=}1\rangle\mapsto-|\cdot\rangle$, RCCX$^\dagger$ = RCCX) and `Builder.mcx` chains; $HZH=X$, $HR_zH=R_x$; CNOT networks as xor-linear permutations, phases through CNOTs, CNOT/diagonal commutation, CCZ from 3 CP + 2 CX (`Bits.lean`, `Peephole.lean` vendored from the sibling, same Mathlib tag); (h) `kron_disjoint_comm`, `outer_electric_commutes`: $(W\otimes1)(1\otimes D)=(1\otimes D)(W\otimes1)$ — $[H_E^{\rm outer},H]=0$ (§2). Measured elaboration of the prototypes (`scratch/planner/lean/P1,P2,P3b,P4`): 7–10 s per file against the sibling's Mathlib build.

**11.2 Exact per-circuit certificates (native-certified).** Field $K=\mathbb Q(i,\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$ stored as 8 rationals ($a+bi+c\sqrt2+di\sqrt2+e\sqrt3+fi\sqrt3+g\sqrt6+hi\sqrt6$): Clifford+T entries lie in $\mathbb Z[\tfrac1{\sqrt2},i]$ (Giles–Selinger, arXiv:1212.0506, Thm. 1), the corner singlets of spin-½ ends and $W$ have coefficients in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$ (measured). Angles: with base angles $\theta_\alpha=\alpha/d_\alpha$, $\theta_\beta=\beta/d_\beta$ every angle-linear gate is a Laurent polynomial in $z_\alpha=e^{i\theta_\alpha}$, $z_\beta=e^{i\theta_\beta}$ over $K$: $e^{im\theta}=z^m$, $\cos m\theta=\tfrac12(z^m+z^{-m})$, $\sin m\theta=\tfrac1{2i}(z^m-z^{-m})$; the target $U_B=\sum_\lambda P_\lambda z_\beta^{-\lambda d_\beta}$ with exact spectral projectors $P_\lambda=\prod_{\mu\ne\lambda}(H_B-\mu)/(\lambda-\mu)$ per outer block ($\lambda\in\{0,\pm\tfrac12,\pm1,\pm2\}$), $U_E$ diagonal in $z_\alpha^{-3nd_\alpha/4}$. The checker (`lake exe su2plaq_cert check`) replays the gate list produced by `sparse_sim._translate` (SIM_BASIS, program order, Qiskit matrices; `unitary` excluded) on the 433 exact physical states and accepts iff $U\psi_k=c\sum_{k'}V_{k'k}\psi_{k'}$ **exactly** for every $k$ with the supplied unit $c=k\,z_\alpha^az_\beta^b$ ($k\in K$, $|k|=1$, fitted by the exporter from the verdict phases at the three angle pairs). An identity of Laurent polynomials holds for all real angles, so one certificate proves block exactness, zero leak, clean ancillas, zero span leak and Gauss for **every** $(\alpha,\beta)$ — stronger than the three numerical angle pairs. Not certifiable (status `n_a`): rotation angles that are not rational multiples of $\alpha$ or $\beta$ with denominator $\le64$ (`Fraction.limit_denominator(64)`, residual $>10^{-12}$), `unitary` gates, a relative phase outside $K$. Kernel `decide` is impossible here (`Rat` arithmetic does not reduce in the kernel; $\ge10^9$ steps), so certificates carry the compiler-trust axiom; the checker's gate semantics are tied to the validated float simulator by a locked test (exact output evaluated at the nominal angles vs `run_batch`, $\le10^{-12}$). Expected cost: seconds to minutes per circuit (general step circuit: $10^7$–$10^8$ exact operations compiled). Exporter cross-checks: exact basis/link operators/target vs the float ones $\le10^{-12}$ per entry.

**11.3 Structure of the problem.** (a) Counts, kernel-checked by `decide` from the corner fusion rule $n_c=|T(j_a,j_b)\cap T(j_c,j_d)|$ ($T(0,0)=\{0\}$, $T(0,\tfrac12)=\{\tfrac12\}$, $T(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)=\{0,1\}$) and the outer-$m$ rule (both outer links at $j=\tfrac12$ with inner pair $(0,0)$ ⇒ $m_c=-m_d$): 433 physical states, 256 link-spin patterns, 95 with ≥ 2 states, 2 closed, far-end weighted 17 362, 3153 supported outer patterns, ≤ 17 states per outer pattern, 3471 don't-care inner codes (measured: 433 and 17 362 ≈ 60–70 s each in the kernel, no axioms; the naive $m$-blind rule gives 3281 ≠ 3153 — the exact-check loop that found the missing constraint). (b) Exact structure certificate (native): the checker recomputes $H_B^{\rm phys}=-(\Psi^\dagger(W\otimes1)\Psi+{\rm h.c.})$ from the exact states and link operators and verifies: Gauss law and orthonormality of the 433 states, $J$ labels; block-diagonality in the outer spins; $J_c$ conservation; every block $=DK_{\rm sub}D$ with the exact $47\times47$ $K_{\rm inner}$ and a $\pm1$ gauge $D$; $K_{\rm inner}$ = 8 bright $2\times2$ pairs (couplings $2$, $1$ (×6), $\tfrac12$) + 31 dark rows; spectrum multiplicities $16,96,16,177,16,96,16$ for $\lambda=-2,-1,-\tfrac12,0,\tfrac12,1,2$ (exact ranks of $H_B-\lambda$ per block); the components of $-(W+W^\dagger)$ on the 625 Paulo inner codes (measured 2026-11-05: 128 edges all of modulus $\tfrac12$; 464 dark codes, 16 pairs, 8 four-cliques, 16 five-cliques, one 17-component; spectrum on the inner span $\pm2$ (1), $\pm1$ (24), $\pm\tfrac12$ (16), 0 (543)); the per-corner kernel dimensions (16 spin cases); and every symmetry found numerically (the 16 candidates = dihedral $D_4$ of the square with orientation reversal $m_L\leftrightarrow m_R$ × charge conjugation $m\to-m$, signs fixed by `gauge_align`) as a signed label permutation commuting with $H_B$ and $H_E$. Lean does not search for structure; the discovery pattern is Python (or a proposer) conjectures, the exact checker verifies in seconds and prints the first violated entry when it fails. **12.2 Evaluator hashes.** Shared list $F$ = `provenance.EVALUATOR_FILES` (now including the registry), own list $F_e$ per encoding. `code_hash()` = sha256 over $F$ (unchanged); `code_hash_for(e)` = sha256 over $F\,\Vert\,F_e$ (file name, NUL, bytes, NUL per file, in list order). An archive entry stores `evaluator_sha256` = `stamp_hash(e)` (the shared hash for twelve, spinnet, fuzzy; the combined hash for rishon — the meanings the locked gate tests of m2, m4, m10, m6 fixed) and `encoding_sha256` = `code_hash_for(e)` for every encoding; the result cache is keyed by `code_hash_for(e)`. An edit in $F$ makes every archive stale, an edit in $F_e$ only archive $e$.

**12.3 Replay.** `python -m su2plaq.compress.replay --encoding E --kind K --tag T` (the procedure of prompts/204 §6.2 for any encoding): the recorded rounds are re-evaluated in recorded order under the current code into a fresh archive; the old files are moved to `results/compress/<E>/pre-<T>/` and `compress/archive/<E>-pre-<T>/`; attempts and discoveries are restored byte for byte; Lean records are carried over for unchanged programs; `replay-<T>.json` lists per entry the old and new validity, reason and key, `lost`, `newly_valid`, old and new best. Invariants required by the gate (M2C-G1): nothing that was valid is lost, every such entry keeps its key exactly, every entry and round file carries the current hashes. A replay is not a round. A tag is never reused; entries are never edited in place.

**12.4 Headline rule (all encodings).** The archive key $(\mathrm{CX},T_{10^{-6}},\mathrm{depth_{CT}},\mathrm{qubits})$ at the nominal angles is unchanged and steers the search. What a report quotes is the valid entry with the smallest robust key $$\Big(\mathrm{CX},\ \sum_{i=1}^{20}T_{10^{-6}}(\alpha_i,\beta_i),\ \rho,\ \sum_{i=1}^{20}\mathrm{depth_{CT}}(\alpha_i,\beta_i),\ \mathrm{qubits}\Big),$$ lexicographic, over the locked set `default_rng(20261107)`: $\alpha_i\sim U(0.05,2.0)$ (20 draws), then $\beta_i\sim U(0.05,1.2)$ (20 draws) — the set of prompts/104 §11 E3 and prompts/203 §13 E3; $\rho=0$ for an angle-linear program (per-encoding test, else: every rotation angle equals $c_\alpha\alpha+c_\beta\beta$ to $10^{-9}$ at the 20 pairs and the nominal pair), else 1; no robust key if the skeleton changes inside the set. Reason: an exact rewrite $R_z(\theta)=R_z(\theta\pm\pi/4)\,T^{\mp}$ costs $+1$ T at every angle but redraws the angle-dependent length of the pygridsynth word (Ross–Selinger, arXiv:1403.2975: $3\log_2(1/\varepsilon)+O(\log\log(1/\varepsilon))$ per rotation, with an angle-dependent remainder), so the nominal T of one program scatters by 12–53 T over angle pairs for 10–28 rotations, while the 20-pair mean is $\sqrt{20}$ times sharper (spinnet round 3: $-24$ T at the nominal pair, $+13.2\pm2.3$ T on average, reports/tests/115). The robust key was **not** made the archive key: on the twelve, fuzzy_ub and fuzzy_link archives both rankings coincide (0 order flips at equal CX among 21, 11, 4 entries), the key is locked in four milestones, and CX leads the search. Angle generality of what is quoted (audit 301 A2): the archive best and the headline are re-verified at two further seeded pairs (`default_rng(20261110)`, same ranges; for the fuzzy link also $(\alpha,\kappa_2)=(0.37,0)$ and $(0.37,0.185)$) with the nominal skeleton and `total_error` $\le10^{-10}$ (M2C-G4); for an angle-linear program with a fixed skeleton the error is a fixed trigonometric polynomial in $(\alpha,\beta)$, so a point chosen independently of the program detects a non-zero error with probability 1, and a Lean certificate proves all angles.
