## 1. What was asked
Build in Qiskit (IBM's open-source circuit library) the circuits for one Trotter step — the product formula that approximates $e^{-i(\alpha H_E+\beta H_B)}$ by alternating the electric part $U_E(\alpha)=e^{-i\alpha H_E}$ and the magnetic part $U_B(\beta)=e^{-i\beta H_B}$ — of the Kogut–Susskind Hamiltonian for one SU(2) plaquette (the smallest closed loop of four links) with 8 outer links at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$, in the **spin-network encoding** decided by Digonto (START_HERE §2/§4): one qubit per link ($j=0\to|0\rangle$, $j=\tfrac12\to|1\rangle$) and one qubit per corner coding the corner's intertwiner (the way the two pairs of spins at a corner couple; $J_c=1\to|1\rangle$, meaningful only when all four links at that corner are $\tfrac12$). The circuit may change the 4 inner-link qubits and the 4 corner qubits (8 data qubits) and may read the 8 outer-link qubits as controls; it must be correct on all 433 Gauss-law states (the states obeying the lattice form of Gauss's law, i.e. a colour singlet at every corner). Count CNOT and T gates, compare like for like with Paulo's 15-qubit spin-network circuit and with the sibling project su2qc-trotter's `spinnet_v2` designs, and label every formal (Lean) claim by its trust level.

## 2. What was done
- **Encoding (codec).** `src/su2plaq/encodings/spinnet.py`. A physical state's label $(j_0..j_{11};J_0..J_3)$ becomes the 16-bit integer $\sum_{l<4}[j_l{=}\tfrac12]2^l+\sum_c[J_c{=}1]2^{4+c}+\sum_k[j_{o_k}{=}\tfrac12]2^{8+k}$ (data qubits 0–3 = inner links $b,r,t,l$, 4–7 = corners $c_0..c_3$, 8–15 = outer links). The 433 physical states are 433 distinct computational-basis states ("unit codes"); 47 of the 256 8-bit data patterns are valid inner labels (16 *bright*, which the plaquette moves, and 31 *dark*, which it annihilates). Outputs that land on a valid inner code but an impossible outer pattern (11 599 cases) are decoded to a fixed product state with Gauss residual $\ge\tfrac12$ (measured minimum exactly $0.5$, reports/tests/100 §2a), so the checker's Gauss test catches them at first order.
- **Exact reference.** Same 433 states and same Hamiltonian convention as Report 1: $H=\frac{g^2}{2}\sum_\ell j_\ell(j_\ell+1)-\frac1{g^2}(\mathrm{Tr}U_P+\mathrm{h.c.})$ (Paulo Eq. 16, $\alpha_P=2\beta$). The target is "route B" (the 6j-symbol construction of $H_B$ in the spin-network basis) brought to the sign convention of "route A" (the electric-basis construction) by a diagonal $\pm1$ matrix (64 minus signs, residual $2.7\times10^{-15}$; `UB.json` `extra.physics.n_gauge_minus_433`, `gauge_residual`). $H_B$ is block-diagonal in the outer pattern (cross-pattern entries $0.0$, `extra.physics.target_cross_pattern_max_abs`) and every block equals one $47\times47$ inner operator $K$ to $2.7\times10^{-17}$ (`target_same_pattern_vs_inner_block_max_dev`). $K$ pairs each bright code with its partner `code XOR 0b1111` (all four inner spins flip): 8 pairs with couplings $-2$ (one pair), $-\tfrac12$ (one pair), $+1$ (six pairs); 31 dark rows (`extra.physics.coupling_values_per_pair`).
- **Circuits** (`src/su2plaq/circuits/spinnet.py`, `src/su2plaq/spinnet/walk.py`):
  - $U_E$: one `p(−3α/4)` per inner-link qubit ($j(j+1)=\tfrac34$ at $j=\tfrac12$). The *outer-link electric row* adds the same phase on the 8 outer qubits (12 rotations) and is reported separately.
  - $U_B$ ("general circuit", skeleton `GENERAL7`): a *frame* of 3 CNOTs that relabels the four link bits so the pair flip becomes a single $X$ on qubit 0; a Hadamard on that qubit turns $e^{-i\beta kX}$ into the diagonal $R_z(2\beta k)$; seven *flags* (ancilla qubits holding Boolean functions of the data bits: bright? vacuum pair? alternating pair?) computed with relative-phase Toffolis (`rccx`, Maslov arXiv:1508.03273; 3 CX + 4 T each); a *phase walk* of 4 $R_z$ rotations on qubit 0 interleaved with CNOTs from the flags, i.e. a Walsh series of the diagonal phase (Welch et al., New J. Phys. 16, 033040 (2014), arXiv:1306.3991 §2); then uncompute, Hadamard, frame reversed. The four angles are *solved* from $K$ at build time, never typed in: $(\theta_0,\theta_F,\theta_1,\theta_2)=(-\tfrac72,-1,3,\tfrac32)\beta$ (`metadata.angle_fractions`, reports/tests/101 §3, 102 §3), giving phase $0$ on dark codes, $+2\beta$ on the six ordinary pairs, $-4\beta$ on the vacuum pair, $-\beta$ on the alternating pair.
  - Trotter steps: order 1 is $U_BU_E$ (electric layer first in the circuit); order 2 is $U_E(\alpha/2)\,U_B\,U_E(\alpha/2)$.
- **Checker.** Every circuit is simulated on all 433 physical states with the 8 outer-link qubits present, on the project's sparse state-vector engine (no approximation; `engine_accuracy 0.0`). A circuit passes if (error on physical states) + (leaked amplitude, i.e. amplitude left on non-physical codes or on dirty ancillas) + (simulator pruning bound) $\le10^{-10}$ for exact angles, or $\le N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon+10^{-9}$ after Clifford+T synthesis (prompts/003–005). Gauss's law of the outputs is checked separately. External circuits are compared *up to a real diagonal sign convention of the code basis* found from the circuit's own $47\times47$ block (`align_inner_block`); the convention found is recorded, and the raw (unconverted) comparison is reported too.
- **T count:** $T=T_{\rm fixed}+\sum_{\rm rot}T_r(\varepsilon)$, each rotation synthesised into Clifford+T gates (the universal fault-tolerant gate set; $T$ is its only expensive gate) by pygridsynth (Ross–Selinger algorithm), with Qiskit's Clifford+T pass manager (seed 12345, optimisation level 3) as a second implementation and $3\log_2(1/\varepsilon)$ per rotation as the leading-order estimate.
- **Lean.** Lean 4 is a proof assistant; "kernel-checked" means its trusted core verified the proof from the definitions by computation (`decide`), with only the standard axiom `propext` or none. Theorems in `lean/Su2Plaq/SpinNet/{Counts,Circuit}.lean`; status in §3.5.

## 4. Physics findings along the way
- **Paulo's Fig. 2 claim verified on the open plaquette through the checker.** The plaquette operator does not depend on the outer links when each corner pairs its two inner links first: the route-B target is block-diagonal in the 128 outer patterns that carry states, every block equals the same $47\times47$ inner operator ($2.7\times10^{-17}$), and Paulo's 15-qubit file, which never touches the outer qubits, passes on all 433 states (§3.4). States per outer pattern: $\{2{:}41,\ 3{:}52,\ 4{:}8,\ 5{:}22,\ 9{:}4,\ 17{:}1\}$ patterns, closed pattern 2 states (`UB.json` `extra.physics.states_per_outer_pattern_histogram`).
- **The sign of $\beta$ is the centre-symmetry convention $(-1)^{2j_l}$ of one link, in every encoding and at every $j_{\max}$** (erratum prompts/101, audit 100 N1). The centre element $-\mathbb 1\in SU(2)$ acting on one inner link $l$ is the diagonal unitary $Z_l=(-1)^{2j_l}$ (since $D^j(-U)=(-1)^{2j}D^j(U)$, Edmonds Ch. 4); the plaquette is odd under it, $H_E$ and Gauss's law commute with it, so $U_B(-\beta)=Z_lU_B(\beta)Z_l$ and $H_E\pm H_B$ are isospectral. Measured: $\max|Z_lH_BZ_l+H_B|=0.0$ for each of the four inner links, routes A and B (audit 100). In the 12-qubit encoding $Z_l$ is a single Pauli $Z$ on the $j$ qubit of link $l$: `Z(q11)·build_UB(0.61)·Z(q11)` is accepted against $U_B(-0.61)$ with error $2.4\times10^{-13}$ and rejected against $U_B(+0.61)$ with $1.88$ (audit 100 N1). Consequence: every "$-\beta$ rejected" check (Report 1's M1-P1, this report's M3-S1) pins the codec's sign convention — Paulo's Eq. 31 for the 12-qubit codec, the route-B choice for the spin-network codec — and is not a physics check of the sign of the coupling. The earlier plan wording "a gauge at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$ because $K$ is a matching; absent in the 12-qubit encoding" was wrong and has been corrected in `docs/METHODS-spinnet.md` §2.
- **Counts and multiplicities.** 47 inner labels, 16 bright, 31 dark, 8 pairs, and the pair-class multiplicities $16/96/16$ (plus $177$ dark states) are kernel-checked; the couplings $-2$ (vacuum pair $0000\leftrightarrow1111$), $-\tfrac12$ (alternating pair $0101\leftrightarrow1010$), $+1$ (six pairs) are numerical (§3.5). Together they give the spectrum of $H_B$: eigenvalues $-2,-1,-\tfrac12,0,\tfrac12,1,2$ with multiplicities $16/96/16/177/16/96/16$ (tester's `eigh`, reports/tests/104 §3; same as Report 1's M0 value).
- **Angles are a closed-form function of $K$.** The 4-rotation Walsh series with angles $(-\tfrac72,-1,3,\tfrac32)\beta$ reproduces $2\beta k$ on all 47 valid slots with least-squares residual $4\times10^{-15}$ (reports/tests/101 §3); this is the exact-angle identity that `walk_table_exact` kernel-checks in integer units of $\beta/2$.

## 5. Problems and assumptions
- **Checker rules decided by Digonto (prompts/003–005), unchanged from Report 1:** Gauss tolerance scales with rounding ($N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon+10^{-9}$ for synthesised circuits); leakage is judged as an amplitude; the pruning cap is relative to the budget. Exact-angle tolerance $10^{-10}$ (prompts/100 §6).
- **Aer MPS unused.** Every verdict in the three JSONs has `engine: sparse`, `engine_accuracy: 0.0`; all rotations sit on the walk qubit and the four link qubits, so the sparse engine never grows. The matrix-product-state simulator's $2\times10^{-10}$ accuracy floor (Report 1) plays no role here.
- **Lean certificate pending** (§3.5, M3-L3): the laptop's `Su2PlaqCert` checker is not merged; until then the per-circuit certificate of the general step is not available and M3-L3 is the single red test of the milestone gate (reports/tests/106).
- **Qiskit's `RC3XGate` is not self-inverse** ($\|C^2-\mathbb 1\|=2.0$, $\|C-C^\dagger\|=2.0$, reports/tests/103 §2; `RCCX` *is* Hermitian and self-inverse, $0.0$). The vendored `spinnet_v2` skeletons therefore uncompute with `RC3XGate().inverse()`, and both are expanded to basis gates (6 CX, 8 T) so only the allowed gate set remains; the negative control with plain `rc3x` in the uncompute is rejected with block error $2.0$. su2qc-trotter is never imported by production code or tests (skeletons vendored with `SOURCE`, sibling commit `feadc42`).
- **Audit 100 N3 (laptop-owned evaluator, listed in `notes/merge-notes-spinnet.md`).** The span leak $S$ is left out of `op_norm_error` and the prompts/004 bound $\|S\|\le4\cdot$Gauss is not tight enough to self-certify synthesised $U_E$ at $10^{-6}$ through that chain ($2.18\times10^{-6}+4\times5.6\times10^{-7}=4.41\times10^{-6}>4.0\times10^{-6}$), although the exact total $2.50\times10^{-6}$ is within budget. - **Audit 100 N4** (Lean model tied to the Python skeleton only outside the suite): addressed in commit d82a472 per the benchmark header; not re-tested by a tester report since (§3.5).
- **Requested changes to laptop-owned files** (`notes/merge-notes-spinnet.md`, not applied): `NOMINAL_ANGLES["spinnet"] = (0.37, 0.61)` in `encodings/base.py`; `evaluator.inner_target` dispatch to `enc.inner_target` when defined (M3 routes around it with `SpinNet.inner_target`); `compress/baselines.import_seed` sources `"paulo15"` and `"spinnet_v2"` via `SpinNet.import_map` (M4 seeds); the two import lines appended to the shared `lean/Su2Plaq.lean`; and the laptop's flaky `test_results_unit.py::test_render_is_deterministic_and_contains_only_json_numbers` (fails when the short hash contains "c1").
