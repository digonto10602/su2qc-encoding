## 1. What was asked
Digonto decided on 2026-11-10 (STATUS Decisions, item 3; origin: audit 200 N1) to **add the determinant term** of the SU(2) quantum link model (QLM — the lattice gauge theory whose link variables are built from rishons, the fermions at the two link ends; Report 5 §1) to the Hamiltonian, as a **third Trotter layer**. Report 5 had set the det coupling to zero ($\gamma=0$), which makes the dynamics invariant under an extra U(1) symmetry and so describes the U(2) quantum link model, not the SU(2) one (Wiese, arXiv:1305.1602 §4.3). The task of milestone-5d was: build the layer as a Qiskit circuit, verify it on every Gauss-law state, count CX (CNOT) and T gates, compute the shared observables again, and keep every M5 result valid.

Rishon here is the SU(2) quantum link model plus the det term, with **38 432 physical states** (states obeying Gauss's law, Report 5 §2). It is a **different model** from the Kogut–Susskind (KS) theory at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$ (433 physical states) used by the twelve-qubit, spin-network and loop-string-hadron encodings; the KS columns below are comparisons between two theories, not checks of one against the other.

## 2. What was done
- **Model** (METHODS-rishon R13; Brower–Chandrasekharan–Wiese arXiv:hep-th/9704106 Eq. 4.5–4.6; Wiese arXiv:1305.1602 Eq. 4.14; equation numbers as quoted in METHODS-rishon R13):
$$H=\frac{g^2}2H_E+\frac1{g^2}H_B+\lambda_DH_D,\qquad H_D=\sum_{\ell=1}^{12}\big(\det U_\ell+\det U_\ell^\dagger\big),\qquad \det U=2\,c_{L1}c^\dagger_{R1}c_{L2}c^\dagger_{R2}.$$
Here $U$ is the $2\times2$ link matrix built from the rishons, $c$ are the four fermion modes of a link and $\lambda_D$ is the det coefficient (Wiese's $\gamma$; this project does not call it $\gamma$, because $\gamma$ already names the $\sqrt2\beta$ angle slot of the compression loop). On the six states of a link, $\det U=+2|B\rangle\langle A|$ ($A$: both rishons at the start end, $B$: both at the end), so per link $H_D$ moves both rishons to the other end and is $2(|A\rangle\langle B|+|B\rangle\langle A|)$; it is zero on the four quartet states. It keeps the SU(2) Gauss law ($[L_a,\det U]=[R_a,\det U]=0$) and breaks the link U(1).
- **Layer and orders.** The layer angle is $\eta=\lambda_D\,dt$, with $\alpha=\tfrac{g^2dt}2$ and $\beta=\tfrac{dt}{g^2}$ unchanged. The electric and det layers commute ($[H_D,H_E]=0$: both $A$ and $B$ have spin 0), so there are still two non-commuting groups and the orders are
$$U_1=U_B(\beta)\,U_D(\eta)\,U_E(\alpha),\qquad U_2=U_E(\tfrac\alpha2)U_D(\tfrac\eta2)\,U_B(\beta)\,U_D(\tfrac\eta2)U_E(\tfrac\alpha2),$$
circuit order for $U_1$: electric, det, magnetic. Measured convergence slopes of the local error: 1.994 (order 1) and 2.994 (order 2) between $dt=0.05$ and $0.025$ (reports/tests/222 rows "order 1/2 error"; prompts/204 and METHODS-rishon R13).
- **Circuit** (`src/su2plaq/circuits/rishon.py`, `build_UD`, `build_step(..., eta=...)`, `prepend_det_layer`). One *gadget* (the fixed small circuit for one link's det rotation): two frame CX, a Hadamard sandwich, two `rz(2η)` rotations between three CX, then the frame again: **8 CX, 2 rotations, no ancilla, global phase 1** per link (METHODS-rishon R14). The inner layer $U_D$ acts on the 4 inner links of the 16 data qubits. The 8 outer links form a separate row (below).
- **Verification.** The det layer touches only the data register, so the same inner-block checker as Report 5 (R6, 1296 inner codes, covering all 38 432 physical states) certifies it with tolerance $10^{-10}$, a sampled Gauss check on 273 physical states, and, since 2026-11-10, a rishon pruning cap of 10 % of the tolerance, $10^{-11}$ (R15; the shared evaluator is unchanged). The outer det row writes outer qubits, so it is certified separately link by link, as the outer electric row of Report 5 §3.7.
- **Defaults taken under the 2026-11-10 autonomy grant (default, not reviewed by Digonto).** *Update 2026-11-20: Digonto accepted the `prompts/204` §14 defaults (`notes/STATUS.md` Decisions).* Digonto gave full autonomy for 24 h on 2026-11-10 and the coordinator accepted the planner's defaults of prompts/204 (STATUS Decisions, "2026-11-10 AUTONOMOUS DECISION"). On 2026-11-16 Digonto asked that they be stated as defaults. They are:
  1. unit coefficient of $H_D$ and plus sign (only $|\lambda_D|$ matters; the map $S=\prod_\ell e^{i\frac\pi4(n_R-n_L)_\ell}$ flips the sign and leaves $H_E,H_B$ unchanged, measured $2\times10^{-16}$);
  2. nominal angle triple $(\alpha,\beta,\eta)=(0.37,0.61,0.29)$, i.e. $\lambda_D=0.4316\approx0.43$ at $g^2=1.1014$, $dt=0.6719$ (a generic test angle, not a physics value); alternates $(0.91,0.23,0.53)$ and $(1.70,1.00,1.07)$;
  3. det term on **all 12 links**, with the 8 outer links as a separate, separately certified row (main gate counts exclude it, like the outer electric row);
  4. compression unit stays $U_BU_E$ (the loop's task is unchanged) with the det layer put in front of it; Report 6 quotes both the unit and the three-layer step; the old archive is replayed under the new rule instead of edited in place (STATUS Decisions 2026-11-10; prompts/204 §6.2).

### 3.5 Structure of the physical space (`sector_structure`)
Without det the 38 432 states split into 431 sectors of conserved corner charges and 31 870 dynamical blocks (largest 3). The inner det terms give 4 976 blocks (largest 17: the 16 electric vacua $\{A,B\}^4$ and the loop). With all 12 links no U(1) charge survives (charge-change rank 4) and 305 blocks remain; the closed sector is one block of $4352=17\times2^8$ states. These integers are exact. The reference state ${\rm vac}_A$ is not an approximate ground state when $\lambda_D\ne0$. The leaving weight (the weight that any det or $H_B$ term sends out of the physical space) is $\le1.6\times10^{-25}$; see §4 for the file's older value.
