## 1. What was asked
Build, in Qiskit (IBM's open-source circuit library), the circuits for one Trotter step of the Kogut–Susskind Hamiltonian (the lattice SU(2) gauge theory) of one plaquette (the smallest closed loop of four links) with 8 outer links at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$ ($j$ = the spin carried by a link), in the **loop-string-hadron (LSH) encoding** that Digonto fixed in the project plan (quoted in `prompts/400` §1). A Trotter step is the product formula that approximates $e^{-i(\alpha H_E+\beta H_B)}$ by alternating the electric part $U_E(\alpha)=e^{-i\alpha H_E}$ and the magnetic part $U_B(\beta)=e^{-i\beta H_B}$. The circuit must be correct on all 433 Gauss-law states (the states obeying the lattice form of Gauss's law, i.e. a colour singlet at every corner), as in Reports 1 and 3. Count CNOT gates (the two-qubit gate) and T gates (the expensive gate of the Clifford+T gate set, the universal set used in fault-tolerant quantum computing), compare like for like with the spin-network encoding, and label every formal (Lean) claim by its trust level.

Jargon, once. *LSH formulation* = a rewriting of the gauge theory (Raychowdhury and Stryker, arXiv:1912.06133, Sec. V) in which every vertex of the lattice carries only gauge-invariant integers; for the pure gauge theory these are *loop numbers* $\ell_{ij}$, the number of flux units running through a three-way vertex from leg $i$ to leg $j$. *Point splitting* = replacing each four-way corner by two three-way sites ("trivalent" sites) joined by a zero-length *virtual link*. *'in' site / 'out' site* (our labels) = the site of a corner that holds its two inner links / its two outer links. *Flux* $N=2j$ of a leg. *Abelian Gauss law (AGL)* = the condition that the flux of a link is the same when read at its two ends (it is weaker than the full SU(2) Gauss law; see §3.7). *Site code* = the 3 loop-number bits of one site. *Flux cap* = the truncation $j\le\tfrac12$ on physical links. *Inner code* = the 12 bits of the four 'in' sites (the data register), *outer code* = the 12 bits of the four 'out' sites (read-only). *Bright / dark* = inner codes that the plaquette moves / annihilates. *Transcoder* (our label, not a term from the literature) = a reversible circuit that rewrites the LSH data bits in place into the bits of the spin-network encoding of Report 3. *Frame, flag, phase walk, ancilla* = building blocks of the magnetic circuit, explained in §2. *Evaluator / checker* = the project's own simulator that runs a circuit on all 433 states. *Kernel-checked* = Lean 4 (a proof assistant) verified a theorem from definitions by computation with only its standard axioms.

## 2. What was done
- **Model** (`docs/METHODS-lsh.md` L1–L3; code `src/su2plaq/lsh/operators.py`). The diagram is point-split with the **(inner | outer) pairing** at all four corners: the 'in' site of corner $c$ has legs $(p(c),n(c),v)$ (previous inner link, next inner link along the loop $b\to r\to t\to l$, virtual link) and the 'out' site has the two outer links of that corner and the same virtual link $v$. At a site with legs $(1,2,3)$ the loop numbers $(\ell_{12},\ell_{23},\ell_{31})$ give the fluxes $N_1=\ell_{31}+\ell_{12}$, $N_2=\ell_{12}+\ell_{23}$, $N_3=\ell_{23}+\ell_{31}$. With $j\le\tfrac12$ on the 12 physical links and spin $J\le1$ on the virtual links every loop number is 0 or 1, so each site is 3 qubits and the register is **24 qubits** (12 data + 12 outer). The five valid site codes (flux cap) are `000`, `001`, `010`, `100`, `110` (read $\ell_{31}\ell_{23}\ell_{12}$). A 24-bit code is physical if all 8 sites are valid and the AGL holds on the 4 inner links and the 4 virtual links. Brute force over all $2^{24}$ codes gives **433** physical codes, exactly the images of the 433 states of the other encodings (`extra.physics.n_physical_states`); the data register has **47** inner codes (16 bright, 31 dark = some site is `110`), the outer register 305 distinct patterns, and without the flux cap the AGL alone would admit 82 inner patterns and 5920 states (`extra.physics.n_inner_codes`, `n_bright_codes`, `n_dark_codes`, `n_outer_site_patterns`, `n_raw_inner_codes`, `n_raw_agl_states`).
- **Operators.** The plaquette operator is built from loop-number arithmetic only (no 6j symbols, no electric basis): Raychowdhury–Stryker's ladder operators, normalised ladder factors, vertex-factor matrices and the trace around the four corners (`docs/METHODS-lsh.md` L4), $H_B=-(\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P+\mathrm{h.c.})$, $H_E=\sum_\ell\frac{N_\ell}2(\frac{N_\ell}2+1)$ with the same normalisation as Reports 1 and 3 (METHODS §2). On the 47 inner codes the LSH plaquette matrix $K_{\rm LSH}$ equals the spin-network 6j matrix $K_{\rm inner}$ entry by entry, $\max|K_{\rm LSH}-K_{6j}|=1.78\times10^{-15}$ (`extra.physics.K_minus_K6j`), **with no sign gauge**: it is a matching of 8 bright pairs with couplings $-2$ (1 pair), $-\tfrac12$ (1 pair), $+1$ (6 pairs) and 31 dark rows (`extra.physics.couplings`).
- **Target and gauge.** The target of every circuit is the LSH operator in the route-B label order, the same $433\times433$ matrix as the spin-network target to $\le10^{-12}$ (locked test M7-C2; tester 401 worst difference $4.6\times10^{-15}$). Route A (the electric-basis construction, METHODS §4) differs from it by a diagonal $\pm1$ sign matrix with 64 minus signs, residual $8.9\times10^{-16}$ (`extra.physics.gauge_minus_count`, `gauge_residual`). Basis-independent wording (audit 303 N6): 64 of the 128 bright pairs of physical states have opposite matrix-element sign in route A and in LSH; which member of a pair carries the minus is a choice made by the alignment routine.
- **Gauss check of outputs** (`docs/METHODS-lsh.md` L6). A circuit output on a code whose data part is one of the 47 inner codes but which breaks the AGL on a virtual link (28 942 such codes, `extra.physics.n_agl_violating_data_valid_codes`) is decoded to a fixed product state with Gauss residual $\ge\tfrac12$ (measured minimum exactly 0.5, reports/tests/401 §2), so the checker's Gauss test catches it at first order. Other invalid codes are counted as leakage.
- **Circuits** (`src/su2plaq/circuits/lsh.py`, `src/su2plaq/lsh/walk.py`; `docs/METHODS-lsh.md` L7):
  - $U_E$: the electric energy of an 'in' site is $\tfrac34$ times its flux $N_n=\ell_{pn}+\ell_{nv}$ (the two bits are never both 1), so $U_E$ is a phase `p(-3α/4)` on each of the 8 link-carrying qubits, no CNOT. The *outer-link electric row* adds `p(-3α/2)`, `p(-3α/4)`, `p(-3α/4)` per 'out' site (12 more phases); it reads the outer register and is reported separately.
  - $U_B$ ("general circuit"): the plaquette move on a bright site is $s\mapsto s\oplus(1,0,0)\oplus w(s)(1,1,1)$ with $w=N_v\bmod2$; the 8 pair classes have 8 different difference vectors, so **no CNOT-only relabelling of the data bits can turn the move into a single-qubit $X$** (`docs/METHODS-lsh.md` L7, L13; the later Lean theorem `eight_differences`). The circuit therefore first applies the *transcoder* $T$: per 'in' site `cx(y,z); cx(y,x); rccx(z,x,y)` (a relative-phase Toffoli, Maslov arXiv:1508.03273, 3 CNOT + 4 T), which writes in place $j_b,j_r,j_t,j_l$ on wires $0,3,6,9$, the corner bits $[J_c=1]$ on wires $1,4,7,10$ and the parities $w_c$ on wires $2,5,8,11$. Together these are the spin-network data code plus redundant parities. $T$ costs 20 CNOT and 16 T, no ancilla (`extra.comparison.transcoder`). On the transcoded wires the circuit is exactly Report 3's construction: a *frame* of 3 CNOTs relabelling the four link bits so that the pair flip is one $X$ on wire 0, a Hadamard, seven *flags* (ancilla qubits holding Boolean functions of the data bits: bright? vacuum pair? alternating pair?, computed by 7 relative-phase Toffolis), a *phase walk* of 4 $R_z$ rotations on wire 0 interleaved with CNOTs from the flags (a Walsh series of the diagonal phase; Welch et al., New J. Phys. 16, 033040 (2014), arXiv:1306.3991), then everything undone in reverse order, and finally $T^\dagger$. $U_B=T^\dagger\,[\text{frame}\cdot H\cdot\text{flags}\cdot\text{walk}\cdot\text{flags}^{-1}\cdot H\cdot\text{frame}^{-1}]\,T$. The four walk angles are *solved* at build time from the LSH coupling table, never typed in: $(\theta_0,\theta_F,\theta_1,\theta_2)=(-\tfrac72,-1,3,\tfrac32)\beta$ (`results/lsh/UB.qasm3`, the four `rz` lines: $-2.135,\ -0.610,\ 1.830,\ 0.915$ at $\beta=0.61$). This is the project's own construction: no published gate-level circuit for the 2+1D pure-gauge LSH plaquette was found (search of 2026-11-10, `docs/METHODS-lsh.md` L1).
  - Trotter steps: order 1 is $U_BU_E$ (electric layer first in the circuit); order 2 is $U_E(\alpha/2)\,U_B\,U_E(\alpha/2)$. Inside the step the electric layer is applied after the transcoder on wires $0,3,6,9$ (one wire per link, 4 phases per layer instead of 8): the transcoder permutes basis states up to a diagonal phase, so a diagonal layer may sit on either side of it (`results/lsh/step.qasm3`: four `p(-0.2775)`, $0.2775=3\alpha/4$ at $\alpha=0.37$, on `d[0], d[3], d[6], d[9]`).
- **Checker.** As in Report 3: every circuit is simulated on all 433 physical states with the 12 outer qubits present, on the project's sparse state-vector engine (no approximation; `engine_accuracy 0.0`). A circuit passes if (error on physical states) + (leaked amplitude, i.e. amplitude left on non-physical codes or on dirty ancillas) + (pruning bound) $\le10^{-10}$ for exact angles, or $\le N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon+10^{-9}$ after Clifford+T synthesis (the checker rules of `prompts/003`–`005`, unchanged). Gauss's law of the outputs is checked separately.
- **T count:** $T=T_{\rm fixed}+\sum_{\rm rot}T_r(\varepsilon)$, each rotation synthesised into Clifford+T gates by pygridsynth (Ross–Selinger algorithm), with $3\log_2(1/\varepsilon)$ per rotation as the leading-order estimate.
- **Lean.** Theorems in `lean/Su2Plaq/LSH/{Counts,Circuit}.lean` and a native per-circuit certificate checker `lean/Su2PlaqCert/LSH/`; status and trust levels in §3.6.

The LSH step costs $+40$ CNOT ($+74\,\%$), $+32$ T at $10^{-6}$ ($+5.7\,\%$, 598 vs 566), $+4$ qubits (19 vs 15) and $+20$ depth (87 vs 67) compared with Report 3's general circuit. Against the twelve-qubit encoding of Report 1 (a different encoding, so not a like-for-like circuit comparison) the LSH general step uses $-68\,\%$ CNOT (94 vs 294) and $-37\,\%$ $T(10^{-6})$ (598 vs 952).
- **Limit of this comparison (audit 303 N7; `docs/METHODS-lsh.md` L1, L16).** With the (inner | outer) pairing the LSH loop basis *is* the spin-network intertwiner basis written in a different bit code; this is why the LSH circuit is the spin-network circuit plus a transcoder. A circuit in the uniform pairing would also need the corner re-coupling as a circuit that writes outer-site qubits; it is not built here.
- The Trotter-order table of Report 3 §3.6 (slopes $1.977$ and $2.980$) applies to the same target matrix and is not repeated; it was not recomputed for LSH.

### 3.7 Physics findings along the way
- **The ladder factors derived from Raychowdhury–Stryker's oscillators give the 6j operator with no sign gauge.** The dressed factors on the five valid site codes are `000`→`001`: $+1$, `001`→`000`: $+1$, `010`→`100`: $-1$, `100`→`010`: $-\tfrac12$ (moduli equal to the 6j coefficients of METHODS §5). The tester re-derived $K$ independently from a Schwinger-boson Fock space (two oscillators per leg, no vertex-factor table): $\max|K_{\rm Fock}-K_{6j}|=3.6\times10^{-15}$, $\max|K_{\rm LSH}-K_{6j}|=1.8\times10^{-15}$ (reports/tests/400). Spectra of $\frac{g^2}2H_E+\frac1{g^2}H_B$ agree with routes A and B to $\le1.0\times10^{-14}$ at $g^2\in\{0.5,1,2\}$ (`extra.physics.spectra_vs_route_A`: $1.02\times10^{-14}$, $5.2\times10^{-15}$, $7.1\times10^{-15}$; `spectra_vs_route_B`: $3.6\times10^{-15}$, $1.8\times10^{-15}$, $5.0\times10^{-16}$). The $H_B$ spectrum has the multiplicities $16/96/16/177/16/96/16$ at $-2,-1,-\tfrac12,0,\tfrac12,1,2$ (reports/tests/400), as in Reports 1 and 3.
- **433 states from two independent rules.** The AGL plus the flux cap give 433 physical codes in Python (brute force over $2^{24}$ codes, tester 400/401) and in Lean (`card_states_lsh`), while the other encodings count 433 from the corner-fusion rule (`card_physical`). The AGL alone (without the cap) gives 5920 states, so the cap is a part of the rule, not an add-on.
- **The Abelian Gauss law creates a "don't-care" space.** Because $\ell_{2v}$ of an 'out' site and $\ell_{pn}$ of the 'in' site of the same corner can never both be 1 on a physical code, a gate such as `cz(o[1], d[0])` is the identity on the 433 states and is accepted by the checker, while a swap of $\ell_{nv},\ell_{vp}$ on a site leaks with weight 1 (research 400 §5; locked M7-G1). A shorter circuit may exploit such gates; that is the idea M8 tests and is not part of this report.
- **The sign of $\beta$ is a basis-sign convention here, not physics.** As in Report 3 §4, $K$ is a matching, so $U_B(-\beta)$ equals a diagonal $\pm1$ conjugate of $U_B(\beta)$; "$-\beta$ rejected" pins the circuit to the target's sign gauge (the LSH sign convention of arXiv:1912.06133, which coincides with route B) and is not a check of the physical sign of the coupling (`docs/METHODS-lsh.md` L5, audit 303 N7, decision D7).
- **Cheaper recodings exist inside the register.** The five wrapped skeletons of §3.4 show that $+40$ CNOT is the cost of the transcoder as built here, not a lower bound; a CNOT-only recoding is impossible (`eight_differences`, `docs/METHODS-lsh.md` L13), one relative-phase Toffoli per site is the minimum of this construction (`prompts/400` §7).
