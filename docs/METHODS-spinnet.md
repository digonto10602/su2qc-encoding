# METHODS-spinnet — spin-network encoding (track S, milestones M3/M4)

Methods and conventions specific to the spin-network encoding, with sources. Complements `docs/METHODS.md` (laptop-owned; its §1–§11 apply unchanged: geometry, Hamiltonian, route A/B, evaluator, metrics, Lean). Written by the planner on 2026-11-06 (`prompts/100-spinnet-m3-plan.md`); numbers quoted as "measured" come from `scratch/planner/proto_m3_spinnet.py` and `scratch/planner/lean/SpinNetCounts.lean` run in the `su2qc-coding` env (`reports/research/100-…`); later steps fill §8.

Jargon, once: *label* = route-B quantum numbers $(j_0..j_{11};J_0..J_3)$ of a physical state; *code* = its 16-bit computational-basis integer; *inner code* = the 8 data bits; *bright / dark* = inner labels the plaquette moves / annihilates; *pair* = two bright labels exchanged by the plaquette; *gauge* = a sign per basis state (unobservable); *frame* = CNOT relabelling of bits; *phase walk* = $R_z$ rotations on one qubit between CNOT toggles (a Walsh series of a diagonal operator); *flag* = ancilla holding a Boolean function of data bits, computed and uncomputed.

## 1. Codec (Digonto's binding decision, START_HERE §4)
Paulo, notes Sec. IV.B (Eqs. 40–44, Fig. 1, Table I): one qubit per link ($j=0\to|0\rangle$, $j=\tfrac12\to|1\rangle$) and one qubit per corner coding the intertwiner (singlet–singlet $\to|0\rangle$, triplet–triplet $\to|1\rangle$), meaningful only when all four links at the corner are $\tfrac12$. In the project's (inner | outer) pairing (METHODS §5) the corner qubit is $[J_c=1]$, where $J_c$ is the spin of the inner pair (= of the outer pair): $J_c=1$ requires $T(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)=\{0,1\}$ on both sides, i.e. all four links $\tfrac12$ — Paulo's rule, with the two intertwiners "singlet–singlet / triplet–triplet" of his Eq. 44 identified with $J_c=0/1$ of the inner pair.

Registers (canonical frame of the encoding; circuits may order registers freely, `layout_of` maps through the register names `d`, `o`): data `d[0..7]` = inner links $b,r,t,l$ at qubits 0–3, corners $c_0..c_3$ at qubits 4–7; outer controls `o[0..7]` = outer links $o_0..o_7$ (METHODS §1 order) at qubits 8–15; ancillas after. Code: $\sum_{l<4}[j_l{=}\tfrac12]2^l+\sum_c[J_c{=}1]2^{4+c}+\sum_k[j_{o_k}{=}\tfrac12]2^{8+k}$. Properties (kernel-checked in `lean/Su2Plaq/SpinNet/Counts.lean`, M3-L1): the admissible $J$'s of a corner never contain both $0$ and $\tfrac12$, so the code is injective (`corner_jbit_injective`); a corner bit needs four $\tfrac12$ links (`corner_bit_needs_four_half`); 47 inner labels (16 bright: all $J\in\{0,\tfrac12\}$; 31 dark: some $J=1$), 8 bright pairs, 433 states. The 433 physical states are 433 distinct unit codes; the valid data codes are the 47 inner labels; every other 8-bit pattern (209) is don't-care. Unit codes make the evaluator's physical vectors trivial (`EncodedBasis` with one code and amplitude 1 per state) and keep `inner_span`/`physical` methods, leak and ancilla bookkeeping exactly as in M1.

## 2. Target: route B, and the gauge to route A
$H_B^{\rm B}=-(\mathcal P+\mathcal P^{\mathsf T})$ with $\mathcal P$ the product of four recoupling $F$-symbols (METHODS §5; Zache–González-Cuadra–Zoller arXiv:2304.02527 Eq. 2; Edmonds Eq. 6.1.5), in route-B label order (= `PhysicalBasis.labels`, asserted); $H_E^{\rm inner}=\sum_{\ell<4}j_\ell(j_\ell+1)$, $H_E^{\rm outer}$ likewise. Targets $U_E(\alpha)=e^{-i\alpha H_E^{\rm inner}}$, $U_B(\beta)=e^{-i\beta H_B^{\rm B}}$, steps as METHODS §2 (Paulo Eq. 16 normalisation, $\alpha_P=2\beta$). Route A is the same operator in a different sign convention of the basis: `gauge_align(H_A,H_B^{\rm B})` gives a real diagonal $D=\pm1$ (64 minus signs) with $H_A=DH_B^{\rm B}D^\dagger$ (residual $2.7\cdot10^{-15}$) and therefore $U^{\rm B}=D^\dagger U^{\rm A}D$ (measured $2.4\cdot10^{-15}$) — the "route B with `gauge_align`" rule of audit 001 N5. The decoded electric vector of the physical code $k$ is $\phi_k=D_k\psi^A_k$ (`decode_state`), which makes $\langle\phi_{k'}|H|\phi_k\rangle=(H_B^{\rm B})_{k'k}$ exact and keeps the Gauss check of outputs consistent with the target.

Inner structure (METHODS §5, measured on the inner codes): $H_B$ is block-diagonal in the 8 outer spins and every block is the restriction of $K=K_{\rm inner}$ ($47\times47$); on the inner codes $K$ connects each bright code with its partner $\mathrm{code}\oplus0b1111$ (all four inner spins flip, corner bits unchanged) with couplings $K=-2$ (vacuum pair $0000\leftrightarrow1111$, Paulo Eq. 37), $-\tfrac12$ (alternating pair $0101\leftrightarrow1010$), $+1$ (the six other pairs); the 31 dark codes have zero rows. The target restricted to any outer pattern equals $e^{-i\beta K}$ on the compatible inner codes (Fig. 2 claim; asserted $\le10^{-12}$, M3-K1), so an outer-blind circuit exact on the 47 inner codes is exact on all 433 states.

**Sign of $\beta$ is a gauge at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$.** $K$ is a matching, so $D'KD'=-K$ for $D'=-1$ on one member of each pair, hence $U_B(-\beta)=D'U_B(\beta)D'$ in the code basis (M3-K1 asserts `gauge_align` residual $\le10^{-10}$ with exactly 8 minus signs) and $H_E\pm H_B$ are isospectral. A circuit for $U_B(\beta)$ is therefore also a circuit for $U_B(-\beta)$ in the opposite pair-sign convention; comparisons with external circuits are made up to such a gauge (§5) and the gauge is recorded. In the 12-qubit electric-basis encoding this freedom does not exist (M1-P1 rejects $-\beta$), because its basis signs are fixed by the CG construction and Paulo's codec.

## 3. Decoding of outputs for the Gauss check (`decode_state`)
The evaluator decodes every output component whose data code is a valid inner code (any outer pattern). Of the $47\cdot256=12\,032$ such codes, 433 are physical and 11 599 are inadmissible labels (a corner whose inner and outer pairs have no common $J$, or a corner bit set where $J=1$ is not admissible). Inadmissible codes are decoded to the **product state** with every spin-$\tfrac12$ end at corner $c$ at $m=+\tfrac12$ if the corner bit is 0 and $-\tfrac12$ if it is 1, far ends of outer links at $+\tfrac12$ (CG first $m$). Properties: (i) an inadmissible corner always carries $\ge1$ spin-$\tfrac12$ end, so the product has $M\neq0$ there and is orthogonal to every physical state (corner singlets have $M=0$); (ii) codes with different link strings, or different corner bits at a corner with spin-$\tfrac12$ ends, are orthogonal — corner bits can only be set at corners with both inner links $\tfrac12$ — so `decode_state` is an isometry on the whole code space; (iii) Gauss residual $\max_{c,a}\|G^a_c\phi\|\ge\|G^z_c\phi\|=k/2\ge\tfrac12$ for $k$ aligned ends (measured minimum over all 11 599 codes: exactly $0.5$). Hence the span-leak bound of prompts/004 ($\|s\|\le4\cdot$`gauss_residual`, hypothesis $\sum\|G^a_cs\|^2\ge\tfrac34\|s\|^2$) holds on this encoding's code space, and any amplitude on an inadmissible code is seen by the Gauss check at first order.

## 4. General circuit for $U_B(\beta)$: frame + flags + phase walk, angles solved from $K$
On a bright pair with coupling $k$, $U_B=e^{-i\beta kX}$ (the pair-flip is $X^{\otimes4}$ on the link qubits); on dark codes $U_B=1$. Construction (`circuits/spinnet.build_UB` = `spinnet.walk.build_walk(GENERAL7)`):
1. **Frame** `cx(1,3); cx(0,1); cx(0,2)` on the link qubits (control, target): $q_0=j_b$, $q_1=m_1=j_b\oplus j_r$, $q_2=m_2=j_b\oplus j_t$, $q_3=m_3=j_r\oplus j_l$; the four-link flip becomes $X$ on $q_0$ and $m$ is invariant (`frame_maps_flip`, kernel-checked). Pair classes by $m$: vacuum $000$, alternating $100$, six others.
2. `h(q0)`, so the pair operator becomes the diagonal $e^{-i\beta kZ_0}=R_z(2\beta k)$ (Qiskit $R_z(\theta)=\mathrm{diag}(e^{-i\theta/2},e^{i\theta/2})$); on dark slots the phase must be $0$ exactly (the partner code of a dark label is invalid: a non-zero angle would leak into it).
3. **Flags** in clean ancillas `a[0..6]` $=(A,B,F,P,Q,G_1,G_2)$ by relative-phase Toffolis (`rccx`, Maslov arXiv:1508.03273; 3 CX, 4 T each): $A=\bar c_0\bar c_1$, $B=\bar c_2\bar c_3$, $F=AB$ (bright flag), $P=\bar m_2\bar m_3$, $Q=FP$, $G_1=Q\bar m_1$ (vacuum pair), $G_2=Qm_1$ (alternating pair); negations by `x` before/after (`flags_correct`, kernel-checked).
4. **Walk** on $q_0$: `rz(θ0)`, then for each flag $w\in\{F,G_1,G_2\}$: `cx(w,q0) rz(θ_w) cx(w,q0)` — a Walsh series $\theta(y)=\theta_0+\theta_F\chi_F(y)+\theta_1\chi_{G_1}(y)+\theta_2\chi_{G_2}(y)$, $\chi_w=(-1)^{w}$ (Welch, Greenbaum, Mostame, Aspuru-Guzik, "Efficient quantum circuits for diagonal unitaries without ancillas", New J. Phys. 16, 033040 (2014), arXiv:1306.3991, Sec. 2: any diagonal unitary is $\exp(i\sum_S\theta_S\bigotimes_{j\in S}Z_j)$ and each Walsh term costs one rotation between CNOT toggles). The angles are **solved** from $\theta(y)=2\beta k(y)$ on the valid slots (dark slots $0$) by least squares (`solve_angles`; residual asserted $\le10^{-9}$, measured $4\cdot10^{-15}$): $(\theta_0,\theta_F,\theta_1,\theta_2)=(-\tfrac72,-1,3,\tfrac32)\beta$, i.e. dark $0$, six pairs $+2\beta$, vacuum $-4\beta$, alternating $-\beta$. Exact rationals (`angle_fractions`) are recorded; `walk_table_exact` kernel-checks the integer identity $\sum_r a_r(-1)^{p_r(y)}=2\cdot(2k(y))$ in units of $\beta/2$ ($a=(-7,-2,6,3)$).
5. **Uncompute** (flags reversed; `rccx` is Hermitian and self-inverse), `h(q0)`, frame reversed. Exactness: the walk is diagonal in the computational basis, so $C^\dagger\,W\,C=\Pi^\dagger W\Pi$ for the relative-phase compute $C=\Pi\cdot{\rm diag}$ (METHODS §11.1 sandwich lemma, `Su2Plaq.sandwich`); ancillas return to $|0\rangle$ exactly.
Measured: 54 CX, 4 rotations, $T_{\rm fixed}=56$ ($14$ `rccx`), depth 67, 7 ancillas (15 qubits; 23 with the outer register); error $1.0$–$1.4\cdot10^{-14}$ at $\beta\in\{0.23,0.61,1.0\}$, leak 0, Gauss $5\cdot10^{-16}$; steps order 1/2: $1.2\cdot10^{-14}$; outer bits read 0 (one block). The skeleton (gate names + qubits) does not depend on $\beta$ and all angles are rational multiples of $\beta$ (denominator 2): angle-linear in the sense of METHODS §11.2.

$U_E(\alpha)$: `p(-3α/4)` on each inner-link qubit (METHODS §2); outer row: the same on `o[0..7]` (12 phases; its synthesised version is `writes_outer` for this evaluator, bound $12\varepsilon$ by the triangle inequality, audit 003 N2). Steps: $U_E$ layer first (order 1), symmetric half layers (order 2), global phase composed once (audit 003 N5).
