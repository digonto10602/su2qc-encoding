# METHODS-spinnet — spin-network encoding (track S, milestones M3/M4)

Methods and conventions specific to the spin-network encoding, with sources. Complements `docs/METHODS.md` (laptop-owned; its §1–§11 apply unchanged: geometry, Hamiltonian, route A/B, evaluator, metrics, Lean). Written by the planner on 2026-11-06 (`prompts/100-spinnet-m3-plan.md`); numbers quoted as "measured" come from `scratch/planner/proto_m3_spinnet.py` and `scratch/planner/lean/SpinNetCounts.lean` run in the `su2qc-coding` env (`reports/research/100-…`). §8 (step 3.8, planner, 2026-11-06) holds the **measured** numbers of the production code, copied from `results/spinnet/{UE,UB,step}.json` (commit `7a716fc`) and `reports/tests/100`–`105`, each with its file and field; where §1–§7 quote a prototype number that §8 supersedes, §8 wins. **Erratum (2026-11-06, audit `reports/audits/100-m3-spinnet-audit.md` N1/N2, `prompts/101-spinnet-m3-erratum-beta-sign-gauge.md`):** the sign-of-$\beta$ paragraph of §2 and the gauge wording in §5 and §8.5 were corrected; the earlier claim that the sign freedom "does not exist in the 12-qubit encoding" was wrong.

Jargon, once: *label* = route-B quantum numbers $(j_0..j_{11};J_0..J_3)$ of a physical state; *code* = its 16-bit computational-basis integer; *inner code* = the 8 data bits; *bright / dark* = inner labels the plaquette moves / annihilates; *pair* = two bright labels exchanged by the plaquette; *gauge* = a sign per basis state (unobservable); *frame* = CNOT relabelling of bits; *phase walk* = $R_z$ rotations on one qubit between CNOT toggles (a Walsh series of a diagonal operator); *flag* = ancilla holding a Boolean function of data bits, computed and uncomputed.

## 1. Codec (Digonto's binding decision, START_HERE §4)
Paulo, notes Sec. IV.B (Eqs. 40–44, Fig. 1, Table I): one qubit per link ($j=0\to|0\rangle$, $j=\tfrac12\to|1\rangle$) and one qubit per corner coding the intertwiner (singlet–singlet $\to|0\rangle$, triplet–triplet $\to|1\rangle$), meaningful only when all four links at the corner are $\tfrac12$. In the project's (inner | outer) pairing (METHODS §5) the corner qubit is $[J_c=1]$, where $J_c$ is the spin of the inner pair (= of the outer pair): $J_c=1$ requires $T(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)=\{0,1\}$ on both sides, i.e. all four links $\tfrac12$ — Paulo's rule, with the two intertwiners "singlet–singlet / triplet–triplet" of his Eq. 44 identified with $J_c=0/1$ of the inner pair.

Registers (canonical frame of the encoding; circuits may order registers freely, `layout_of` maps through the register names `d`, `o`): data `d[0..7]` = inner links $b,r,t,l$ at qubits 0–3, corners $c_0..c_3$ at qubits 4–7; outer controls `o[0..7]` = outer links $o_0..o_7$ (METHODS §1 order) at qubits 8–15; ancillas after. Code: $\sum_{l<4}[j_l{=}\tfrac12]2^l+\sum_c[J_c{=}1]2^{4+c}+\sum_k[j_{o_k}{=}\tfrac12]2^{8+k}$. Properties (kernel-checked in `lean/Su2Plaq/SpinNet/Counts.lean`, M3-L1): the admissible $J$'s of a corner never contain both $0$ and $\tfrac12$, so the code is injective (`corner_jbit_injective`); a corner bit needs four $\tfrac12$ links (`corner_bit_needs_four_half`); 47 inner labels (16 bright: all $J\in\{0,\tfrac12\}$; 31 dark: some $J=1$), 8 bright pairs, 433 states. The 433 physical states are 433 distinct unit codes; the valid data codes are the 47 inner labels; every other 8-bit pattern (209) is don't-care. Unit codes make the evaluator's physical vectors trivial (`EncodedBasis` with one code and amplitude 1 per state) and keep `inner_span`/`physical` methods, leak and ancilla bookkeeping exactly as in M1.

## 2. Target: route B, and the gauge to route A
$H_B^{\rm B}=-(\mathcal P+\mathcal P^{\mathsf T})$ with $\mathcal P$ the product of four recoupling $F$-symbols (METHODS §5; Zache–González-Cuadra–Zoller arXiv:2304.02527 Eq. 2; Edmonds Eq. 6.1.5), in route-B label order (= `PhysicalBasis.labels`, asserted); $H_E^{\rm inner}=\sum_{\ell<4}j_\ell(j_\ell+1)$, $H_E^{\rm outer}$ likewise. Targets $U_E(\alpha)=e^{-i\alpha H_E^{\rm inner}}$, $U_B(\beta)=e^{-i\beta H_B^{\rm B}}$, steps as METHODS §2 (Paulo Eq. 16 normalisation, $\alpha_P=2\beta$). Route A is the same operator in a different sign convention of the basis: `gauge_align(H_A,H_B^{\rm B})` gives a real diagonal $D=\pm1$ (64 minus signs) with $H_A=DH_B^{\rm B}D^\dagger$ (residual $2.7\cdot10^{-15}$) and therefore $U^{\rm B}=D^\dagger U^{\rm A}D$ (measured $2.4\cdot10^{-15}$) — the "route B with `gauge_align`" rule of audit 001 N5. The decoded electric vector of the physical code $k$ is $\phi_k=D_k\psi^A_k$ (`decode_state`), which makes $\langle\phi_{k'}|H|\phi_k\rangle=(H_B^{\rm B})_{k'k}$ exact and keeps the Gauss check of outputs consistent with the target.

Inner structure (METHODS §5, measured on the inner codes): $H_B$ is block-diagonal in the 8 outer spins and every block is the restriction of $K=K_{\rm inner}$ ($47\times47$); on the inner codes $K$ connects each bright code with its partner $\mathrm{code}\oplus0b1111$ (all four inner spins flip, corner bits unchanged) with couplings $K=-2$ (vacuum pair $0000\leftrightarrow1111$, Paulo Eq. 37), $-\tfrac12$ (alternating pair $0101\leftrightarrow1010$), $+1$ (the six other pairs); the 31 dark codes have zero rows. The target restricted to any outer pattern equals $e^{-i\beta K}$ on the compatible inner codes (Fig. 2 claim; asserted $\le10^{-12}$, M3-K1), so an outer-blind circuit exact on the 47 inner codes is exact on all 433 states.

**Sign of $\beta$ is a basis-sign convention in every encoding and at every $j_{\max}$ (corrected per audit 100 N1).** The sign flip of $\beta$ is generated by the centre element $-\mathbb 1\in SU(2)$ acting on **one** inner link $l$. Since $D^j(-U)=(-1)^{2j}D^j(U)$ (Edmonds, *Angular Momentum in Quantum Mechanics*, Ch. 4; $D^j(-\mathbb 1)=(-1)^{2j}\mathbb 1$), the unitary $Z_l=(-1)^{2j_l}$ — diagonal in every electric or spin-network basis — maps $U_l\to-U_l$; the plaquette is a product of four link operators, each odd under this map, so
$$Z_l\,\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P\,Z_l=-\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P,\qquad Z_lH_BZ_l=-H_B,\qquad [Z_l,H_E]=[Z_l,G^a_c]=0,$$
hence $U_B(-\beta)=Z_lU_B(\beta)Z_l$ for the single plaquette with static outer links at **any** $j_{\max}$, and $H_E\pm H_B$ are isospectral ($Z_l(H_E+H_B)Z_l=H_E-H_B$). Measured (audit 100 §3 N1, `audit_center.py`): $\max|Z_lH_BZ_l+H_B|=0.0$ for each of the four inner links, in route B and in route A. At $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$ the same fact reads: $K$ is a matching in which the partner flips all four links, so $Z_l$ is $-1$ on exactly one member of each bright pair, i.e. one of the "one $-1$ per pair" diagonals $D'$ with $D'KD'=-K$ (M3-K1 asserts `gauge_align` residual $\le10^{-10}$ with exactly 8 minus signs; the locked test `tests/acceptance/m3/test_spinnet_external.py` accepts only this class). A circuit for $U_B(\beta)$ is therefore also a circuit for $U_B(-\beta)$ in the opposite sign convention of link $l$'s $j=\tfrac12$ states; comparisons with external circuits are made up to such a gauge (§5) and the gauge is recorded.

The same freedom **exists in the 12-qubit electric-basis encoding**, where $Z_l$ is a single Pauli $Z$ on the $j$ qubit of link $l$ (link $l$ occupies qubits 9–11, $j$ qubit $q_{11}$): `Z(q11)·build_UB(0.61)·Z(q11)` is accepted by the evaluator against $U_B(-0.61)$ with error $2.4\cdot10^{-13}$ and rejected against $U_B(+0.61)$ with error $1.88$ (audit 100 N1, `audit_twelve_center.py`). Consequently every "$-\beta$ rejected" check (M1-P1 in the 12-qubit encoding, M3-S1 and the §8.1 negative control here) pins the **codec's** sign convention — fixed by Paulo's Eq. 31 for the 12-qubit codec and by the route-B choice (§2, audit 001 N5) for the spin-network codec — and is not a physics check of the sign of the coupling. Paulo's 15-qubit file differs from route B by exactly $(-1)^{2j_l}$ on the left link (§5).

## 3. Decoding of outputs for the Gauss check (`decode_state`)
The evaluator decodes every output component whose data code is a valid inner code (any outer pattern). Of the $47\cdot256=12\,032$ such codes, 433 are physical and 11 599 are inadmissible labels (a corner whose inner and outer pairs have no common $J$, or a corner bit set where $J=1$ is not admissible). Inadmissible codes are decoded to the **product state** in which every spin-$\tfrac12$ end at corner $c$ is an eigenstate of that corner's Gauss generator $G^z_c$ with eigenvalue
$$g_c=+\tfrac12\ \text{(corner bit 0)},\qquad g_c=-\tfrac12\ \text{(corner bit 1)},$$
and the far ends of outer links are at $m=+\tfrac12$ (CG first $m$). *Convention that makes this concrete* (`LinkSpace` generators, asserted by the tester in reports/tests/100 §2): on an "out" end the diagonal generator is $L^z=-m_L$, on an "in" end it is $R^z=+m_R$. The eigenvalue $g_c$ is therefore realised by
$$m_L=-g_c\ \text{on out ends},\qquad m_R=+g_c\ \text{on in ends}$$
(`SpinNet._fallback` in `src/su2plaq/encodings/spinnet.py`). All $k$ spin-$\tfrac12$ ends at a corner then carry the same sign of $G^z$, so $G^z_c\phi=\pm\tfrac k2\phi$ with $|G^z_c|=k/2\ge\tfrac12$.

**Do not use the prototype's rule** "$m=\pm\tfrac12$ on every end" (reports/research/100, `scratch/planner/proto_m3_spinnet.py`): fixing $m$ instead of $G^z$ gives an out end and an in end at the same corner opposite $G^z$ signs, which can cancel ($G^z_c=0$, an $M=0$ component along the corner singlet), so inadmissible codes overlapped physical states — 76 codes in the builder's reproduction, 60 in the tester's, maximum overlap $0.354$ in both (reports/tests/100 §2a; the count depends on how the discarded rule is reconstructed, the defect does not). The builder's deviation above fixes this; it was judged correct by the tester (reports/tests/100 §2a).

Properties, with the measured values (reports/tests/100 §2a, own Gauss operator and the library `gauss_residuals_coo`, which agree to $0.0$): (i) an inadmissible corner always carries $\ge1$ spin-$\tfrac12$ end, so $G^z_c\ne0$ there and the product is orthogonal to every physical state (corner singlets have $G_c=0$): overlap with any of the 433 physical states exactly $0$; (ii) codes with different link strings, or different corner bits at a corner with spin-$\tfrac12$ ends, are orthogonal — corner bits can only be set at corners with both inner links $\tfrac12$ — so `decode_state` is an isometry on the whole code space: Gram matrix of all $47\cdot256=12\,032$ decoded codes equals $\mathbb 1$ to $\max|G-\mathbb 1|=7.3\cdot10^{-15}$ (the physical block's route-A rounding; inadmissible–inadmissible off-diagonal exactly $0$); (iii) Gauss residual $\max_{c,a}\|G^a_c\phi\|\ge\|G^z_c\phi\|=k/2\ge\tfrac12$: minimum over all 11 599 inadmissible codes exactly $0.5$, maximum $2.0$; (iv) the 433 physical codes never reach the fallback (they are looked up in `_index` first; decoded vector $=$ `gauge`$\,\times$ route-A state to $0.0$, Gauss residual $5.4\cdot10^{-16}$). Hence the span-leak bound of prompts/004 ($\|s\|\le4\cdot$`gauss_residual`, hypothesis $\sum\|G^a_cs\|^2\ge\tfrac34\|s\|^2$) holds on this encoding's code space, and any amplitude on an inadmissible code is seen by the Gauss check at first order. Acceptance: M3-C1 (fallback Gauss $\ge\tfrac12-10^{-10}$, overlaps $\le10^{-12}$; justification in prompts/100 §6).

## 4. General circuit for $U_B(\beta)$: frame + flags + phase walk, angles solved from $K$
On a bright pair with coupling $k$, $U_B=e^{-i\beta kX}$ (the pair-flip is $X^{\otimes4}$ on the link qubits); on dark codes $U_B=1$. Construction (`circuits/spinnet.build_UB` = `spinnet.walk.build_walk(GENERAL7)`):
1. **Frame** `cx(1,3); cx(0,1); cx(0,2)` on the link qubits (control, target): $q_0=j_b$, $q_1=m_1=j_b\oplus j_r$, $q_2=m_2=j_b\oplus j_t$, $q_3=m_3=j_r\oplus j_l$; the four-link flip becomes $X$ on $q_0$ and $m$ is invariant (`frame_maps_flip`, kernel-checked). Pair classes by $m$: vacuum $000$, alternating $100$, six others.
2. `h(q0)`, so the pair operator becomes the diagonal $e^{-i\beta kZ_0}=R_z(2\beta k)$ (Qiskit $R_z(\theta)=\mathrm{diag}(e^{-i\theta/2},e^{i\theta/2})$); on dark slots the phase must be $0$ exactly (the partner code of a dark label is invalid: a non-zero angle would leak into it).
3. **Flags** in clean ancillas `a[0..6]` $=(A,B,F,P,Q,G_1,G_2)$ by relative-phase Toffolis (`rccx`, Maslov arXiv:1508.03273; 3 CX, 4 T each): $A=\bar c_0\bar c_1$, $B=\bar c_2\bar c_3$, $F=AB$ (bright flag), $P=\bar m_2\bar m_3$, $Q=FP$, $G_1=Q\bar m_1$ (vacuum pair), $G_2=Qm_1$ (alternating pair); negations by `x` before/after (`flags_correct`, kernel-checked).
4. **Walk** on $q_0$: `rz(θ0)`, then for each flag $w\in\{F,G_1,G_2\}$: `cx(w,q0) rz(θ_w) cx(w,q0)` — a Walsh series $\theta(y)=\theta_0+\theta_F\chi_F(y)+\theta_1\chi_{G_1}(y)+\theta_2\chi_{G_2}(y)$, $\chi_w=(-1)^{w}$ (Welch, Greenbaum, Mostame, Aspuru-Guzik, "Efficient quantum circuits for diagonal unitaries without ancillas", New J. Phys. 16, 033040 (2014), arXiv:1306.3991, Sec. 2: any diagonal unitary is $\exp(i\sum_S\theta_S\bigotimes_{j\in S}Z_j)$ and each Walsh term costs one rotation between CNOT toggles). The angles are **solved** from $\theta(y)=2\beta k(y)$ on the valid slots (dark slots $0$) by least squares (`solve_angles`; residual asserted $\le10^{-9}$, measured $4\cdot10^{-15}$): $(\theta_0,\theta_F,\theta_1,\theta_2)=(-\tfrac72,-1,3,\tfrac32)\beta$, i.e. dark $0$, six pairs $+2\beta$, vacuum $-4\beta$, alternating $-\beta$. Exact rationals (`angle_fractions`) are recorded; `walk_table_exact` kernel-checks the integer identity $\sum_r a_r(-1)^{p_r(y)}=2\cdot(2k(y))$ in units of $\beta/2$ ($a=(-7,-2,6,3)$).
   **Snapping of the coupling table** (`spinnet.walk.coupling_table`, reports/tests/105): the table $k(y)$ fed to `solve_angles` and to `lean_export` is read column by column from `enc.K_codes`; each bright column must have exactly one non-zero entry, at the partner $y\oplus0b1111$ (otherwise `ValueError`, "not a pair matching"); the entry is then **snapped to the nearest half-integer**, $k\mapsto\mathrm{round}(2k)/2$, and the function raises `ValueError` if $|2k-\mathrm{round}(2k)|>10^{-12}$. Justification: the exact couplings are $-2,-\tfrac12,+1$ (products of F-symbols with Edmonds' conventions), while the float64 $K$ carries $\sim10^{-15}$ rounding from the `eigh`-built corner singlets (measured $-2.0000000000000018$, $1.0000000000000004$, reports/tests/100 §2b); the threshold $10^{-12}$ is three orders above that rounding and twelve orders below the half-integer spacing $\tfrac12$, so it can neither accept a wrong coupling nor reject a correct one. Effect: the solved `rz` angles become exact multiples of $\beta/2$ (change versus the unsnapped solve $\le6.2\cdot10^{-15}$ at $\beta\le2$, reports/tests/105), and the integer tables exported to Lean are exactly $2K$ rather than a float rounded on export.
5. **Uncompute** (flags reversed; `rccx` is Hermitian and self-inverse), `h(q0)`, frame reversed. Exactness: the walk is diagonal in the computational basis, so $C^\dagger\,W\,C=\Pi^\dagger W\Pi$ for the relative-phase compute $C=\Pi\cdot{\rm diag}$ (METHODS §11.1 sandwich lemma, `Su2Plaq.sandwich`); ancillas return to $|0\rangle$ exactly.
Measured: 54 CX, 4 rotations, $T_{\rm fixed}=56$ ($14$ `rccx`), depth 67, 7 ancillas (15 qubits; 23 with the outer register); error $1.0$–$1.4\cdot10^{-14}$ at $\beta\in\{0.23,0.61,1.0\}$, leak 0, Gauss $5\cdot10^{-16}$; steps order 1/2: $1.2\cdot10^{-14}$; outer bits read 0 (one block). The skeleton (gate names + qubits) does not depend on $\beta$ and all angles are rational multiples of $\beta$ (denominator 2): angle-linear in the sense of METHODS §11.2.

$U_E(\alpha)$: `p(-3α/4)` on each inner-link qubit (METHODS §2); outer row: the same on `o[0..7]` (12 phases; its synthesised version is `writes_outer` for this evaluator, bound $12\varepsilon$ by the triangle inequality, audit 003 N2). Steps: $U_E$ layer first (order 1), symmetric half layers (order 2), global phase composed once (audit 003 N5).

**9.1 Exact arithmetic.** Rationals are unnormalised pairs $(\mathrm{num}\in\mathbb Z,\mathrm{den}\in\mathbb N_{>0})$ compared by cross-multiplication (`Frac`); a 6j symbol is kept as $q\sqrt r$ (`SR`, $q,r\in\mathbb Q$, $r>0$); products stay of that form; `SR.toFrac?` returns the rational value when the reduced $r$ is a square of integers (bounded integer square root, `List.range 512`), `none` otherwise. **9.2 6j and F-symbols** (Edmonds 6.3.7 / 6.1.5, doubled spins, exactly the formulas of `su2plaq/su2/wigner.py`):
$$\begin{Bmatrix}a&b&c\\d&e&f\end{Bmatrix}=\Delta(abc)\Delta(aef)\Delta(dbf)\Delta(dec)\sum_{z=\max t_i}^{\min q_j}\frac{(-1)^z(z+1)!}{\prod_i(z-t_i)!\prod_j(q_j-z)!},\quad \Delta(abc)=\sqrt{\tfrac{(a+b-c)!(a-b+c)!(-a+b+c)!}{(a+b+c+1)!}},$$
$t=(a{+}b{+}c,\ a{+}e{+}f,\ d{+}b{+}f,\ d{+}e{+}c)$, $q=(a{+}b{+}d{+}e,\ a{+}c{+}d{+}f,\ b{+}c{+}e{+}f)$, zero unless the four triads are triangles with integer sum; $F^{j_1j_2j_{12}}_{j_3Jj_{23}}=(-1)^{j_1+j_2+j_3+J}\sqrt{(2j_{12}+1)(2j_{23}+1)}\{j_1j_2j_{12};j_3Jj_{23}\}$ with the phase exponent taken as $\lfloor(2j_1+2j_2+2j_3+2J)/2\rfloor$ (as the code). Checked values at $j\le1$: research 101 §3.4. Zero pattern on $\{0,\tfrac12,1\}^6$ (prompts/103, `scratch/planner/m3x_test_fix_probe.py`, sympy 1.14.0 `wigner_6j`): the symbol is non-zero exactly on the 47 of 729 tuples whose four triads obey the triangle rule (no accidental zeros at $j\le1$); smallest non-zero $|6j|=\tfrac16$; the nine distinct non-zero values are $\pm$ among $\{\tfrac{\sqrt2}{2},\tfrac12,\tfrac13,\tfrac16,\tfrac{\sqrt6}{6},\tfrac{\sqrt3}{3},1\}$ (signs: $-\tfrac{\sqrt2}{2},-\tfrac12,-\tfrac13$ negative, the rest positive). M3X-W1 asserts this pattern, exact `0.0` elsewhere, and agreement with sympy to $10^{-12}$.

**9.3 Codec and route-B $K$ in Lean** (§1, §2): $J_c(y)=1$ if bit $4+c$, else $\tfrac12$ if $j_{p(c)}\ne j_{n(c)}$, else $0$; `validCode` = every set corner bit sits at a corner with both inner links $\tfrac12$; $P[y',y]=\prod_cF^{J_c\,j_p\,j_n}_{\frac12\,j'_n\,j'_p}$ iff the four link bits are flipped and all $J_c$ agree, else $0$; $K=-(P+P^{\mathsf T})$. **9.4 Exact gate semantics** (Qiskit matrices, little-endian, $z=e^{i\beta/4}$): coefficients in $\mathbb Z[i][z^{\pm1}]$; $R_z(a\beta/2)=\mathrm{diag}(z^{-a},z^{a})$; $H$ unnormalised with the global $(1/\sqrt2)^h$ counted (`ub_h_count : hCount ubGates = 2`); `rccx`$(a,b,t)$: $|1,1,t\rangle\mapsto\pm i|1,1,\neg t\rangle$ ($+i$ for $t{:}0\to1$), $|1,0,1\rangle\mapsto-|1,0,1\rangle$, all else fixed (`RCCXGate().to_matrix()`, index $a+2b+4t$). Target $2e^{-i\beta K}|y\rangle=(z^{4k}+z^{-4k})|y\rangle-(z^{4k}-z^{-4k})|y\oplus15\rangle$ with $4k=2\cdot(2K)$, dark $2|y\rangle$.
