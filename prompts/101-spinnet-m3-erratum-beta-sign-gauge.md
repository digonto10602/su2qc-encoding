## What was wrong
prompts/100 §1.3 and the old METHODS-spinnet §2 said that the sign of $\beta$ is a gauge "at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$, because $K$ is a matching", and that "in the 12-qubit electric-basis encoding this freedom does not exist (M1-P1 rejects $-\beta$)". The second sentence is false and the first gives the wrong reason.

## Corrected statement (now in `docs/METHODS-spinnet.md` §2)
The sign flip of $\beta$ is generated by the centre element $-\mathbb 1\in SU(2)$ acting on **one** inner link $l$. Since $D^j(-U)=(-1)^{2j}D^j(U)$ (Edmonds, *Angular Momentum in Quantum Mechanics*, Ch. 4), the unitary $Z_l=(-1)^{2j_l}$, diagonal in every electric or spin-network basis, maps $U_l\to-U_l$; the plaquette is a product of four link operators, each odd under this map, so
$$Z_l\,\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P\,Z_l=-\mathrm{Tr}\,U_P,\qquad Z_lH_BZ_l=-H_B,\qquad [Z_l,H_E]=[Z_l,G^a_c]=0,$$
hence $U_B(-\beta)=Z_lU_B(\beta)Z_l$ for the single plaquette with static outer links **at every $j_{\max}$**, and $H_E\pm H_B$ are isospectral. Measured by the auditor (audit 100 N1, `audit_center.py`): $\max|Z_lH_BZ_l+H_B|=0.0$ for each of the four inner links, route A and route B.

- Spin-network encoding ($j_{\max}=\tfrac12$): the partner of a bright code flips all four links, so $Z_l$ is $-1$ on exactly one member of each bright pair — one of the "one $-1$ per pair" diagonals $D'$ with $D'KD'=-K$ that M3-K1 and `tests/acceptance/m3/test_spinnet_external.py` already require. The matching argument is a special case, not the reason.
- 12-qubit electric-basis encoding: $Z_l$ is a single Pauli $Z$ on the $j$ qubit of link $l$ ($q_{11}$ for link $l$ on qubits 9–11). `Z(q11)·build_UB(0.61)·Z(q11)` is accepted against $U_B(-0.61)$ with error $2.4\cdot10^{-13}$ and rejected against $U_B(+0.61)$ (error $1.88$) (audit 100 N1, `audit_twelve_center.py`). So the freedom exists there too.
- Consequence for the checks: every "$-\beta$ rejected" result (M1-P1, M3-S1, the §8.1 negative control) pins the **codec's** sign convention — Paulo's Eq. 31 for the 12-qubit codec, the route-B choice (audit 001 N5) for the spin-network codec — and is not a physics check of the sign of the coupling. prompts/100 §1.3(ii) "(not a physics check; in M1 it was one)" is to be read as "(not a physics check, in M1 either)".
- Paulo's 15-qubit file: the recorded gauge (`gauge_minus_codes` $=\{8,\dots,15\}$) is exactly the bright codes with $j_l=\tfrac12$ (bit 3, file `q[3]`), i.e. $(-1)^{2j_l}$ on the left link; it is $\beta$-independent (same minus codes at $\alpha_P\in\{0.1,0.37,0.9,1.7,3.0\}$, residual $\le3.0\cdot10^{-15}$; audit 100 §2.2). In route-B signs the file equals $U_B(-\beta)=e^{+i\beta H_B}$ with no gauge. At fixed codec this cannot be told apart from an overall sign of $\alpha_P$ in the file, and it changes no expectation value of a real observable in a real initial state ($e^{+i\beta H}=(e^{-i\beta H})^*$ for real $H$).
