## 1. Diagnosis of the open items (what this plan settles)

The cap (prompts/005, 1 % of `tol_err`, Digonto's decision, implemented in laptop code) **stays in force unchanged**: a rishon-specific cap would need a laptop keyword (`evaluate(prune_fraction=…)`, diff in §9) and Digonto's yes (§8 D2). No code of ours changes, loosens or bypasses the rule.
2. **Phase rule of the outer-row certificate (prompts/202 §1 vs code).** The anchored phase $\varphi_\ell=\arg\big(\overline{(T_\ell)_{aa}}(V_\ell)_{aa}\big)$ at the type-$A$ code (METHODS-rishon R9 last bullet) **is the convention**; the "fitted" wording of prompts/202 §1 is superseded by this paragraph. ## 2. Method (equations, conventions; sources in research 202 and METHODS-rishon R10–R12)

**Task.** $U=U_B(\beta)U_E(\alpha)$ (electric layer first), $U_E=e^{-i\alpha H_E^{\rm in}}$, $U_B=e^{-i\beta H_B^{\rm in}}$ on the inner codes (R4), nominal $(\alpha,\beta)=(0.37,0.61)$, alternates $(0.91,0.23)$, $(1.70,1.00)$ with an identical gate skeleton (METHODS §2; prompts/001 §4). Validity, all required (prompts/001 §4, instantiated): allowed gates only (`BASIS` ∪ `MACROS` of prompts/002 §2.10, class identity); peak qubits ≤ 16 + 32 + 32 = 80; exact on all 1296 inner codes — hence on all 38 432 physical states by the inner-block soundness theorem (R6 item 1) — at the three angle pairs: `op_norm_error` = block error + leak amplitude + certified pruning bound ≤ $10^{-10}$ with `dropped_total` ≤ $10^{-12}$ (prompts/004, 005); Gauss residual on the 273 sampled physical states (17 closed + 256 seeded, seed 20261104) ≤ $10^{-10}$ (R6 item 3); no outer qubit read or written; every ancilla returned to $|0\rangle$; one global phase; verdict `certified` (sparse engine); evaluation wall ≤ 300 s at the nominal pair and ≤ 120 s per alternate pair. Score: key $(\mathrm{CX},T_{10^{-6}},\mathrm{depth}_{\rm CT},\mathrm{qubits})$ lexicographic, $T_\varepsilon=T_{\rm fixed}+\sum_rT_r(\varepsilon)$ with pygridsynth (METHODS §7), MAP-Elites cells $(\lfloor8\log_2(\mathrm{CX}+1)\rfloor,\lfloor8\log_2(T_\varepsilon+1)\rfloor)$; invalid ⇒ ∞. The round's global best is additionally re-verified after Clifford+T synthesis at $\varepsilon=10^{-6}$ with `synth_tolerances` (prompts/003), as the laptop does.

**Trial mode** (seconds, for the proposers' iteration): 64 seeded inner codes (`trial_seed` 0), nominal angles, sparse engine, no Gauss sample, T estimate $3\log_2(1/\varepsilon)$ per rotation. **Full mode**: all 1296 codes, three angle pairs, Gauss sample, pygridsynth. Validity is decided in full mode only (archive rule `mode == "full"`).

**Rounds** (prompts/001 §4): round 0 = seeds (§2.4 of research 202: general, general + polish, Qiskit L3 windowed, general-rccx, general-mcx; external sources recorded as reasons); ideation = one proposer, spec only, ≤ 5 families → `compress/ideation/rishon.md`; rounds 1…R: one proposer per active family (≤ 5 in rounds 1–2, best 3 after; a family with no valid program after round 2 is dropped), parent = family elite, migration every third round; stop at R = 8, or after 3 consecutive rounds without a better global key, or at 20 proposer sessions (ideation counts). Coordinator per round: briefs → proposers (background, concurrent) → `round --evaluate` → tester verification → commit `compress rishon round rrr (prompts/203, reports/tests/NNN)`.

**Lean** (prompts/006 trust levels): structure facts for the spec from M5/M5x (kernel: counts 38 432 / 4352 / 1296, JW string-freeness, corner kernels, $U_E$ semantics; native: $H_B$ bright pairs); per-circuit certificates through a track-owned three-variable Laurent checker (research 202 §4): $z_\alpha=e^{i\alpha/d_\alpha}$, $z_\beta=e^{i\beta/d_\beta}$, $z_\gamma=e^{i\sqrt2\beta/d_\gamma}$, gate angle $=m_\alpha\theta_\alpha+m_\beta\theta_\beta+m_\gamma\theta_\gamma$; accept iff for every inner code $c$: $U'|c\rangle=k\,z^{\mathbf m}\sum_{c'}(V_{\rm in})_{c'c}|c'\rangle$ exactly over $K[z^{\pm1}]$ with ancillas 0 and no weight outside the code space (prompts/006 §1 M4 with three variables). Status labels: `certified` (native), `rejected`, `n_a` (reasons `non_linear_angle:<gate>:<theta>` — not of the form above with denominators ≤ 64 —, `unitary_gate`, `phase_not_in_K`, `too_many_qubits`), `failed`, `not_run`. ## 4. Task-specification hand text (`compress/specs/rishon-step.md`, rendered by `render_spec`; numbers filled from the spec, the encoding, `MACRO_COST`, `structure_facts()`; nothing typed in the renderer)
> **Task.** Implement $U=U_B(\beta)\,U_E(\alpha)$ (electric layer first in the circuit) for the SU(2) **quantum link model** (two fermionic rishons per link, 6 link states; Report 5, `docs/METHODS-rishon.md`) on the plaquette with 8 outer links, in the 48-qubit Jordan–Wigner encoding, exactly (up to one global phase) on every one of the 1296 valid inner codes — which certifies all 38 432 physical states (METHODS-rishon R6) — at $(\alpha,\beta)=(0.37,0.61)$ and, with the same gate skeleton, at $(0.91,0.23)$ and $(1.70,1.00)$. > **Circuit model.** As `twelve`: gates `x z h s sdg t tdg rz p cx cz`, macros `ccx rccx ccz swap ch crz crx cp` with costs (CX, T, rotations) from `MACRO_COST`; `Builder.alloc/free`, `Builder.mcx` (≥ 3 controls: (k−2) rccx + ccx + (k−2) rccx); at most 80 qubits; no measurement; emission-time classical computation allowed (`su2plaq.compress.oracle`, `su2plaq.compress.spec.get_encoding("rishon")`, numpy).

> **Lean.** Rotation angles of the form $(m_\alpha\alpha+m_\beta\beta+m_\gamma\sqrt2\beta)/d$, $d\le64$, make the circuit certifiable for all angles by the rishon three-angle checker (status `certified`); other emission-time angles give `n_a`. Informative only.

## 11. Risks and stop conditions
- **12 000-line seed programs.** The general step as a Builder program is ~4 600 lines (one macro per line); the worker's 120 s build limit is far (measured build of the M1 program: seconds). If `load_program` is slow, the builder emits loops in `program_source`? No — `program_source` is laptop code; accept the long file (it is generated once, immutable).
- **Round wall.** 5 programs × (nominal ≤ 300 s + 2 × ≤ 120 s) + synthesis of the best (≈ 300 s for a general-sized circuit, far less for a compressed one) + Lean ≤ 1200 s ≈ ≤ 70 min worst, typically 10–15 min.
- **Prune cap decides an archive candidate** (block error + leak ≤ `tol_err` but `dropped_total` > cap): record, keep the rule, raise D2 with the numbers; never loosen in the loop.
- **Soundness contradiction**: a circuit `ok` on the inner block but `gauss` > tolerance on the sample, with no bug found → **stop and ask Digonto** (contradicts R6 item 1).
- **Lean 3-angle checker** cannot be finished (two fixer NOT FIXED) → planner downgrade to `n_a` with a plan addendum (D4); rounds continue meanwhile.
- **Certificate REJECT on a numerically valid circuit** with the semantics test green → stop and ask (exact vs float disagreement, prompts/006 §10).
- **Proposer budget exhausted without improvement** over the general circuit → report the round-0 best as the result; that is a valid outcome.
- **Stop and ask Digonto** also when: any locked tolerance is believed wrong; the tester's second engine cannot re-verify an archived circuit; the loop needs outer reads to make progress (would reopen §1.6).

Reading. The bound is rounding noise ($\sim10^{-16}$ residues in branches opened by $H$-type gates, dropped and tallied, Frobenius over 1296 columns), $5\times10^{-16}$–$1.1\times10^{-15}$ **per branching gate of the engine's lowering**, independent of how the circuit is written; `ccx` macros are free of it because the engine executes them as permutations. The "per ccx / per rccx" rates of research 202 §3, METHODS-rishon R11 (first text) and `spec.PRUNE_RATES` are a misattribution: the general circuit's $3\times10^{-13}$ comes from its 600 frame gates (34 h + 202 ch + 81 crx), not from the 1402 ccx. At the cap $10^{-12}$ the budget is **≈ 900 (worst measured rate) to 1 900 branching gates** on this task: any number of macro `ccx`/`ccz`, but at most ≈ 450–950 `rccx` or bare Toffolis (2 h each), ≈ 450–950 `ch`/`crx` (2 each), ≈ 900–1 900 bare `h`/`sx`.

**Decisions.**
(a) **S3 fix (test, patch).** `qiskit_l3` joins `general-rccx`/`general-mcx` in the "recorded as judged" clause: reason ∈ {`ok`, `pruning_bound`}, `valid == (reason == "ok")`, and a rejected row must show block error + leak ≤ $10^{-10}$ with `dropped_total` > $10^{-12}$ (its true reason, never another one); `general` and `general+polish` (macro circuits, measured ok) must still be `ok`; the best key is still the minimum over the valid rows. What S3 protects — round 0 recorded honestly, every seed row present with the evaluator's reason — is unchanged; the test no longer asserts a validity that the rule in force does not grant. K2's check of the `prune_rates` fact is updated to the new shape below (keys, not numbers).
(b) **Is the cap the right validity rule here, and is D2 blocking?** Plainly: under the 1 % rule every *exact* circuit with more than ≈ 900–1 900 branching gates is invalid on this task because of simulator rounding, not physics. That excludes (i) every bare-gate form of the uncompressed circuit, hence the honest L3 baseline row, (ii) the two cheaper exact ladder styles (−32 % CX), (iii) mid-size designs (≈ 500–1 000 `rccx` or bare Toffolis) that a proposer might pass through on the way down. It does **not** exclude the loop's targets: a compressed circuit at the scale of the twelve result (111 CX, ≈ 30 h) sits two orders of magnitude below the cap, and macro `ccx` ladders of any depth are free. So the loop can proceed and D2 is not blocking for the compression goal; it is blocking for a fair round-0 baseline and for the mid-size part of the search space. **Default in force (no code of ours changes the rule): keep 1 %**, record every `pruning_bound` with its block error (Report 6 §3.3, §5), and proposers budget with the numbers above. **Recommendation to Digonto (D2): set the cap to 10 % of `tol_err` for this encoding** (cap $10^{-11}$, budget ≈ 9 000–19 000 branching gates — every circuit up to the uncompressed size passes), implemented by the laptop keyword of §9 item 3 and, until it merges, by a one-line constant in our `rishon.compress.evaluate` that re-classifies a laptop `pruning_bound` verdict as `ok` only when `dropped_total` ≤ $0.1\,$`tol_err` **and** the full budget `op_norm_error` ≤ `tol_err` still holds (the certified bound stays rigorous: the cap only limits the simulator's share of the budget, prompts/005 §3). This re-classification is *not* applied without Digonto's yes; if he says yes, round 0 is re-run (the archive is reset; the seeds `general-rccx`, L3 become valid rows and the round-0 best becomes the 6 866-CX circuit).
(c) **Spec/brief wording (builder, step 6.4 follow-up).** `spec.PRUNE_RATES` becomes one measured rate per branching gate with provenance, and the structure fact `prune_rates` takes the shape `{"per_branching_gate": 1.1e-15, "budget_at_cap": floor(cap / rate) = 909, "branching": ["h", "sx", "u", "ch (2)", "crx (2)", "rccx (2)", "crz (0)", "cp (0)"], "engine_exact": ["x", "z", "s", "sdg", "t", "tdg", "p", "rz", "cx", "cz", "cp", "swap", "ccx", "ccz"], "provenance": "prompts/203 §13 E2 table"}` (the worst measured rate, $1.1\times10^{-15}$, is the conservative one). `prune_budget(spec)` returns that single budget (floor, not two-significant-digit rounding). The spec's **Correctness** paragraph replaces its "per ccx / per rccx … 4 500 ccx or 700 rccx" sentence by: "measured on this task the engine's certified pruning bound grows by about $1.1\times10^{-15}$ per branching gate of its lowering (h, sx, u; ch, crx and rccx count 2; macro ccx/ccz and all permutation/phase gates count 0), so the cap allows about 900 branching gates; exact circuits above that are rejected as `pruning_bound` — the uncompressed circuit in bare h/t form, its rccx-ladder variants and its Qiskit L3 transpile all are." The brief's pointer line adds "prune budget: see Correctness". (K2 asserts only the keys and the floor identity, not the rate.)
