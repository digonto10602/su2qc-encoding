## 1. Method

### 1.1 Task (METHODS-lsh L12)
Unit of compression: the first-order step $U=U_B(\beta)\,U_E(\alpha)$ (electric layer first) on the 24-qubit LSH register, $U_E=e^{-i\alpha H_E^{\rm inner}}$ with $H_E^{\rm inner}=\tfrac34\sum_cN_n(c)$, $U_B=e^{-i\beta K_{\rm LSH}}$ on the 47 inner codes (L4, L5), identity structure in the outer-site pattern. Validity (shared evaluator, full mode): exact up to one global phase on all 433 physical codes at $(\alpha,\beta)=(0.37,0.61)$ and with the same gate skeleton at $(0.91,0.23)$, $(1.70,1.00)$: `total_error` $=$ block error $+$ pruning bound $+$ leak amplitude $+$ span leak $\le10^{-10}$ (`prompts/004`, `008` §5); Gauss residual $\le10^{-10}$ (a final state that breaks the Abelian Gauss law or the flux cap is an error, L6); ancillas clean; outer qubits never written; gates `x z h s sdg t tdg rz p cx cz` + macros `ccx rccx ccz swap ch crz crx cp`, `Builder.mcx` for $\ge3$ controls; at most $12+12+32=56$ qubits; wall $\le300$ s. The numerical evaluator is validity; Lean never changes a verdict. The proposer may recode the sites or not.

### 1.2 What the loop can gain beyond spin-network M4 (answer to audit 303 N7)
With the (inner | outer) pairing the LSH loop basis is the spin-network basis in another bit code, so the round-0 best is known in advance: the M4 headline `r02-F1-002` (31 CX) wrapped by the transcoder, **71 CX** (measured key $(71,1118,2276,25)$, robust key $(71,22480,0,46384,25)$, Lean-certified by the existing LSH checker in 1.3 s). The loop therefore does not re-run M4; it measures **how much of the transcoder cost ($+40$ CX, $+32$ T $=$ 16 CX linear $+$ four relative-phase Toffoli pairs) is intrinsic to the LSH register**. Report 8 states three numbers: 31 (spin network), 71 (wrapped), $X$ (LSH headline), with `native_gain_cx` $=71-X$ and `overhead_cx` $=X-31$. No numeric target is locked (none is derivable: a CNOT-only recoding is impossible, a relative-phase Toffoli needs 3 CX [arXiv:quant-ph/0312225], and no lower bound better than the spin-network optimum is known). $X=71$ is a valid result and must be reported as "the recoding cost was not reduced".
Levers that exist only in LSH variables, kernel-checked and given to the proposers as facts (L13; research 401 §3): dark $\Leftrightarrow$ some site has $\ell_{nv}\wedge\ell_{vp}$; on bright codes the coupling is a function of $\sum_c\ell_{nv}(c)\in\{0,1,2\}$ alone ($k=-2,+1,-\tfrac12$), and the parity of the four $\ell_{nv}$ bits is move-invariant (the spin-network flag $[u{=}v{=}0]$, a Toffoli pair there, equals a parity here on bright codes); every inner link bit is readable at two sites; the move flips $\ell_{pn}$ where $w=0$ and $\ell_{nv},\ell_{vp}$ where $w=1$. They are facts, not a design: no construction below 71 CX was found by the planner.
Target decision: **the full LSH-register step, transcoder included** (not "a native walk" as a separate task): one task, two kinds of families (§1.7).

### 1.6 Stop rule (METHODS-lsh L14)
After every evaluated round $r$ the headline record $R_r=\{\texttt{id},\mathrm{CX},\sum T,\rho,\mathrm{qubits},N_{\rm rot},T_{\rm fixed}\}$ is appended to `results/compress/lsh/stop.json`. $R$ **improves** on the last improving record $R'$ iff
$$(\mathrm{CX},\textstyle\sum T,\rho,\mathrm{qubits})_R<(\mathrm{CX},\textstyle\sum T,\rho,\mathrm{qubits})_{R'}\ \text{lexicographically},$$
except when $\mathrm{CX}$, $N_{\rm rot}$ and $T_{\rm fixed}$ are all equal and $|\sum T_R-\sum T_{R'}|\le20\,N_{\rm rot}$: then $\sum T$ is a tie and $(\rho,\mathrm{qubits})$ decide. The depth component of the robust key is not used (audit 302 §7.7: $\le0.6\,\%$ synthesis-word noise decided a round in M4). The band: at fixed structure $\sum T$ moves only through the Ross–Selinger fluctuation of each rotation, of which tuning can extract $\lesssim1$ T per rotation and pair at 20 pairs (`prompts/104` §11 E3). The loop **stops** after round 8 (`max_rounds`), at 20 proposer sessions with ideation counted as one (`max_sessions`), or after 3 consecutive rounds without improvement (`no_gain`); round 0 always improves. A replay is not a round. The coordinator does not stop earlier by judgement (M8-G3 requires a recorded stop reason).

`lsh.loop.BRIEF_NOTE` (returned by the hook `brief_note`, appended by the shared brief code to the ideation brief and to every round brief; the builder copies this text):
> **LSH note (prompts/401).** The target is the step on the full 24-qubit LSH register (12 data loop-number qubits, 12 read-only outer qubits). You are free to recode the sites or not: nothing requires the spin-network code to appear. For orientation: the cheapest known circuit is a spin-network circuit between the per-site transcoder (`cx`, `cx`, `rccx`) and its inverse; the transcoder pair alone costs 40 CX and 32 T, of which 24 CX and 32 T are four relative-phase Toffoli pairs. This loop asks how much of that is needed. Families are of two kinds: *transcoding* (a spin-network-style walk after recoding all four sites; gains come from merging or thinning the recoding) and *native* (work on the raw loop-number bits and never hold the complete spin-network code of all four sites). An ideation file must contain at least two native families. Facts you may rely on (kernel-checked, see the structure file): a code is dark iff some site has `l_nv = l_vp = 1`; on bright codes the coupling depends only on the number of set `l_nv` bits (0, 1, 2 give k = -2, +1, -1/2) and the parity of the four `l_nv` bits is unchanged by the plaquette move; every inner link bit can be read at two sites (Abelian Gauss law: `l_pn xor l_nv` at one site equals `l_pn xor l_vp` at the next); the move flips `l_pn` where `w = l_nv xor l_vp` is 0, and `l_nv`, `l_vp` where `w` is 1. A final state that breaks the Abelian Gauss law or the flux cap on a physical input makes the program invalid; intermediate states are unrestricted. Outer qubits may be used as controls only.

The round briefs also carry the shared headline paragraph (`robust.HEADLINE_RULE_SECTION`). `lsh.loop.render_spec(spec)` = the shared default text + a section "LSH register and rules" (layout `d[3c..3c+2] = (l_pn, l_nv, l_vp)`, `o[12..23]` read-only, valid site codes, the Abelian Gauss law, the target in LSH variables, the electric phase) + "Proved structure (Lean)" from `structure_facts()` + the headline section before "Tools"; it names no seed and no spin-network entry (M8-S0).

## 9. Risks and stop conditions
- **Stop and ask Digonto** (project rules): a locked test or tolerance is believed wrong; a result contradicts established physics and no bug is found; this plan has escalated back twice; pushing, deleting data or history.
