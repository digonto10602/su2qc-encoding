## 1. Method (equations; conventions as `docs/METHODS.md` §2, §6, §7, §9, §11 and `docs/METHODS-spinnet.md` §1–§6; new text in `docs/METHODS-spinnet.md` §10)

**Task (spec hand-text, instantiated by `TaskSpec.render()` + our post-processing).** Implement $U=U_B(\beta)\,U_E(\alpha)$ (electric layer first; order 1) on the 16-qubit spin-network register, exactly up to one global phase on all 433 physical states (computational-basis codes $y+256\,o$: 8 data bits $=4$ link bits $j_l$ + 4 corner bits $J_c$, 8 outer bits read-only), at $(\alpha,\beta)=(0.37,0.61)$ and, with the same skeleton, at $(0.91,0.23)$ and $(1.70,1.00)$; ancillas clean. $U_E=\prod_{l}p(-\tfrac34\alpha)_{j_l}$; $U_B=e^{-i\beta K}$ on the 47 admissible inner codes, $K$ = 8 bright pairs $y\leftrightarrow y\oplus0b1111$ (corner bits 0) with coupling $k=-2$ ($0000\leftrightarrow1111$), $-\tfrac12$ ($0101\leftrightarrow1010$), $+1$ (six others), and $K=0$ on the 31 dark codes (any corner bit set). Route-B sign gauge of METHODS-spinnet §2 (the $-\beta$ convention is rejected by the evaluator, M3-S1). Correctness, cost and validity rules are the laptop's (`TaskSpec` defaults): `op_norm_error` $\le10^{-10}$ (block error + leak amplitude + certified pruning bound), Gauss $\le10^{-10}$, allowed gates `x z h s sdg t tdg rz p cx cz` + macros `ccx rccx ccz swap ch crz crx cp`, `Builder.mcx` for $\ge3$ controls, $\le48$ qubits, wall $\le300$ s, $\le5\cdot10^6$ amplitudes, pruning bound $\le10^{-2}$ tol, synthesised re-verification at $N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon+10^{-9}$. Key $(\mathrm{CX},T_{10^{-6}},\mathrm{depth}_{\rm CT},\mathrm{qubits})$ lexicographic, $T_\varepsilon=T_{\rm fixed}+\sum_rT_r(\varepsilon)$ by pygridsynth; cells $(\lfloor8\log_2(\mathrm{CX}+1)\rfloor,\lfloor8\log_2(T_\varepsilon+1)\rfloor)$. **The numerical evaluator is validity; Lean never changes a verdict.**
