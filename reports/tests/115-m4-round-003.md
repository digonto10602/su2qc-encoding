## Notes (no failures)

- **N1 (info, the shifts and the Lean linearity rule).** Both F1 and F2 add constants $c=\pi/4$ (and $5\pi/4$) to affine angles. Valid for the evaluator and the spec (angles still computed from `spec.alpha`, `spec.beta`; the shift is a Clifford+T-equivalent identity, checked above to $10^{-15}$). The Lean certificate format needs a rational multiple of $\alpha$ or $\beta$, so the shifted angle breaks it: `n_a`. F3's $\pm\pi/2$ offsets do the same (as in report 114).
- **N2 (important for the final report, not a test failure).** The nominal-angle T gain is bought at the price of the Lean guarantee: `r02-F1-002` $(31,1086,2258,17)$ is certified, `r03-F1-003` $(31,1062,2189,17)$ is `n_a`.
