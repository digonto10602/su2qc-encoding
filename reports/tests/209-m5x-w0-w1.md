## Statement review (HBLink.lean)
- `hb_link_ops_from_jw`: for all $i,j<2$: list $2i+j$ of `linkOpsData` equals (as a set, same length, mutual containment) `sectorEntries (Uop i j)`; list $4+2i+j$ equals `sectorEntries (Udop i j)`; list $4+2i+j$ equals the transpose `(e.2.1, e.1, e.2.2)` of list $2i+j$; and `Uop i j`, `Udop i j` keep the six-state sector (no leakage, so the restriction loses nothing). `Udop i j` is defined in this file as $c_{R,j}c^\dagger_{L,i}$ (the adjoint of $\mathrm{Uop}\,i\,j = c_{L,i}c^\dagger_{R,j}$). Matches plan section 2 and the acceptance docstring. The set-equality check is sound because the JW side has no duplicate entries; the duplicate-with-equal-length mutant is rejected.
- Genuinely about the actual data: `linkOpsData := rishonHBCert.linkOps` verbatim (identical to the test's required string), where `rishonHBCert` is the generated `HBData.lean` value; the JW side is `JW.lean`'s `Uop`/`mode`/`comp`, not a copy. The row/column convention (row = image, column = source) agrees with the test's own numpy JW matrices.
- `hb_link_ops_count`: `linkOpsData.length = 8`. `det_u_identity`: `OpEq 4 (U00 U11 + (-1) U01 U10) detU` with `detU = 2 U00 U11`, over all $2^4=16$ link basis states (BCW Eq. 4.6). `u_commute`: all four-fold bounded quantifier $\forall i,j,k,l<2$ of `OpEq 4 (U_ij U_kl) (U_kl U_ij)`.
- Axioms: no `native_decide`; all `decide +kernel`; `hb_link_ops_from_jw` depends on `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` (not on the native axiom of `HB.lean`, although `HBData` imports it).
