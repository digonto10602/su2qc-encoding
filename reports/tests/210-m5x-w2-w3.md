## 4. Statement review

**W2 `corner_kernel_dims`.** For every end configuration $m\in\{0,\dots,80\}$ the theorem asserts (a) the three operators $2G^z=\sum_e(n_{e,1}-n_{e,2})$, $G^+=\sum_e c^\dagger_{e,1}c_{e,2}$, $G^-=\sum_e c^\dagger_{e,2}c_{e,1}$, built from `JW.lean`'s `mode`/`comp` on 8 modes, map the subspace spanned by `cornerBasis m` into itself, and (b) $D-\operatorname{rank}$ of the stacked $3D\times D$ integer matrix equals `cornerCountR`. The rank is exact integer arithmetic: column sweep with the fraction-free row update $r\leftarrow p\,r-a\,r_{\rm piv}$ (no division, `Int`), whose rank equals the rank over $\mathbb Q$ (each step multiplies the non-pivot rows by the non-zero $p$). It is computed on the corner subspace of fixed end configuration, which is the right space: all three generators conserve the occupation of each end, so the joint kernel on the 256-dimensional space is the direct sum over $m$. The joint kernel of $(G^z,G^\pm)$ is the singlet space. Limits worth stating (not defects): `cornerCountR` is a copy of `cornerCount`; their equality is W4 (`corner_count_agree`, not built yet); the tie to the production `physical.corner_kernel` is the locked Python W2 test.

**Finding (equivalent mutants, not a gap).** Stacking $(2G^z,G^+,G^-)$ is redundant: for a finite-dimensional $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ representation any two of the three have the same joint kernel as all three ($G^-v=0$ with weight 0 means a lowest-weight vector of weight 0, i.e. spin 0; $[G^+,G^-]=2G^z$ makes $G^z$ follow from $G^\pm$). Consequently dropping one generator, or duplicating $G^+$ as $G^-$, or swapping $G^+\leftrightarrow G^-$, cannot be detected by any dimension count; I confirmed independently (numpy rank, 81 configurations) that $\{G^+,G^-\}$, $\{G^z,G^-\}$, $\{G^z,G^+\}$ give the identical 81 dimensions (sum 42), while $\{G^+\}$ alone gives sum 126 and $\{G^z\}$ alone sum 70.

**W3 `ue_exact_all_codes` and companions.** `runGates ueGates [(y,one)] == [(y, [(-3*nQ y,1,0)])]` for all 1296 codes $y=a+16b+256c+4096d$, $a,b,c,d\in$ `sector`: the output is a single basis vector equal to the input code (clean: nothing leaves the computational basis state), with coefficient the Laurent monomial $z^{-3\#Q}$ with Gaussian-integer coefficient exactly $1$. Since $z=e^{i\alpha/4}$ is an indeterminate, this is the identity for all $\alpha$ at once; the Python side supplies "$\alpha$-independence of the literal" (locked test) and the integrality of the angles (`ue_gate_ints` raises on a non-integer multiple of $\alpha/4$). `ue_gadget_sector` proves the parity trick (phase on exactly the quartet $\{5,6,9,10\}$) rather than assuming it. `ue_gates_structure`/`ue_outer_gates_structure` pin the rendered lists to 4 resp. 12 gadgets in order. Limit: the 48-qubit outer list gets only structural content in Lean (that it is 12 consecutive gadgets); its semantics follow from `ue_gadget_sector` plus the tensor-product argument, which is the planned O1 numerical certificate, not a Lean theorem. Real content: the 12-gate and 36-gate literals are produced by `write_lean_ue_gates` from the Qiskit circuits (reproduced byte-identically).

**Stub list.** `tests/unit/test_rishon_stubs_unit.py` loses exactly the six names `ue_gate_ints, ue_gate_literal, write_lean_ue_gates, lean_eval, lean_ue_gate_images, lean_corner_kernel_dims` from the still-raises list (and their ARGS); all six are now implemented and exercised by the locked W2/W3 tests and by the new unit tests. `outer_electric_certify` (O1) stays listed. The W2 dimension test cannot distinguish the redundant generator subsets; this is representation theory, not a test weakness, but a reader of the theorem should know that "$(2G^z,G^+,G^-)$" is more than needed. The `invariant` clause is the only thing that would flag a generator leaving the configuration subspace.
- N2. The locked W2 test is the only guard against a shrunk configuration range (`List.range 80`); Lean alone accepts it.
- N3. Lean covers `ueOuterGates` only structurally; its phase semantics rest on the (planned) numerical O1 certificate.
- N4. `cornerCountR` = `cornerCount` is not yet proved (W4 Bridge).
