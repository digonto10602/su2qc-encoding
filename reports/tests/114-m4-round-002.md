#### F3's $\pm\pi/2$ electric angles: valid under the spec's rules?

F3 builds the four U_E phases as `p(lam ± π/2)` with $\lambda=-\tfrac34\alpha$. What the rules say:

- Spec (rendered in `TaskSpec`): the skeleton (gate names and qubits) must be identical at the three angle pairs; angles may differ; "angles must be computed from the spec's `alpha`/`beta` (and the oracle), never typed in". F3's angles are computed from `spec.alpha`/`spec.beta`; the added $\pm\pi/2$ is a Clifford offset ($S$ or $S^\dagger$ worth), not a typed answer. A circuit that is exact at three pairs with an affine angle is exact at all pairs (an affine map on $\mathbb R^2$ is fixed by three non-collinear points), and the 8-pair fit above confirms the affine form.
- Evaluator: valid in mode `full`, skeleton equal, errors $6.8,6.5,7.4\times10^{-15}$ (entry) and $2\times10^{-15}$ (mine).
- Lean (METHODS §11.2, prompts/006 §8.2, plan row 4.7 default): a certificate needs every angle a rational multiple of $\alpha$ or $\beta$; an offset of $\pi/2$ breaks this, so `export_certificate` returns `non_linear_angle:p:1.2932963267948967` $(=-\tfrac34\cdot0.37+\pi/2)$ (my run, `lean_others.out`). The plan's default is: report `n_a`, do not reject.
- F3 is not the global best (key $(32,\dots)$ against F1's $(31,\dots)$), so no certificate is required of it this round. If F3's trick (or the same trick in a future best) wins, the final best would be `n_a` for Lean, which row 4.7/L5 accepts (`n_a` with reason starting `non_linear_angle`). F1's angles are all linear ($c=0$), so the current best stays certified.

Verdict: F3 is valid; its Lean status would be `n_a` (info N1).
