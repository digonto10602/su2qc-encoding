## Results, part 2: the claim "no exact walk of $\le 21$ toggles in the 64-point space"

Claim (quoted from the task): in the 64-point space $(p,y_1,y_2,y_3,e,l_{2v}(0))$ of the F3 fold frame, generators $p,y_1,y_2,y_3,e,l_{2v}(0),l_{v1}(0)=l_{2v}(0)\oplus e$, both walk ends free in $\{0,e\}$, no exact walk of $\le21$ toggles exists. Notation: point $x\in\mathbb{F}_2^6$ with bits $p{=}1,y_1{=}2,y_2{=}4,y_3{=}8,e{=}16,a{=}32$ where $a=l_{2v}(0)$; a label is a physical state's value $L\in\mathbb{F}_2^6$; the walk visits a set $U$ of points; it is exact iff the target $g$ on the 37 labels lies in $\mathrm{span}\{\chi_x:x\in U\}$, $\chi_x(L)=(-1)^{x\cdot L}$.

### (b) The 37 labels and the target $2K$, re-derived (own model)

### (a) Reading of `x.c`: are its reductions sound?
My judgement after reading and testing each step (all statements are about the model above).

1. **Every walk visits all 16 classes of $\mathbb{F}_2^6/\langle e,\,p{+}y_1{+}y_2{+}y_3{+}a\rangle$: SOUND.** $H=\{L:e=0,\,p{+}y_1{+}y_2{+}y_3{+}a=0\}$ has 16 elements, all are among the 37 labels, and $g|_H=-4\delta_0$ (`class_claim.py`). The characters of $H$ are the cosets of $H^\perp=\{0,16,47,63\}$ (16 cosets of 4 points); $-4\delta_0$ has all 16 Fourier coefficients equal to $-\tfrac14\ne0$, so every coset must contain a visited point. Direct confirmation with exact rational arithmetic (sympy): for each of the 16 classes, $g\notin\mathrm{span}\{\chi_x:x\notin\text{class}\}$ on the 37 labels (16 of 16), and $g\in\mathrm{span}$ of all 64 characters.
2. **Closed Q5 shadow, $m$ even $\in\{16,18,20\}$, plus at most $21-m$ e-moves: SOUND.** Dropping $e$ maps generators 1,2,4,8 and 32,48 to Q5 moves and 16 to a null move; ends in $\{0,16\}$ project to 0, so the Q5 walk is closed at 0 and (bipartite) has even length $m$; classes map to antipodal Q5 pairs (checked), 16 classes need 16 distinct visited points after the start, so $m\ge16$; $m+\#e\le21$ gives $m\le20$. The "extension of shorter walks to exactly 21" is not needed: the search budget is "$\le$" everywhere (`r=LT-d`, `K=LT-m`, "used $\le K$").
3. **Class-cover DP bound `CD[v][M]`: SOUND** (minimum, over orders of first visits of the uncovered classes, of the Hamming distances plus the return to 0; any walk's first-visit order gives a path at least that long; standing on a vertex covers its class for free via `Mp`; ordering of the table fill is valid). Tested: a version with a much weaker bound (number of uncovered classes) gives identical leaf counts, see (d).
4. **Superset feasibility pruning (phase 1: points reachable and returnable within the remaining budget; phase 2: all future points with both $e$ values): SOUND** (visited set $\subseteq$ current set $\cup$ superset; feasibility is monotone in the point set). Tested by removing it, see (d).
5. **E-blind label test on the Q5 projection: SOUND.** For labels with $e=0$ (23 of 37), $\chi_x(L)$ does not depend on the $e$-bit of $x$, so $g_0\in\mathrm{span}\{\chi_{\pi(x)}\}$ is necessary. Tested independently on sampled leaves, see (d).
6. **E-lifts (phase 2): SOUND.** Per position none / flip (cost 1, visits both $e$ values) / spur (cost 2, visits both, $e$ unchanged); $\ge3$ moves at one position visit the same two points as 1 or 2 moves, so they are dominated; at the last position the spur is skipped (the flip visits the same points, cost 1, and the end $e$ is free); each a-move may carry an $e$ flip (generator 48). Tested against a brute force that does NOT assume domination, see (d).
7. **Free ends in $\{0,e\}$ and generators:** by construction of the model; free toggle $e$ (merged as `cz` into the collapse `cx(0,9)`), checked by the merge identity above. This is a scope statement, see below.
8. **Splitting** `((k0*5+k1)*5+k2) % 7` at depth 3 partitions all walks (7 residues cover all 125 triples); the 7 splits reproduced the proposer's logs digit for digit.

Minor remarks: feasibility in phase 1 is floating point (Gram-Schmidt, tolerances $10^{-7}$ / $10^{-10}$); the exact mod-$p$ cross-check exists only in `x2.c` under `-DXCHECK` (I reran it, below). `x3.c` is `x2.c` with a PREFIX option (its `mismatches=0` printout is trivially 0 because it is built without `-DXCHECK`).
