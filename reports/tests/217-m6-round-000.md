Second engines (neither imports `su2plaq.evaluate`):

1. **My own sparse engine** (`tester-217-engine.py`, numpy only): every gate reduced to (controls, target, 2x2 matrix) from Qiskit's own `base_gate` matrices, or Qiskit's `definition` for `rccx`; diagonal gates multiply amplitudes, X-type gates flip keys, general matrices split and merge; pruning is absolute ($|a|<10^{-15}$) with my own bookkeeping of the dropped weight. All 1296 inner codes in one batch, three angle pairs.
2. **Targets independent of `operators.inner_target`**: $W=\sum_{abcd}U_b{}_{ab}U_r{}_{bc}(U_t^\dagger)_{cd}(U_l^\dagger)_{da}$ built from `rishon.algebra.RishonLink` with `embed_link_op` on four links (65 536-dim sparse), restricted to the 1296 codes; $H_B=-(W+W^\dagger)$; $H_E=\sum_{\rm links}\tfrac12(L^2+R^2)$ from the algebra Casimirs; $U_B=$ `scipy.linalg.expm`$(-i\beta H_B)$ (dense 1296), $U_E=\mathrm{diag}\,e^{-i\alpha H_E}$, step target $U_BU_E$ (order 1). Cross-checked against the earlier tester's 16-mode global Jordan-Wigner build (`tester-204-mysim.py::own_reference`): codes equal, $\max|H_B-H_B'|=0$, $\max|H_E-H_E'|=0$; $H_B$ spectrum $\pm4\sqrt2\,(1)$, $\pm2\,(48)$, $\pm1\,(32)$, $0\,(1134)$; $H_E\in\{0,\tfrac34,\tfrac32,\tfrac94,3\}$; unitarity of $U_B$ $\le 8.9\times10^{-16}$. Against `operators.inner_target` (side check only): $4.4\times10^{-16}$.
3. **Aer statevector** (`qiskit_aer` 0.17.2, double precision, 1 thread per run, fusion on) on the 16 data qubits + the ancillas that are non-zero for that input, outer register removed (the circuits touch no qubit in 16..47, confirmed by the engine and by G3).

How the qubit budget was handled. The step circuit has 10 work ancillas, so the full inner register needs 26 qubits for the codes that open the longest control ladders. The number of ancillas that are non-zero in some branch depends on the input code (my engine records it per code). Histogram over the 1296 codes (qubits = 16 + ancillas): 18 qubits: 360, 20: 104, 21: 236, 22: 8, 23: 140, 24: 142, 26: 306. **So 990 codes fit in $\le24$ qubits** (they are exactly the 990 dark codes by count; I did not test that identity), and 306 codes need 26 qubits. For a code, qubits never non-zero are removed together with the gates touching them (identity on that input according to my engine). This makes the Aer check conditional on my engine's liveness for those gates. To remove that dependence I also ran 7 codes with 10 live ancillas on the **unreduced 26-qubit circuit** (all 4289 gates).
