Jargon, once: *rishon* = a fermionic constituent of a gauge link in the quantum link model (Hebrew "first", BCW footnote 1); *quantum link model (QLM)* = a lattice gauge theory whose link variables are operators in a finite-dimensional Hilbert space instead of group elements; *Jordan–Wigner (JW)* = the standard map from fermion modes to qubits, $c_k=(\prod_{m<k}Z_m)\,\sigma^-_k$; *singlet* = a state of total colour spin 0; *Casimir* = $\sum_a L_a^2$, the "electric energy" of a link end; *bright pair* = two orthonormal vectors coupled by the magnetic Hamiltonian, so that the Hamiltonian is a $2\times2$ block on them; *dark state* = a state annihilated by the magnetic Hamiltonian.

## 2. Model decision and its reasons

**Decided:** SU(2) QLM in BCW's 6-state representation; SU(2) Gauss law only; $\gamma=0$.

* *SU(2) vs U(2).* With fermionic rishons and exactly $N=2$ rishons per link, the link operators generate SU(4) and both $\det U$ and $\det U^\dagger$ are non-zero (BCW Eq. 4.6). The gauge symmetry of $H_E+H_B$ alone is U(2) (Wiese Eq. 4.11); what makes the theory SU(2) is (i) imposing only the SU(2) Gauss law and (ii) optionally breaking the U(1) with $\gamma(\det U+\det U^\dagger)$. The brief fixes (i). For (ii) we keep $\gamma=0$ in M5: the Trotter step then has the same two layers $U_E(\alpha)$, $U_B(\beta)$ as Kogut–Susskind, which is what the shared-observable comparison needs; the U(1) charges $Q_x=\sum_{\text{ends at }x}n_{\rm end}$ are then conserved by $U_E$ and $U_B$ and label 431 superselection sectors of the physical space (§4). A circuit exact on the whole SU(2)-Gauss space is exact on every sector, in particular on the U(2)-QLM sector $Q_x=4$. The det term is defined in METHODS-rishon ($\det U_\ell=2\,c_{L1}c^\dagger_{R1}c_{L2}c^\dagger_{R2}=2|B\rangle\langle A|$ on the sector; it commutes with $H_E$, so a layer $e^{-i\gamma\sum_\ell(\det U_\ell+{\rm h.c.})}$ is 12 independent $2\times2$ rotations) but no circuit for it is built in M5.
* *Electric operator.* BCW have none; Banerjee et al. add $\tfrac{g^2}2\sum(L^aL^a+R^aR^a)$ with Gell-Mann normalisation. We use the project's $T_a=\sigma_a/2$ and $H_E=\sum_\ell\tfrac12\sum_a(L_a^2+R_a^2)$, which equals $j_\ell(j_\ell+1)$ on every link ($0$ on $A$, $B$; $\tfrac34$ on $Q$, measured §3) — identical in form to KS, so "electric energy per link" is literally the same observable.
* *Magnetic normalisation.* $U_{ij}=c_{L,i}c^\dagger_{R,j}$ without a factor (BCW Eq. 4.3). Consequence: $\|\mathrm{Tr}U_P|{\rm vac}_A\rangle\|=4$ versus 1 in KS at $j_{\max}=\tfrac12$ (where $U_{ij}$ has entries $1/\sqrt2$). This is a property of the model; we do not rescale $\beta$ (the comparison table shows the raw values and the note states the factor).

## 3. Link algebra (prototype §"link algebra checks", all residuals $0.0$)

Qubits of link $\ell$: $(4\ell,4\ell+1,4\ell+2,4\ell+3)=(L_1,L_2,R_1,R_2)$, colour 1 = up ($m=+\tfrac12$), 2 = down; bit 1 = occupied; $c_k=(\prod_{m<k}Z_m)\sigma^-_k$ with $\sigma^-=|0\rangle\langle1|$. $U_{ij}=c_{L,i}c^\dagger_{R,j}$, $L_a=\sum_{ij}(T_a)_{ij}c^\dagger_{L,i}c_{L,j}$, $R_a=\sum_{ij}(T_a)_{ij}c^\dagger_{R,i}c_{R,j}$, $N=\sum_kc^\dagger_kc_k$, $T_{\rm BCW}=N_R-N_L$.
Checked: $[L_a,U_{ij}]=-(T_aU)_{ij}$, $[R_a,U_{ij}]=+(UT_a)_{ij}$; $[L_a,L_b]=i\epsilon_{abc}L_c$, same for $R$; $[L_a,R_b]=0$; $[U_{ij},U_{kl}]=0$; $[N,U_{ij}]=0$; $[T_{\rm BCW},U_{ij}]=2U_{ij}$ (BCW Eq. 4.1); $\det U=U_{11}U_{22}-U_{12}U_{21}=2c_{L1}c^\dagger_{R1}c_{L2}c^\dagger_{R2}$ and on the sector $\det U=2|B\rangle\langle A|$, zero on the quartet. $N=2$ sector = integers $\{3,5,6,9,10,12\}$ = bit strings $L_1L_2R_1R_2\in\{1100,1010,0110,1001,0101,0011\}$; Casimirs $(0,\tfrac34,\tfrac34,\tfrac34,\tfrac34,0)$ for both $L^2$ and $R^2$.
String-freeness: the end generators are exactly 2-qubit local ($L_a$ on qubits $0,1$; $R_a$ on $2,3$; assertion in the prototype), hence for link-contiguous modes every link bilinear and every end bilinear carries no string outside its link; $W=\mathrm{Tr}(U_bU_rU_t^\dagger U_l^\dagger)=\sum_{abcd}u^{(b)}_{ab}\otimes u^{(r)}_{bc}\otimes u^{(t)\dagger}_{dc}\otimes u^{(l)\dagger}_{ad}$ (4096 non-zeros on $2^{16}$).

## 4. Physical states and counts (prototype §"counts")

Per link the 6 states split into types $A$ ($j_L=j_R=0$), $B$ ($0,0$), $Q$ ($\tfrac12,\tfrac12$). Gauss's law at corner $c$ with ends $(e_1..e_4)$: kernel of $\sum_a(\sum_eG^a_e)^2$ on the product of the end spaces (1-dim for an $A/B$ end, 2-dim for a $Q$ end) — computed numerically in the qubit representation, exactly as the KS `corner_singlets` but with the rishon end operators. The number of singlets at a corner depends only on the end spins, so it equals the KS fusion count $n_c=|T(j_a,j_b)\cap T(j_c,j_d)|$. Far ends of outer links: for $A/B$ determined; for $Q$ fixed to colour 1 (spectator). Hence

$$N_{\rm rishon}=\sum_{j\in\{0,\frac12\}^{12}}\Big(\prod_cn_c(j)\Big)\,2^{\#\{\ell:\,j_\ell=0\}}=\mathbf{38\,432}.$$

Route 1 (types $\times$ fusion, 531 441 patterns, 35 073 of them supported): 38 432 in 1.3 s. Route 1′ (explicit corner kernels; 713 sampled states built as 48-bit COO, norm deviation $7.8\times10^{-16}$, Gauss residual with the full end operators $5.1\times10^{-16}$): 38 432. Route 2 (KS labels of `physical_basis` weighted by $2^{n_0}$): 38 432. Closed sector (all outer $j=0$): $4352=2^8\cdot17$ (16 vacua + 1 loop per outer singlet choice). Total COO size of all states: 508 688 entries. U(1) sectors $(Q_0..Q_3)$: 431; largest $(4,4,4,4)$: 2192 (= the U(2) QLM physical space); next $(4,4,4,2)$ etc.: 1004. Lean: `card_rishon_physical : rishonTotal = 38432` and `card_rishon_closed = 4352` by `decide +kernel` on top of the laptop's `patternCount` (`scratch/planner/lean/RishonCount.lean`, 8.0 s wall, 8.9 GB RSS, exit 0).

## 5. Inner span (prototype `proto_rishon_rank.py`)

Physical states are products of corner tensors, and the outer colours at different corners are independent, so the span of the inner components of all physical states with a given inner type pattern is $\bigotimes_c S_c$ with $S_c$ the span of the inner-end components of the corner kernels over all compatible outer-end choices. Computed: every $S_c$ is the full inner-end space, so $\mathrm{rank}\,S=\sum_{\text{inner patterns}}\prod_c\dim=\mathbf{1296}$ (full). (A 2 % sample of states gave the lower bound 777.) Therefore, for an outer-blind circuit $U=U_{\rm in}\otimes1$: $\|(U-e^{i\varphi}V)\Psi_k\|^2=\sum_o\|(M-e^{i\varphi}V_{\rm in})\psi^o_k\|^2\le\|(M-e^{i\varphi}V_{\rm in})\|_2^2$ for every physical state, and conversely exactness on all physical states forces $M=e^{i\varphi}V_{\rm in}$ on all 1296 codes.

## 6. Structure of $H_B^{\rm in}$ (prototype §"chain / block structure", "bright pairs")

$H_B^{\rm in}=-(W+W^\dagger)$ restricted to the 1296 codes: real symmetric ($0.0$), $[W,G^a_c{}^{\rm in}]=0$ ($0.0$). Heights: $h(A)=0,h(Q)=1,h(B)=2$ for $b,r$ and $h(B)=0,h(Q)=1,h(A)=2$ for $t,l$; $W$ changes $(h_b,h_r,h_t,h_l)$ by exactly $(1,1,1,1)$ (so $W^3=0$ and the operator is block diagonal in 65 "chain shapes"). Connected components of $|H_B^{\rm in}|$: 990 isolated dark codes, 32 of dimension 2 (eigenvalues $\pm1$), 16 of dimension 4 (rank 2, $\pm2$), 32 of dimension 5 (rank 2, $\pm2$), 1 of dimension 18 (rank 2, $\pm4\sqrt2$). Every component has rank 2, so
$$H_B^{\rm in}=-\sum_{p=1}^{81}\kappa_p\big(|u_p\rangle\langle v_p|+|v_p\rangle\langle u_p|\big),\qquad \kappa_p\in\{1\ (32),\ 2\ (48),\ 4\sqrt2\ (1)\},$$
with $\{u_p,v_p\}$ orthonormal and real, supports (nnz of $u$, $v$): $(1,1)\times32$, $(1,4)\times24$, $(4,1)\times8$, $(2,2)\times16$, $(16,2)\times1$; amplitude alphabet $\{\pm1,\pm1/\sqrt2,\pm1/2,\pm1/4\}$. Spectrum multiplicities: $\pm4\sqrt2$: 1, $\pm2$: 48, $\pm1$: 32, $0$: 1134 ($=1296-2\cdot81$). Hence $U_B(\beta)=\prod_p\exp\big(i\beta\kappa_p(|u_p\rangle\langle v_p|+{\rm h.c.})\big)$ — 81 commuting two-level rotations with angles $2\kappa_p\beta\in\{2\beta,4\beta,8\sqrt2\beta\}$; all frames needed to map $(u_p,v_p)$ to computational states have Clifford-type amplitudes.

## 7. Closed sector

Reference vacuum $|{\rm vac}_A\rangle$: $b,r$ of type $A$ (rishons at the start of the link), $t,l$ of type $B$ (rishons at the end); the only vacuum on which $\mathrm{Tr}U_P$ does not vanish ($U_b,U_r$ need a rishon at the L end, $U^\dagger_t,U^\dagger_l$ at the R end). $\mathrm{Tr}U_P|{\rm vac}_A\rangle=4|\ell\rangle$ with $|\ell\rangle$ the normalised all-quartet colour-loop state (16 codes, amplitudes $\pm\tfrac14$), $\mathrm{Tr}U_P|\ell\rangle=4|{\rm vac}_B\rangle$ (types $B,B,A,A$), $\mathrm{Tr}U_P|{\rm vac}_B\rangle=0$; $W^\dagger$ reverses. $H_E|\ell\rangle=3|\ell\rangle$. Bright block in $\{|{\rm vac}_A\rangle,|\ell\rangle,|{\rm vac}_B\rangle\}$:
$$H_{\rm closed}=\frac{g^2}2\,\mathrm{diag}(0,3,0)-\frac4{g^2}\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad \text{eigenvalues }0,\ \tfrac34g^2\pm\sqrt{\tfrac9{16}g^4+\tfrac{32}{g^4}}$$
(measured at $g^2=1$: $-4.956356, 0, 6.456356$; KS closed block $\big[\begin{smallmatrix}0&-2/g^2\\-2/g^2&3g^2/2\end{smallmatrix}\big]$: $-1.386001, 2.886001$). The remaining 14 vacua per outer choice are dark (energy 0). In the bright/dark basis $|b\rangle=(|{\rm vac}_A\rangle+|{\rm vac}_B\rangle)/\sqrt2$, $|d\rangle=(|{\rm vac}_A\rangle-|{\rm vac}_B\rangle)/\sqrt2$: $H_B=-4\sqrt2(|b\rangle\langle\ell|+{\rm h.c.})$, so $U_B(\beta)$ acts on $\{|b\rangle,|\ell\rangle\}$ as $\exp(i\,4\sqrt2\beta\,\sigma_x)$ and leaves $|d\rangle$ fixed.

## 8. Shared observables (definitions and values)

For a theory with vacuum $|\Omega\rangle$ (KS: $|0000\rangle$ with all outer $j=0$; rishon: $|{\rm vac}_A\rangle$ with every outer link in its near-end-empty singlet) and first-order step $U_1=U_B(\beta)U_E(\alpha)$:
* $E_{\rm link}(n)=\tfrac14\langle\Omega|U_1^{-n}H_E^{\rm inner}U_1^n|\Omega\rangle$;
* $P(n)=\langle\Omega|U_1^{-n}\,\tfrac12(\mathrm{Tr}U_P+\mathrm{Tr}U_P^\dagger)\,U_1^n|\Omega\rangle=-\tfrac12\langle H_B\rangle$;
* $\mathcal L(n)=|\langle\Omega|U_1^n|\Omega\rangle|^2$ (Loschmidt echo);
* closed-sector spectrum of $H(g^2)$, $g^2\in\{0.5,1,2\}$.
Closed forms (from §7 and the KS $2\times2$ block; $\theta_{\rm KS}=2\beta$, $\theta_{\rm R}=4\sqrt2\beta$): $E_{\rm link}(1)=\tfrac34\sin^2\theta_{\rm KS}$ vs $\tfrac38\sin^2\theta_{\rm R}$; $\mathcal L(1)=\cos^2\theta_{\rm KS}$ vs $\tfrac14(1+\cos\theta_{\rm R})^2$; $P(1)=0$ in both theories (identity: $U_E|\Omega\rangle$ is a phase and $U_B$ conserves $H_B$). ## 9. Verification design (evaluator fit)

The laptop evaluator (`evaluate(qc, enc, target)`) assembles a dense $\dim\times\dim$ matrix and per-outer-pattern dense blocks; with $\dim=38\,432$ and $\sim10^5$ (state, outer-pattern) pairs this needs $>20$ GB and is out. Sound replacement, built only on public laptop functions:
1. **Inner view.** An encoding-like object whose "physical vectors" are the 1296 two-rishon inner codes (outer bits 0) and whose target is the 1296×1296 inner operator $V_{\rm in}$ ($U_E^{\rm in}$ diagonal, $U_B^{\rm in}=e^{-i\beta H_B^{\rm in}}$, steps by composition). `evaluate(qc, inner_view, V_in, gauss=False, ...)` then returns block error $\|M-e^{i\varphi}V_{\rm in}\|_2$, exact leak amplitude $\|X\|_2$, the certified pruning bound, engine and certification flags — unchanged code. By §5 this certifies **every** physical state for an outer-blind circuit (the prototype of the theorem is the same inequality the laptop proved as `verdict_sound`, with $\Phi$ the code-space isometry). The check is first order in every deviation, including gauge-breaking ones (it compares full vectors on the code space, not projections).
2. **Outer reads.** If any outer qubit is read, the inner view is not sound (it only sees the all-zero outer pattern): the rishon verifier returns `outer_read_unsupported` in M5. The block-wise exact path for M6 is defined (per read-pattern group, the span $S_r=\bigoplus_{\rm inner\ patterns}\bigotimes_cS_c(o_c)$ from the corner factorisation of §5) but not built in M5.
3. **Gauss residual (sampled).** Outputs of the 17 closed reference states and of 256 seeded physical states (seed 20261104) are assembled from the simulated inner block and checked with the rishon end operators (2-qubit local); tolerance $10^{-10}$ (exact angles) / $N_{\rm rot}\varepsilon+10^{-9}$ (synthesised), the binding rule. This is a cross-check: item 1 already bounds the Gauss residual by $2\times$ the certified error.
4. **Physical cross-check (sampled).** The explicit $A_{k'k}=\langle\Psi_{k'}|U|\Psi_k\rangle$ on the same sample against $\langle\Psi_{k'}|V_{\rm in}\otimes1|\Psi_k\rangle$ (tester tool; not a verdict input).
What is certified: block exactness + leak + pruning on all 38 432 states (outer-blind circuits), Gauss on the sample. What is only sampled: Gauss and the explicit physical assembly.

## 10. Lean feasibility

* Counts: kernel `decide` works (8 s, §4) by reusing `Su2Plaq.patternCount` and `bit` (read-only import of `Su2Plaq.Structure.Counts`).
* JW facts: represent $c_k$ on $n$ modes as signed partial permutations of `Fin (2^n)` (`Fin N → Option (Fin N × Int)`); products are compositions; CAR for $n=4,8$, link-bilinear string-freeness for two links ($n=8$: the global $c_kc_l^\dagger$ of link 1 equals the embedded link-local one), the 6-element sector and the $A\to Q\to B$ ladder are all `decide`-able (256 evaluations per identity).
* $H_B^{\rm in}$ structure: with $4u_p,4v_p\in\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]^{1296}$ and $\kappa_p\in\{1,2,4\sqrt2\}$ the identity $16H_B^{\rm in}=-\sum_p\kappa_p(4u_p)(4v_p)^{\mathsf T}+{\rm h.c.}$ and orthonormality are integer-pair arithmetic; $1.7\times10^6$ entries × 81 pairs is far beyond kernel `decide` but seconds for compiled code → native certificate (theorem file in `lean/certs/`, the folder prompts/006 reserves for `native_decide`).
* Per-circuit certificates: the laptop checker (2.L4+) is not in this bundle (`Su2PlaqCert/*.lean` are namespace stubs); in addition $U_B$ contains the angle $8\sqrt2\beta$, not a rational multiple of $\beta$, so the certificate needs a third base angle $z'=e^{i\sqrt2\beta}$ (algebraically independent of $e^{i\beta}$ over $K$). $U_E$ (angles $-3\alpha/4$) is certifiable as is. Status: pending laptop; recorded as a requested feature in the merge notes.
