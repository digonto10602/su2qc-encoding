Words used below, once. *LSH register*: 12 data qubits (4 'in' sites × 3 loop-number bits $(\ell_{pn},\ell_{nv},\ell_{vp})$, written $(x_c,y_c,z_c)$ here — my shorthand) + 12 read-only outer qubits. *Transcoder*: the per-site reversible circuit `cx(y,z); cx(y,x); rccx(z,x,y)` of M7 that rewrites a site into (link bit, corner bit, $w$). *Wrapped circuit*: a spin-network circuit between the transcoder and its inverse. *Native circuit* (my label): an LSH circuit that never recodes all four sites into the spin-network code. *Robust key*: the headline ranking $(\mathrm{CX},\sum_{20}T,\rho,\sum_{20}\mathrm{depth},\mathrm{qubits})$ of `prompts/008` §6. *Kernel-checked*: a Lean proof re-verified by the Lean kernel with the standard axioms. *Native-certified*: accepted by the compiled Lean checker (trusts the Lean compiler). *Shim*: a track module that patches shared code at run time.

## 0. Summary (what the plan takes from this note)

1. **Target of the loop = the full LSH-register step** $U_B(\beta)U_E(\alpha)$ on 12 + 12 qubits; proposers may transcode or not. The round-0 best is the spin-network M4 headline wrapped by the transcoder: key $(71, 1118, 2276, 25)$, robust key $(71, 22480, \cdot, 46384, 25)$, i.e. $31+40$ CX. It is Lean-certifiable by the existing LSH checker (1.3 s). The general M7 circuit is $(94, 598, 880, 31)$.
2. **What M8 can gain beyond spin-network M4**: nothing is gained by construction (LSH with the inner|outer pairing *is* the spin-network basis in another bit code, audit 303 N7); the loop measures **how much of the +40 CX / +32 T recoding cost is intrinsic to the LSH register**. The cost splits as 16 CX linear + 24 CX / 32 T in four relative-phase Toffoli pairs. A linear (CNOT-only) recoding is impossible (kernel-checked: 8 different move vectors), and a relative-phase Toffoli needs 3 CX (Song–Klappenecker). Levers that exist only in LSH variables were found and are offered to the proposers as kernel-checked facts (§3): the coupling class is a function of the number of set $\ell_{nv}$ bits alone, so on bright codes the spin-network flag $A=[u{=}v{=}0]$ (one Toffoli pair there) equals a **parity of four raw bits** here; dark sites are a raw AND; every link bit is readable at two sites. No lower bound better than "the spin-network optimum" is known; the honest outcomes are a headline in $[\,{\sim}31,\,71\,]$ CX, reported as `native_gain_cx` $=71-\mathrm{CX}$ and `overhead_cx` $=\mathrm{CX}-31$.
3. **No fourth shim.** `lsh` enters the shared registry of `prompts/008` (one data-only edit of two hashed files, inside the 008 window); everything else of M8 lives in LSH-owned, unhashed files. Audit follow-ups and all LSH-side code can start now.
4. **Stop rule** counted on the robust key without its depth component, with a synthesis-noise band of $20\,N_{\rm rot}$ T at unchanged structure (§5).
5. **Uniform Raychowdhury–Stryker split**: added as a *structure row* (no circuit): 400 non-zeros, up to 4 per row, 9 moduli, outer-dependent, 6 sites / 18 qubits, against 256 / 1 / 3 / no / 4 / 12 (§6). A circuit for it would be a sixth encoding (it must write outer-site qubits).
6. Audit 303 follow-ups prototyped: the Lean tie theorem (N1) and seven structure theorems elaborate in 5.0 s; nine checker-level negative controls are `rejected` (N4); a theorem file with the binding fields inside the literal elaborates in 1.9 s.

## 3. What the LSH register offers that the spin-network register does not (measured, then kernel-checked)

Valid site codes $(x,y,z)=(\ell_{pn},\ell_{nv},\ell_{vp})$: `000`, `100`, `010`, `001`, `011`$\,=(0,1,1)$ (the dark site, $J=1$); on bright codes $x=j_pj_n$, $y=\bar j_pj_n$, $z=j_p\bar j_n$ (one-hot in the pair of link bits). Facts, all checked on the 47 codes by `scratch/planner/m8/proto_m8_facts.py` (production `inner_codes`, `coupling_table`, `partner_table`) and proved by `decide` in `scratch/planner/lean/LSHTie.lean` (`lake env lean`: 5.0 s, axioms `propext` only, one theorem also `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`):

| fact (Lean theorem name in the plan) | statement | use for a circuit |
|---|---|---|
| `inner_codes_from_rules` | the exported list `lshInnerCodes` equals $\{x<4096:\ \texttt{innerOK}\,x\}$ | audit 303 N1: the circuit theorems speak about the counted codes |
| `coupling_zero_iff_dark`, `dark_site_is_and` | a code is dark iff some site has $y_c\wedge z_c$ | darkness = one AND of raw bits per site, no recoding needed |
| `move_per_site` | the plaquette move flips $x_c$ if $w_c=y_c\oplus z_c=0$, and $y_c,z_c$ if $w_c=1$ | after `cx(y,z)`, `cx(y,x)`: $x'$ always flips, $y$ flips iff $w$ |
| `eight_differences` | the 8 bright pairs have 8 different move vectors | no CNOT-only frame (M7 statement, now kernel-checked) |
| `coupling_by_y_weight` | on bright codes $2K=-4,+2,-1$ for $\sum_cy_c=0,1,2$; $\sum y=\sum z\le2$ | the coupling class is a symmetric function of four raw bits: $k=-2+3\sum_cy_c-\tfrac92(y_0y_2+y_1y_3)$ |
| `y_parity_invariant` | $\bigoplus_cy_c$ is unchanged by the move and equals $[k=+1]$ | on bright codes the spin-network flag $A=[u{=}v{=}0]$ (a Toffoli pair: 6 CX, 8 T) equals $\lnot\bigoplus_cy_c$: a parity |
| `link_bit_two_ways` | $x_c\oplus y_c=x_{c+1}\oplus z_{c+1}$ (the Abelian Gauss law as a parity) | every link bit is available at two sites; $w_c$ is one CX away |

Why the move needs one Toffoli-type gate per site in the transcoding designs: with $x'=x\oplus y=j_n$ and $w=y\oplus z$, bright codes have $y=w\wedge x'$, so $y$ flips together with $x'$ exactly when $w=1$; making $y$ move-invariant is $y\oplus(w\wedge x')$ — which is also the corner bit $[J=1]$. The move-invariant parities of the bright codes are $w_0,w_1,w_2$ and $\bigoplus_cy_c$ (4 of them, after removing the 4 Abelian-Gauss-law relations; hand derivation, consistent with the kernel-checked facts above). Two trades follow on paper and were **not prototyped**: (a) since $\sum_cw_c$ is even, three Toffoli pairs plus three CX might replace four (same CX, 8 T fewer); (b) replacing the Toffoli pair of the flag $A$ by the raw parity (the two agree on bright codes only, so the walk polynomial of the spin-network headline would have to be re-solved on the dark codes). I did not find a construction below the wrapped headline's 71 CX in the time of this run. The levers make a gain in T plausible and a gain in CX possible; neither is promised. They are given to the proposers as facts, not as a design.

## 5. Stop rule: which changes of the headline count

`prompts/008` §6: future loops count rounds without a better robust key. Audit 302 §7.7: the fourth component (summed depth$_{\rm CT}$) differs by $\le0.6\,\%$ between programs of identical structure and alone decided "no improvement" in spin-network round 5 — a stop rule should treat it as a tie. The same origin (the Ross–Selinger count $T_r(\theta)=3\log_2(1/\varepsilon)+\delta(\theta)$, $\delta$ a few T wide) makes $\sum T$ noisy at fixed structure: `prompts/104` §11 E3 bounds what tuning can extract by $\sigma/\sqrt{M}\lesssim1$ T per rotation and pair at $M=20$, i.e. $20\,N_{\rm rot}$ T in the sum (340 of 22 480 for the wrapped headline, 1.5 %). Rule adopted: compare $(\mathrm{CX},\sum T,\rho,\mathrm{qubits})$; if CX, $N_{\rm rot}$ and $T_{\rm fixed}$ are all unchanged and $|\Delta\sum T|\le20N_{\rm rot}$, the $\sum T$ difference is a tie and $(\rho,\mathrm{qubits})$ decide. A structural change (a rotation or a fixed T fewer) always counts through the plain comparison. Prototype `proto/lsh_loop.py::improves`; three mutants of it are rejected by the locked test (`mutation_check.py`).

## 6. Uniform Raychowdhury–Stryker splitting as a comparison row

`scratch/planner/m8/proto/lsh_rs_split.py` (0.5 s): recoupling $R$ of corners 1 and 3 from (inner, inner | outer, outer) to (inner, outer | inner, outer), built from the production `corner_singlets`; $\lVert R^{\mathsf T}R-1\rVert_{\max}=2.7\cdot10^{-15}$, $\det R=1$, entries in $\{1,\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}2\}\times\{1,\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}2\}$ (the four-spin-$\tfrac12$ recoupling coefficients), 200 of 433 rows mixed. $H=R^{\mathsf T}H_AR$: 400 non-zeros, at most 4 per row, 324 non-zero rows, moduli $\{\tfrac14,\tfrac{\sqrt3}4,\tfrac12,\tfrac34,\tfrac{\sqrt3}2,1,\tfrac32,\sqrt3,2\}$, 16 of 47 inner patterns outer-dependent, spectrum equal to $3.3\cdot10^{-15}$; ours: 256, 1, 256, $\{\tfrac12,1,2\}$. Sites holding an inner link end: 6 (18 qubits) against 4 (12). The other mixed pairing (inner a with outer c) gives the same numbers (mutant check), so the row does not depend on which outer link is paired with which inner one.
A circuit is not planned: $U_B^{\rm RS}=R^\dagger U_BR$ needs $R$ as a circuit on 6 sites that **writes outer-site qubits** (the evaluator's read-only boundary would have to move: a new encoding with 18 data qubits), plus rotations by the fixed angle $\arccos\tfrac12$-type recoupling (non-Clifford, synthesised). That is a sixth encoding, not a variant of this one.
