Jargon, once. *Lean 4* = an interactive theorem prover whose small kernel re-checks every proof; *Mathlib* = its community mathematics library; *axiom* = a statement accepted without proof — a Lean proof is only as trustworthy as the axioms it depends on, printed by `#print axioms`; the three *standard axioms* are `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`; *`decide`* = a tactic that proves a decidable statement by having the kernel evaluate it (slow but fully trusted); *`native_decide`* = the same, but the evaluation is done by compiled code, so the proof additionally trusts the Lean compiler (it shows up as an extra, per-theorem axiom named `…native_decide.ax_…` in Lean 4.35); *certificate* = data generated outside Lean (here by Python) that a small Lean checker verifies; *operator norm* $\|M\|_2$ = largest factor by which $M$ stretches a vector; *Laurent polynomial* = a polynomial allowed to have negative powers, here in the unit-circle variables $z_\alpha=e^{i\theta_\alpha}$, $z_\beta=e^{i\theta_\beta}$.

## 1. What the compression machinery relies on, and how each fact is handled

The evaluator's verdict (`prompts/004` §2, `prompts/005` §2, `docs/METHODS.md` §6) is the inequality chain

$$\|U\Phi-e^{i\varphi}\Phi V\|_2\ \le\ \underbrace{\|A-e^{i\varphi}V\|_2}_{\texttt{block\_error}}+\underbrace{\max_r t_r}_{\texttt{dropped\_total}}+\underbrace{\|X\|_2}_{\texttt{leak\_amplitude}}+\underbrace{\|S\|_2}_{\le 4\cdot\texttt{gauss\_residual}},$$

with $U'\Phi=\Phi A+S+X$ (three column-wise orthogonal pieces), $U'=U+E$ the pruned simulation, $E$ block-diagonal in the outer pattern. Every term is a theorem of finite-dimensional linear algebra except the numbers themselves. The table decides, for every fact, whether it becomes a **generic Lean theorem** (proved once, standard axioms only), a **per-object Lean-checked certificate** (exact data generated by Python, verified by a Lean program), or stays **numerical** (float64, ground truth of the loop).

### 1.1 Why kernel `decide` cannot do per-circuit checks
The kernel reduces terms symbolically (GMP-accelerated only for `Nat` literals); `Rat` arithmetic uses `Nat.gcd` by well-founded recursion, which the kernel does not unfold (P5: "reduction got stuck at … `Rat.mul`"). Even with an integer-scaled field, a 3 000-gate, 34-qubit circuit on $433$ inputs is $\sim10^9$ ring operations — days in the kernel. `native_decide` (or, equivalently in trust, a compiled checker executable) is the established route for large finite computations (Lean API: *"native computation … asserting the result as an axiom"*; see the Zulip threads on `Lean.ofReduceBool` and the 2026 change to per-theorem axioms, sources below). **Decision for Digonto:** certificates add exactly one non-standard axiom per theorem (`<name>._native.native_decide.ax_1_1`), i.e. trust in the Lean compiler and in the ~400-line checker source (reviewed by the auditor, and whose gate semantics are cross-checked against the validated float simulator by a locked test). The generic theorems and the counting theorems (§2.1) use the standard axioms only.

### 1.2 Exact arithmetic that covers everything in this project
* Clifford+T entries lie in $\mathbb Z[\tfrac1{\sqrt2},i]$ (Kliuchnikov–Maslov–Mosca; Giles–Selinger arXiv:1212.0506, Theorem 1), so $H,S,T,SX$ and all monomial gates have entries in $K_0=\mathbb Q(i,\sqrt2)$.
* Route-A physical states are products of four corner singlets of spin-$\tfrac12$ ends; their coefficients are in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$ (measured: $\pm1,\pm\tfrac12,\pm\tfrac1{\sqrt2},\pm\tfrac1{\sqrt3},\pm\tfrac1{\sqrt6},\pm\tfrac1{2\sqrt3}$), and the plaquette $W$ has entries $\pm\tfrac1{\sqrt2},\pm\tfrac12,\pm1$. Hence everything lives in the degree-8 field $K=\mathbb Q(i,\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$, stored as 8 rationals (P5 `K`, multiplication by the tower $(a+bi+c\sqrt2+di\sqrt2)+(\dots)\sqrt3$).
* Rotation angles: every rotation in the general circuit is a rational multiple of $\alpha$ or $\beta$ (`p(−3α/4)`, Paulo's `crx` at $\pm\beta,\pm2\beta$, $\alpha_P=2\beta$). With base angles $\theta_\alpha=\alpha/d_\alpha$, $\theta_\beta=\beta/d_\beta$ ($d$ = lcm of the denominators met) each gate entry is a Laurent polynomial in $z_\alpha=e^{i\theta_\alpha}$, $z_\beta=e^{i\theta_\beta}$ over $K$: $e^{im\theta}=z^m$, $\cos m\theta=\tfrac12(z^m+z^{-m})$, $-i\sin m\theta=-\tfrac12(z^m-z^{-m})$ (P5 `cosB`, `negISinB`, `pythag_3`). An identity between Laurent polynomials holds for **all real angles**, so one certificate proves the circuit for every $(\alpha,\beta)$ — strictly stronger than the three numerical angle pairs. The target $U_B=e^{-i\beta H_B}$ has eigenvalues $\{0,\pm\tfrac12,\pm1,\pm2\}$, so $V=\sum_\lambda P_\lambda z_\beta^{-\lambda d_\beta}$ with exact spectral projectors $P_\lambda=\prod_{\mu\ne\lambda}(H_B-\mu)/(\lambda-\mu)$ (rational coefficients, per outer block $\le17\times17$); $U_E$ is diagonal with $z_\alpha^{-3nd_\alpha/4}$.
* Circuits with emission-time angles that are **not** small rational multiples of $\alpha,\beta$ (`Fraction.limit_denominator(64)` residual $>10^{-12}$) get status `n_a` — they stay numerically verified only. The global phase between circuit and target is $k\,z_\alpha^az_\beta^b$ with $|k|=1$, $k\in K$; the exporter fits $(k,a,b)$ from the verdict phases at the three angle pairs and the checker verifies it.
* Cost estimate (compiled Lean, `precompileModules`): the general step circuit after `_translate` ($\approx1\,200$–$1\,500$ SIM_BASIS gates, most monomial; branching only at `h`, `sx`, `crx`-expansions) on 433 physical inputs each a superposition of $\le17$ basis states $\to$ $\sim10^7$–$10^8$ $K$-Laurent operations, i.e. **seconds to a few minutes**. Paulo's 3 207-gate compiled circuit is comparable. This is why certificates run once per global-best change, not per candidate.

## 2. Structure of the physics problem — what Lean can decide, verify or prove

### 2.1 Counts from the corner fusion rule — kernel-checked (`decide`, no axioms at all)
Spins in $\{0,\tfrac12\}$ as `Bool`; a corner with inner ends $(a,b)$ and outer ends $(c,d)$ has $|T(j_a,j_b)\cap T(j_c,j_d)|$ singlets, $T(0,0)=\{0\},T(0,\tfrac12)=T(\tfrac12,0)=\{\tfrac12\},T(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)=\{0,1\}$. P6 (`P6_count.lean`, plain Lean, structural recursion only):
* `total_eq : total = 433`, `farEndWeighted_eq = 17362` — **proved by `decide`, "does not depend on any axioms"**, $\approx60$–$70$ s each (4096 patterns; `maxRecDepth 100000`). `linkPatterns = 256`, `twoOrMore = 95`, `closed = 2`, `maxPerOuter = 17` evaluate correctly (`#eval`) and are the same size.
* Outer-pattern table: the naive rule "every $m$ pattern of a supported spin pattern is supported" gives **3281**, not 3153. The exact corner rule — when both outer links carry $j=\tfrac12$ and the inner pair is $(0,0)$ the outer pair must be an $m$-singlet, $m_c=-m_d$ — gives `supportedOuterM = 3153` and `maxPerOuterM = 17` (`#eval`, 10 s). The `decide` proof over $6561\times16$ patterns exceeded the default 200 000 heartbeats; with `maxHeartbeats 0` it is $\approx25\times$ the 4096 case ($\lesssim30$ min, builder measures) — or restructure as a sum over the 256 outer spin patterns. **This is the "discovery" pattern:** a conjectured rule is written as a decidable function, `#eval` compares it with the numerics in seconds, the mismatch (3281 vs 3153) points at the missing constraint, and the corrected rule becomes a theorem.

### 2.2 Exact certificates (compiled checker, `native_decide`-level trust)
* **Physical basis**: 433 exact $K$-vectors (sympy nullspaces of $\le16\times16$ rational corner Casimirs, cross-checked to the float basis $\le10^{-12}$); the checker verifies $G^a_c\psi_k=0$ exactly (spin-$\tfrac12$ generators have entries $\pm\tfrac12,\pm\tfrac i2\in K$), orthonormality, the $J$ labels ($\hat J_{\rm in}^2\psi=J(J+1)\psi$), and that the count equals the fusion count of §2.1. Completeness ("no other Gauss-law states") = §2.1 count + per-corner exact kernel dimension (16 cases) + the tensor-product lemma "kernel of a sum of positive operators on disjoint factors is the tensor product of kernels" — the lemma is **stated, not proved** (Mathlib tensor products of Hilbert spaces: out of budget).
* **$H_B$ block structure** (route A, (inner|outer) pairing; M0-X5): the checker recomputes $H_B^{\rm phys}=-(\Psi^\dagger(W\otimes1)\Psi+{\rm h.c.})$ from the exact $\Psi$ and the exact per-link $U_{ij}$ (CG coefficients in $K$), then verifies: block-diagonal in the outer spins; $J_c$ conserved; every block equals $D\,K_{\rm sub}D$ for the exact $47\times47$ $K_{\rm inner}$ (entries $0,\pm\tfrac12,\pm1,\pm2$) and a supplied $\pm1$ gauge $D$ (from `gauge_align`); $K$ = 8 bright $2\times2$ pairs (couplings $2$, $1\times6$, $\tfrac12$) + 31 dark rows — a `decide` on the rational $47\times47$; spectrum multiplicities $(16,96,16,177,16,96,16)$ for $\lambda=(-2,-1,-\tfrac12,0,\tfrac12,1,2)$ by exact ranks of $(H_B-\lambda)$ per block.
* **12-qubit inner operator on the 625 codes** (Paulo basis), measured today for the record (`plaquette_W(convention="paulo")`, $-(W+W^\dagger)$): 128 edges, all of modulus $\tfrac12$; connected components: 464 dark codes, 16 pairs, 8 four-cliques, 16 five-cliques, one 17-component; spectrum on the (full-rank, 625) inner span: $\pm2$ (1), $\pm1$ (24), $\pm\tfrac12$ (16), $0$ (543). *The coordinator's quoted "41 bright pairs with couplings 1, ½, ¼" was not reproduced by this computation; the exporter computes whatever decomposition is present and the checker verifies it, so the plan does not hard-code those numbers.* Don't-care codes: $4096-625=3471$ (Report 1 note), `decide`.
* **Symmetries**: Python enumerates the 16 candidates (dihedral $D_4$ of the square acting on links with orientation reversal $m_L\leftrightarrow m_R$, times charge conjugation $m\to-m$ with CG signs) and finds the signed label permutations that commute with $H_B$ and $H_E$ (`gauge_align` fixes signs); Lean verifies each found symmetry exactly on the 433 states. Lean does **not** search; it makes the exact check of a conjecture cheap and gives a counterexample (the `#eval` of the checker prints the first violated entry) when it fails.

### 2.3 Symbolic theorems (Mathlib, standard axioms)
* $[H_E^{\rm outer},H]=0$: $H_E^{\rm outer}=1\otimes D$, $W=W_{\rm in}\otimes1$ commute (`kron_disjoint_comm`, sibling `Sanity.lean`); the projected statement follows because both preserve the Gauss kernel (`exp_agree_on_invariant`, sibling `Exp.lean`).
* Out of reach in budget (stated as hypotheses or verified exactly instead): SU(2) recoupling (6j symbols, Zache et al. Eq. 2), the spectral gap $\tfrac34$ of $\sum(G^a_c)^2$, completeness of the fusion count, Paulo's Fig. 2 claim as a general theorem.

## 3. Lean inside the Gu-et-al loop (design)
1. **Structure library for proposers**: `lean/Su2Plaq/Structure/*.lean` (theorems) + `compress/lean/structure.json` (machine-readable: dark labels, bright pairs with couplings, don't-care codes, outer-pattern support table, symmetries, each tagged `proved_by: decide | native | numerical`), rendered into the task spec as a section "Proved structure (Lean)". Proposers may rely on these facts without re-deriving them.
2. **Proposer lemma files**: a program `NNN-slug.py` may be accompanied by `NNN-slug.lean` (same folder — already inside the proposer's allowed write paths) importing `Su2Plaq`; `evaluate_program` runs `lake env lean` on it with a 120 s budget and records `{"status": proved|failed|none, "axioms_ok"}` in `ProgramResult.lean_lemmas` — informative, never part of validity.
3. **Certified polish rules**: every rewrite rule of `passes.polish` names a theorem in `Su2Plaq/Rewrites.lean` (`rules.LEAN_RULES`); a locked test checks the mapping and the axioms, so `polish` applies only Lean-proved rules.
4. **Per-circuit certificates** (§1.2): generated and checked for the round-0 general seed, every new global best, and the final best per encoding; status in `Entry.lean` and the report table. Budget: $\le600$ s per certificate, $\le1$ certificate + $\le5$ lemma files per round, after the numerical verdicts — Lean never decides validity and never blocks a verdict.
5. **Proposer agent definition**: needs a short "Lean" paragraph (where the structure facts are, how to write an optional lemma file, that validity stays numerical) — listed for the coordinator in the plan; the guard hook needs no change.

## 4. Budget and risks
* Mathlib: the sibling has the `v4.35.0` build in `.lake/packages` (6.7 GB olean cache). Copying `.lake/packages` into `lean/` (or `lake exe cache get`, network) avoids a 2–3 h Mathlib build. Each Mathlib-importing file elaborates in 5–60 s; the counts file $\approx$ 5–10 min (`decide`); full `lake build` $\lesssim15$ min cold, seconds incremental.
* Builder skill risk: Sonnet writing Mathlib proofs. Mitigated by the prototypes (P1, P2, P3b, P4 give working proofs of the hardest generic statements) and by keeping the checker Mathlib-free.
* Trust: certificates depend on the compiler axiom; stated in every report line as "Lean-certified (native)" vs "Lean-proved (kernel)".
