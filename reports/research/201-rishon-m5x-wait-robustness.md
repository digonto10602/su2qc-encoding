## 2. Sources used (opened and read)

- BCW: R. Brower, S. Chandrasekharan, U.-J. Wiese, *QCD as a quantum link model*, Phys. Rev. D 60, 094502 (1999), arXiv:hep-th/9704106 — Sec. 4, Eq. 4.3 (rishon form $U_{ij}=c_{L,i}c^\dagger_{R,j}$), Eq. 4.6 ($\det U$ for $N=2$). Already the basis of METHODS-rishon R1 (research 200 §2).
- Lean 4 reference, *Tactic reference → Decision procedures*: `decide` "attempts to prove the main goal … by synthesizing an instance of `Decidable p`, then reduces that instance"; `decide +kernel` "uses kernel for reduction instead of the elaborator"; `decide +native` "uses the native code compiler … admitting the result via an axiom" and "depends on the correctness of the Lean compiler". https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Tactic-Proofs/Tactic-Reference/ (section "Decision Procedures"). This is why every new theorem uses `decide +kernel` and none `native_decide`.
- Qiskit `PhaseGate`: $P(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&e^{i\theta}\end{pmatrix}$, $P(\theta)=e^{i\theta/2}R_z(\theta)$. https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/docs/en/api/qiskit/qiskit.circuit.library.PhaseGate. Checked numerically (`proto_outer_row.py`): `Operator` of `p(0.3)` on qubit 1 of 2 is $\mathrm{diag}(1,1,e^{0.3i},e^{0.3i})$ (little-endian), `cx(0,1)` is the permutation $[0,3,2,1]$.
- Fraction-free (integer-preserving) Gaussian elimination: E. H. Bareiss, *Sylvester's identity and multistep integer-preserving Gaussian elimination*, Math. Comp. 22 (1968) 565–578, DOI 10.1090/S0025-5718-1968-0226829-0 (resolves to the AMS page of vol. 22 no. 103). The prototype uses the simpler one-step cross-multiplication $r_j\leftarrow p\,r_j-a_{jc}\,r_{\rm piv}$ with no division at all (entries stay integers; matrix sizes $\le48\times16$, entries $0,\pm1$, so growth is irrelevant); the rank over $\mathbb Q$ is the number of pivots because row operations with non-zero integer multipliers preserve the row space over $\mathbb Q$ (elementary linear algebra; Bareiss §1 for the fraction-free idea).
- Spinnet template (track S, read-only): `~/Projects/su2qc-spinnet/prompts/102-spinnet-m3-wait-lean-structure.md`, `lean/Su2Plaq/SpinNet/Semantics.lean` (Laurent-polynomial gate semantics over $\mathbb Z[i][z^{\pm1}]$), `tests/acceptance/m3x/test_spinnet_lean_exact.py` (structure of the locked tests, `lean_eval`, `lean_gate_images`). ## 3. Candidate methods, per item, and what the prototype measured

### 3.1 Item 1 — tie `HBData.linkOps` to `JW.lean` (P1)
Candidates: (a) a kernel `decide` lemma in `lean/Su2Plaq/Rishon/` importing both `Su2Plaq.Rishon.JW` and `Su2PlaqCert.Rishon.HBData` and comparing the 8 sparse lists with `Uop i j` / `Udop i j` restricted to `sector` (as sets of `(row, col, value)` triples with equal lengths); (b) adding a `Ud = Uᵀ` test to `checkHB` (native, weaker: only internal consistency); (c) matching outside Lean (what tester 206 did; not formal). **Chosen (a)**: it is the only option that closes the chain JW definition → certificate data → native checker. Statement (prototype, verbatim in the plan §2): `hb_link_ops_from_jw` = for $i,j<2$: data$[2i+j]$ ≡ entries of `Uop i j` on the sector; data$[4+2i+j]$ ≡ entries of `Udop i j := comp (mode false 4 (2+j)) (mode true 4 i)` ($U_{ij}^\dagger=c_{R,j}c^\dagger_{L,i}$) **and** ≡ the transpose of data$[2i+j]$; both operators keep the sector. Measured: part of a 5.95 s / 2.02 GB file. Mutants rejected: a sign flip of every certificate entry; "Ud = U" instead of "Ud = Uᵀ".

### 3.2 Item 2 — $\det U$ operator identity (P2)
`det_u_identity : OpEq 4 (U₁₁U₂₂ − U₁₂U₂₁) detU` on all 16 basis states (the full 4-mode space, so it is an operator identity, not a sector statement), with `detU := smul 2 (comp (Uop 0 0) (Uop 1 1))` from `JW.lean`. Derivation: $U_{12}U_{21}=c_{L1}c^\dagger_{R2}c_{L2}c^\dagger_{R1}=-c_{L1}c^\dagger_{R1}c_{L2}c^\dagger_{R2}=-U_{11}U_{22}$ (three anticommutations), hence $\det U=2U_{11}U_{22}$ — BCW Eq. 4.6 for $N=2$. Bonus `u_commute : [U_{ij},U_{kl}]=0` (BCW Eq. 4.1 / METHODS-rishon R1, "measured exact" so far). Measured: same file; axioms **none**. Mutant rejected: coefficient 3.

### 3.3 Item 3 — the reduction to the KS counting formula (P3)
What `card_rishon_physical` proves: $\sum_{n<4096}\mathrm{patternCount}(n)\,2^{\mathrm{zeros}(n)}=38\,432$, with `patternCount` $=\prod_c$ `cornerCount` (KS fusion count at the corner). The missing steps of the reduction "rishon physical states ↔ this formula" are:
1. for a fixed assignment of link types the Gauss kernel is $\bigotimes_c\ker G_c$ (each $G^a_c$ acts on the four ends at $c$; the type fixes each link's space to a product of its two end spaces: 1-dim for a singlet end, 2-dim for a quartet end) — a linear-algebra fact (kernel of commuting operators acting on disjoint tensor factors), **documented, not decidable as such**;
2. $\dim\ker G_c$ for a corner with end spins $(s_1..s_4)$, $s_e\in\{0,\tfrac12\}$, equals the SU(2) fusion count `cornerCount` — **decidable** (P3);
3. the sum over type patterns groups into spin patterns with weight $2^{\mathrm{zeros}(n)}$ (two types $A,B$ per $j=0$ link, one type $Q$ per $j=\tfrac12$ link) — trivial counting, documented.

Candidates for 2: (a) Mathlib `Matrix.rank` / `LinearMap.ker` — noncomputable, not `decide`-able, rejected; (b) `decide` over all $3^{12}=531\,441$ type patterns with a per-corner table — estimated $\ge10^3$ s in the kernel (Counts.lean needs 8 s for 4096 patterns), rejected; (c) **chosen**: per corner, all $3^4=81$ end configurations $m$ (each end empty `00`, full `11`, or one rishon `01`/`10`), basis = the $2^{\#Q}\le16$ eight-bit states, generators $2G^z=\sum_e(n_{e1}-n_{e2})$, $G^+=\sum_ec^\dagger_{e1}c_{e2}$, $G^-=\sum_ec^\dagger_{e2}c_{e1}$ built with `JW.lean`'s `mode`/`comp` on 8 modes (so the theorem is about the encoding's own JW operators; by `end_bilinear_local` they are 2-qubit local), invariance of the subspace checked, kernel dimension $=D-\mathrm{rank}$ of the stacked $3D\times D$ integer matrix by fraction-free elimination, compared with `cornerCountR` (a Mathlib-free copy of `fuse`/`cornerCount`). Joint kernel of $G^z,G^+,G^-$ = joint kernel of $G^x,G^y,G^z$ because $G^x=\tfrac12(G^++G^-)$, $G^y=\tfrac1{2i}(G^+-G^-)$. A Bridge file (imports `Su2Plaq.Rishon.Counts`, hence Mathlib) proves `cornerCountR = cornerCount` on the 16 Boolean inputs by `decide`. Measured: P1+P2+P3 in one Mathlib-free file: **5.95 s wall, 2.02 GB peak RSS**; axioms `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` (from `List`/`Int` decidability instances — standard). Mutants rejected: `fuse true true = [0]` (dropping the $J=1$ channel), and $G^-$ built with the wrong mode (a number operator instead of a hop).

### 3.4 Item 4 — certified outer electric row
Candidates: (a) the existing seeded sample on ≤ 4000 of the 48-bit codes (`outer_electric_check`, evidence only); (b) **chosen**: the circuit's gates are partitioned by link ($q\,\mathrm{div}\,4$); if any gate straddles two links the row is not certifiable (`cross_link_gate`); otherwise each link's gate list is a 4-qubit sub-circuit whose exact $16\times16$ Qiskit `Operator` is compared, on the 6 sector codes (columns), with $\mathrm{diag}(e^{-i\frac34\alpha[\text{type}=Q]})$ after one global phase per link; the error of the full 48-qubit operator on every superposition of valid codes is bounded by $\sum_\ell\|V_\ell-e^{i\varphi_\ell}T_\ell\|_2$ (telescoping of a tensor product of unitaries: $\|\bigotimes_\ell V_\ell-\bigotimes_\ell T_\ell\|\le\sum_\ell\|V_\ell-T_\ell\|$ with $\|V_\ell\|=\|T_\ell\|=1$ — the same inequality as `synth_bound` / `prod_sub_prod_le` in the laptop's Lean library, METHODS §11). This is exhaustive over all $6^{12}$ valid 48-bit codes and hence over all 38 432 physical states, which are superpositions of valid codes. Measured (`proto_outer_row.py`): 12 links, error $0.0$ at $\alpha=0.37$ and $0.91$, 5 ms; mutant `p(10^{-7})` on an outer qubit → bound $1.0\times10^{-7}$ (detected at 1000× the $10^{-10}$ tolerance); `cx(3,4)` → `cross_link_gate`; an `x` on an outer qubit made the prototype's phase fit divide by zero — the production function must guard the phase fit (fit on the trace over the 6 code columns; if $|z|<10^{-300}$ use phase 1) and never raise on a syntactically valid circuit.
(c) Lean: the same fact as a kernel theorem on the rendered gate list (P4 below: `ue_gadget_sector` on the 6 sector codes + `ue_outer_gates_structure` = the 36-gate list is exactly 12 gadget instances). Both (b) and (c) are built: (b) certifies the shipped Qiskit object numerically-exactly, (c) certifies the gate list symbolically for all $\alpha$.

### 3.5 Item 5 — exact gate semantics
`build_UE` (P4): gates `cx`, `p` only; coefficient ring $\mathbb Z[i][z^{\pm1}]$ with $z=e^{i\alpha/4}$ (`p q a` $=\mathrm{diag}(1,z^a)$, $a=-3$ for `p(−3α/4)`); target $z^{-3\#Q(y)}|y\rangle$ with $\#Q$ counted from the link codes ($\{5,6,9,10\}$ = `linkQ` of `JW.lean`), **not** from the parity trick, so the theorem checks the trick. `innerCodes` = the $6^4$ products of `sector` (`card_inner_codes = 1296`). Measured: **5.64 s / 1.84 GB** for `ue_exact_all_codes` (1296 codes × 12 gates), `ue_gadget_sector`, the two structure theorems and the count; axioms standard. Mutants rejected: `p` exponent sign, target sign, one CX reversed.

`build_UB`: 10 094 CX, 1402 `ccx`, 202 `ch`, 81 `crx`, Hadamard frames; a kernel evaluation of the 1296 codes through ~12 000 gates with branching states costs, at the ~2 ms per sparse operator application measured on `JW.lean` (`car_8` + string-freeness ≈ $10^5$ applications in 200–265 s), about $1296\times1.2\times10^4\times2\,\mathrm{ms}\approx3\times10^4$ s before branching — **infeasible** for `decide` (and `native_decide` is barred under `lean/Su2Plaq/`). Per-block semantics (81 blocks × 1296 codes × ~150 gates ≈ $1.6\times10^7$ applications) is equally out. A purely syntactic structural statement (angle multiset = $2\kappa$ of the certificate; `ccx` targets are ancillas; each pre-cancellation block is a palindrome around its `crx`) would need a Lean re-implementation of `_cancel_adjacent` to connect to the shipped circuit and would be superseded by the laptop's native per-circuit checker in M6; the angle multiset is already locked numerically (M5-B1). **Dropped**, stated in the plan.

### 3.6 Item 6 — suite hygiene
Benchmark 200: `T1[UE]` 12–43 s, `T1[UB]` 377–416 s, `T1[step]` 581–671 s (one process each; the non-slow wall time equals the longest test). Marking `step` slow brings the non-slow m5 suite to ≈ `T1[UB]` ≈ 7 min < 10 min; marking both `UB` and `step` would give ≈ 100 s but removes the synthesised-$U_B$ re-verification from every step-level run. Recommendation: mark only `step` (minimal change; the full gate still runs it). It is a change to a locked m5 file → plan §7 gives the exact diff and the LOCK.json procedure of prompts/201; it needs Digonto's approval (re-locking m5).
